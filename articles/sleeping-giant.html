The Sleeping Giant

Most Christians are concerned about the current darkness the world finds itself in — and I am concerned too. I also know that times of trial and struggle historically bring great seasons of revival, as men and women go to their knees and cry out to God for divine wisdom and help.

Scripture says, “If my people, who are called by my name, will humble themselves and pray and seek my face and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear from heaven and will forgive their sin and heal their land” (2 Chronicles 7:14).

I’ve often referred to the modern Church as a sleeping giant. This is because many contemporary Christians don’t utilize the weapon of prayer to win the spiritual battle that is going on today. We have become like the children of Ephraim. The Bible says of them in Psalm 78:9, “The children of Ephraim, being armed, and carrying bows, turned back in the day of battle.”

It’s time for the Church to arise! When we arise in fervent prayer, the light of God will overshadow the prevailing darkness around us.

Isaiah 60:1-2 says, “Arise, shine, for your light has come, and the glory of the Lord rises upon you. See, darkness covers the earth and thick darkness is over the peoples, but the Lord rises upon you and his glory appears over you.”

We have been called to be light to this world. “Nations will come to your light, and kings to the brightness of your dawn” (Isaiah 60:3).

Hang on! I believe we are on the verge of one of the greatest revivals the world has ever known! However, the cost-price of this revival is intense and consistent prayer. The Kingdoms of this world must become the kingdoms of our God and of His Christ; the glory of the knowledge of the Lord must cover the earth as the waters cover the sea. Let the sleeping giants awake. We will win more battles as a Church praying than complaining about the darkness around us.
