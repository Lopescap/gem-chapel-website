You Carry Something Precious!

You are not good for nothing; you are too precious to be wasted. You may not be like somebody but that doesn’t mean you are a nobody. You carry something precious.

God’s gift is the instrument for man’s lift. In other words, your lifting is at the mercy of your gifting. You cannot be gifted and not be lifted. If you want a lift secure a gift.

Gifted people will always end as the elevated ones. Your greatest elevator in life is inside you. It is the gift that God has given you. If you are downtrodden never look for who to blame. Rather examine your gift for it is the lifting power of your life.

The gifting of Joseph was responsible for his lifting (Gen. 41:1-46). Joseph would have died as a prisoner had it not been for the gift of interpretation of dreams that God gave him. After years of suffering and affliction a prisoner and a foreigner became the next in command after Pharaoh because of his gift.

All lifted men were gifted men. David was lifted from shepherding the few sheep of his father Jesse to shepherding and ruling the whole nation of Israel (1 Sam. 16:13-22). Daniel enjoyed uncommon promotions in the land of Babylon because of his unusual gifting (Dan. 2:19-48).

Truly, the gift of a man makes room for him. There is something within you that the world cannot do without. There is a divine treasure in you, yearning for expression. Discover that gift in you by making a prayer of enquiry to God. After the discovery, do not let it remain dormant; always use it for the benefit of others and for the Kingdom and you will enjoy continuous lifting. Indeed, your lifting will not come from without but from within.
