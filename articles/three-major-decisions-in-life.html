The Three Major Decisions in Life

There are three major decisions that every person will make at one point or the other of his or her life. These are:

  • Master — who to serve
  • Mission — what to do
  • Mate — who to marry

Master

The first major decision that every human being must make is who to serve. This decision is very important because it will determine where you will spend eternity.

Jesus said in Luke 16:13, “No servant can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other, or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.”

Some people (including certain Christians) hold the notion that there are many ways to get to God. They even support such an erroneous assertion with the common proverb, “there are many ways to kill a cat”. Stop right there!! You can kill a cat in many ways but friend, God is not a cat, you cannot get to Him through various ways. You can only get to God through Jesus Christ (John 14:6).

“For there is one God, and ONE MEDIATOR between God and men, THE MAN CHRIST JESUS” — I Timothy 2:5

Jesus Christ is either Master of all or He is not Master at all.

In the Book of John chapter 6, when most of the disciples were leaving Jesus because of His teaching, He asked the twelve whether they will also leave Him. Peter however told Him they have no other place to go (John 6:67). In this era where many people are turning their backs to Jesus, choose to cleave to Him and you will be blessed.

Joshua and his family chose to serve the only true God (Josh. 24:15). Ruth also made a decision to follow the God of Naomi and this decision launched her into a glorious future (Ruth 1:15-18). Elijah admonished all Israel to choose the God they want to serve (1 Kings 18:21).

We also accepted Jesus Christ as our Lord and Master many years ago, and He has never disappointed us.

If Jesus Christ is not your Master, you are not secured. If you are not saved, you are not safe. Pray this prayer before you continue reading.

“Lord Jesus, I confess that you are the Son of God, that you died for my sins and rose again on the third day. I accept you this day as my personal Lord and Saviour. Come into my life and take control, make me a better person. I want to live my life for you. From today, I forsake all my sinful ways and dedicate myself to the glory of God. Amen!”

Mission

This decision relates to your assignment, task or work in life. What kind of career do you want to pursue? If you are not doing the kind of job you were designed for, you will never find fulfillment in life.

This is the reason why you have to carefully decide on the kind of work you would like to do.

Your appropriate mission will guarantee your appropriate provision. Until you are walking in your mission you will not be in a position to receive the right provision. Your assignment will also guarantee your consignment.

Mate

If you have not decided to take an oath of celibacy (the choice not to marry), then you will definitely have to make a choice as to who to marry.

Although your joy is related to your relationship with the Holy Spirit, your happiness in life is related to the person you marry. The wrong choices made by people in the choice of their marriage partners have resulted in the shattering of their bright future.

May you never make a mistake in the choice of a partner in Jesus Name!

Forbidden Fruits in the Garden of Marriage

Genesis 2:16-17, “And the Lord God commanded the man, saying of every tree in the garden thou mayest freely eat but of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it: For in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die.”

When God created man, He placed him in a beautiful garden. Furthermore, God instructed man to freely eat every fruit in the Garden of Eden except the fruits from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. The struggles of man began the day that he disobeyed the commandment of God.

It is the same with marriage. Marriage is like a garden and in this garden, there are certain forbidden fruits. There are certain people you must never consider marrying. What you must understand is that “It is only the Word of God which is the final authority on any subject.”

The Bible says “Forever O Lord, thy Word is settled in Heaven” Psalm 119:89. While the world is trying to settle, the Word is already settled.

1 Corinthians 1:25 also says “…the foolishness of God is wiser than men and the weakness of God is stronger than men.”

Friend, you are not wiser than God. The all knowing God has laid down certain regulations in His Word to govern us in the choice of a marriage partner. I call these rules “the forbidden fruits” of marriage. All those who have gone in for these forbidden fruits in the school of marriage have never experienced peace in their lives. You may see some of these people smiling outside but deep within them they are very troubled. If you don’t want to be like them just be obedient to God’s Word no matter how you may be feeling and it shall surely be well with you. You must never prove in your own life what God has not approved in His Word and you must never qualify someone that God has disqualified.

Job 36:11, “If they obey and serve Him, they shall spend their days in prosperity, and their years in pleasures.”

People You Can Never Marry

Never marry an unbeliever

2 Corinthians 6:14, “Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers: for what fellowship hath righteousness with unrighteousness? And what communion hath light with darkness?”

As a born again believer, your standard for marriage is the written Word of God. If you want to succeed in life, then you must not allow logic or emotionalism to have an upper hand over the written Word of God. The first thing God created was light and darkness. The Bible however says that the next thing God did after creating light and darkness was to separate them. Friend, in the choice of a marriage partner, what God has put asunder, let no man put together!!

There is no reason or wisdom that is stronger than God’s Word. Many singles today see unbelievers as partners because of their material possessions, nice appearances, academic success, calmness, etc. There is usually the temptation to marry an unbeliever who is attracted to you with the hope of converting him/her. It is more common in a situation where the person meets all your standards apart from the fact that he/she is not a Christian.

“The convert and marry syndrome” is the most dangerous gamble that you can ever engage in. You don’t know tomorrow and you don’t have the power to convert any man. You must just obey the Word of God. As a Christian lady or guy, you can never marry a Muslim, Buddhist, an idol worshipper or anyone who has not accepted Christ as His Lord and personal Saviour and does not believe that Jesus Christ is the only way to God or Heaven.

Furthermore, even in the choice of a believer, you must carefully consider the doctrine of your prospective partner’s Church if they are in line with God’s Word. For instance, if you always pay your tithe but the Church of your prospective partner does not believe in tithing, there is bound to be future troubles.

Never consider a backslider as a marriage partner

James 5:19-20, “Brethren, if any of you do err from the truth, and one convert him; Let him know, that he which converteth THE SINNER from the error of his way shall save a soul from death, and shall hide a multitude of sins.”

A backslider is a person who was once saved but has now gone back into the world. James 5:20 calls a backslider a sinner. There is therefore no difference between a backslider and a sinner. There are many of such people in the world today who are deceiving ignorant Christians. Such backsliders know a lot of the “Kingdom jargons” and Scriptures and can come to you as “angels of light”. You must be careful to really wait on God for a clear direction. There may even be backsliders sitting in Churches and going through all the religious motions, but who are still far from God.

I heard a story of a man who was so desperate to marry one lady in a certain Church. Although the man had stopped going to church, he begun to move to the Church of that particular lady and even joined the “prayer department” in order to look spiritual. After about six months in the church, this man proposed marriage to that undiscerning lady who was on fire for God and she accepted. After a flamboyant wedding, the man who has been pretending for a very long time went back to his old ways of drinking, clubbing, listening to worldly music and other things. Marriage became unbearable for that innocent sister. What a pity!!

You must therefore simply obey the Word of God. Never allow your desperation to overrule God’s revelation from His Word.

Never marry a person who is in an already existing marriage

Polygamy is strictly forbidden by God. You must therefore never choose to marry someone who is already engaged to another person. If you disobey God in this area, you are simply “putting asunder what God has put together” and you will regret later on.

If you look at the marriage that God conducted in the Garden of Eden, it was between Adam and Eve only and not “Adam, Eve and Eva” or “Eve, Adam and Sadam”. You must therefore be very careful. Young lady, to accept to become a second wife is a direct contradiction to God’s infallible word. Young man, to be flirting with somebody’s wife and going ahead to marry her is an affront to God’s holy institution of marriage.

1 Corinthians 7:2, “Nevertheless, to avoid fornication, let every man have his own wife, and let every woman have her own husband.” Titus 1:6, “If any be blameless, the husband of one wife…”

God never supports polygamy because in the beginning it was not so.

Never marry a lesbian or a homosexual

You see marriage as originally designed by God is “one man, one woman”. On the contrary, lesbianism is a relationship between a woman and her fellow woman whilst homosexuality is a relationship between a man and his fellow man.

No matter the number of reasons that human right activists are putting across, it is clearly forbidden in God’s Word. If you marry a lesbian or a homosexual, you will never have peace in your marriage and sexual abuse will become common in your home.

Once again, God conducted marriage between “Adam and Eve” and not “Adam and Steve” or “Eve and Eva”.
