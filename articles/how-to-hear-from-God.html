How to Hear From God

Many people make a mistake of running ahead of God. They take hasty steps and at the end of the day live frustrated lives. It is those who hear from Heaven who eventually impact their world. Every glorious destiny thrives on account of “Thus saith the Lord.”

All successful people as well as great men always make reference to a clear word they received from the Lord. No great venture is established by goodwill and support of men but by a word from God. Therefore, listen to men with your ears but hear God with your heart, lend your ears to men but give your heart to God. Watch men but focus on God.

It is only what God says and shows to you that is error-free. Never mistake the words of men for the voice of God. Jeremiah 10:23 says categorically that “It is not in man to direct his steps”. To direct your own life is like personally directing a movie in which you are the main actor. Let God direct the affairs of your life. The voice you hear will determine the choice you make.

Therefore, decide to spend time in the presence of God to access His voice. Only decide on matters after prayerful consideration. Never make a choice without the consent and assent of God. Simply, HEAR FROM GOD ON EVERY ISSUE OF YOUR LIFE!

Stay in the Presence of God until you hear His voice — Jeremiah 23:21-22, “I have not sent these prophets, yet they ran. I have not spoken to them, yet they prophesied. But if they had stood in My counsel, And had caused My people to hear My words, Then they would have turned them from their evil way And from the evil of their doings.”
