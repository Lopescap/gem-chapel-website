Rev. Ebenezer Owusu-Ansah

Ga-West Regional Overseer, Greater Accra

Rev. Ebenezer Owusu-Ansah is a passionate lover of God and a compassionate lover of people. He carries a special teaching and prayer mandate and has raised a lot of giants in the Kingdom. Rev Eben has established many branches of Gem Chapel all over the Greater Accra Region, Eastern Region and across the globe.

He holds a BSc Degree in Human Resource from the University of Ghana Business School and a Master's Degree in Biblical Theology from the Great Expectations Theological College of Anointing.

God has used him to perform notable and diverse miracles and his weekly Atmosphere of Glory and Miracles has been a blessing to many people.

As a prolific author, Rev Eben has co-authored 5 books with his supernatural twin Apostle Steve and has consistently helped in the writing and the publishing of some of the books of the General Overseers.
