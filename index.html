Our Story

Born from Great
Expectations

God showed the General Overseer, Bishop Dr. Kwame Owusu-Ansah, a vivid picture of his future and gave him a mandate to preach the Word of faith to the world. “As you do so, great expectations will be born in the hearts of people which will in turn produce great manifestations in their lives.”

Established on November 10, 1989 in Kumasi, Ghana with just a family and a few converts, GEM Chapel has grown into a vibrant ministry with branches across Ghana and beyond — where the blind see, the lame walk, and lives are transformed by God’s power.

What We Do

Advancing God’s Kingdom

Through every means possible, we spread the Word — in cities, villages, prisons, and beyond.

Crusades & Rallies

Powerful gospel rallies and crusades throughout Ghana where signs, wonders, and miracles confirm the preached Word.

Fresh Fire Devotional

Our quarterly devotional “Fresh Fire for Today” has been a blessing to multitudes around the world for over 16 years.

Prison & Orphanage Ministry

Regular visits to prisons, orphanages, and care homes to engage in charity works and share the love of Christ.

Easter Camp Meeting

Our flagship annual convention where the faithful gather to wait on God and be empowered to go and do exploits for His Kingdom.

GEM Model Schools

Establishing Christian model schools to inculcate life-changing Kingdom principles in children at every branch location.

10-Day January Fast

A powerful annual tradition of fasting and prayer to begin each year, known for miracle, deliverance, and prayer services.

Anointed Leadership

KO

Bishop Dr. Kwame & Rev. Dr. Mrs. Beatrice Owusu-Ansah

Founders & General Overseers

Prolific authors of over 40 best-selling books, they have ministered extensively across the USA and Europe, raising Kingdom ministers and Christian workers across the globe.
SO

Apostle Stephen Owusu-Ansah

International Convener & Ashanti Regional Overseer

A passionate church planter who founded the World Changers Movement. His rallies and ministrations across Ghana and the UK have transformed lives through signs, wonders, and miracles.
EO

Rev. Ebenezer Owusu-Ansah

Ga-West Regional Overseer, Greater Accra

Carries a special teaching and prayer mandate. His weekly Atmosphere of Glory and Miracles has been a blessing to many, establishing branches across Greater Accra and beyond.
JO

Apostle Jonathan Owusu-Ansah

Regional Overseer, Ga-East, Greater Accra

A dedicated shepherd advancing the ministry in the Greater Accra Region, committed to raising disciples and expanding the Kingdom of God.
