How to Be Fruitful in the Land of Suffering

In short: I52 Joseph named his second son Ephraim, for he said, “God has made me fruitful in this land of my grief.”

51 Joseph named his older son Manasseh, for he said, “God has made me forget all my troubles and everyone in my father’s family.

— Genesis 41:51-52

I52 Joseph named his second son Ephraim, for he said, “God has made me fruitful in this land of my grief.”

t is one thing to experience significant suffering and to see with hindsight what God has done and how he has used it for good. It’s far more thrilling when we begin to allow God to use the suffering in our lives even while we are in the midst of it. God does not want to wait until everything is perfect in your life to begin to use you. He wants you to be fruitful right now even in the midst of your pain and suffering.

Joseph had many justifiable reasons to hold a grudge against his brothers; he loved them enough to send them food but they hated him enough to sell him as a slave to foreigners. He however decided to forget all their acts of wickedness against him. Joseph named his first son Manasseh meaning, I have forgotten and named his second son Ephraim which means fruitfulness – Gen. 41:51-52.

By this act, Joseph revealed the secret to being fruitful in the land of suffering – Forgetting the past. Unless you forget the past, you can never enter into the glorious future God has designed for you. You must be determined never to become a prisoner of your past; whether past failures or victories. You can simply not be driving on the highway of life and be looking back; you will have a fatal accident if you do that.

Always remember that before Ephraim (fruitfulness), there should be Manasseh (forgetfulness).

Prayer

O Lord, release my destiny from the prison of the past in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am fruitful in all I do!
