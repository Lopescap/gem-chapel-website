Don’t Barter Away Your Destiny

In short: In our world today, most people major in the minors and minor in the majors. This generation usually values the trivial and treats the important with contempt. As Isaac’s firstborn son, Esau was entitled to the birthright and inheritance.

Then Jacob said, “Swear to me this day.” So he swore to him, and he sold his birthright to Jacob. Then Jacob gave Esau bread and lentil stew. Then he ate and drank, arose, and went his way. Thus Esau despised his birthright.

— Genesis 25:33-34

In our world today, most people major in the minors and minor in the majors. This generation usually values the trivial and treats the important with contempt. As Isaac’s firstborn son, Esau was entitled to the birthright and inheritance. The birth right, which was a preserve of the firstborn simply meant that the owner of it was to experience a double-portion of his father’s inheritance. However, we do not speak of “Abraham, Isaac, and Esau.” Esau sold his birthright for just one meal. When Jacob offered food and asked for his birthright, Esau could have said, “No! It is not for sale, I will never sell my birthright” but he did not. He sold out the eternal for a moment’s temporal satisfaction.

Beloved, you were bought with a price--the precious Blood of Christ. Nothing in you or what you have is for sale. You and everything about you belongs to Jesus.

The devil will offer you monetary satisfaction as he reaches for your birthright. He will tell you it is all right to lie, steal, fornicate, have a side-chick apart from your wife, keep your tithe, all right to keep your offering and satisfy the lusts of the flesh and of this world. He will tempt you to sell your Kingdom commitment for monetary pleasure. He’ll encourage you to barter away your salvation and your godly inheritance. Resist the devil’s counsel. Like Daniel and his friends purposed in their hearts not to defile themselves with the king’s meat; NEVER SELL YOUR BIRTHRIGHT as a child of God.

Prayer

O Lord, always keep me from falling in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will never sacrifice the ultimate for the immediate!
