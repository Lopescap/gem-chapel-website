God Can Handle It

In short: In the realm of God, there is nothing which is impossible. What is beyond men is below God. Men can fail, but God will always prevail.

But Jesus beheld them, and said unto them, with men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible”.

— Matthew 19:26

In the realm of God, there is nothing which is impossible. What is beyond men is below God. Men can fail, but God will always prevail. No matter the challenge you face in life, always declare, “God can handle it.” True faith does not look at the intensity/gravity of a problem, but the immensity of God’s power. Let’s look at some scriptures that changed my view about God’s dealings with men:

Matt. 28:18, “And Jesus came and spoke unto them, saying, ALL POWER is given unto me in heaven and in earth”.

Psalm 62:11, “God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this, that Power belonged unto God”.

Mark 10:27, “And Jesus looking upon them saith, with men it is impossible, but not with God, for with God, all things are POSSIBLE”.

Luke 1:37, “For with God nothing shall be IMPOSSIBLE”. Luke 18:27, “And he said, the things which are impossible with men are POSSIBLE with God”

Jer. 32:17, “Ah Lord God I behold, Thou has made the heavens and the earth by thy great power and stretched out arm. And there is NOTHING TOO HARD FOR THEE?

Jer. 32:27, “Behold, I am the LORD, the God of all flesh: is there ANYTHING TOO HARD for me?

Gen. 18:14, “Is there ANYTHING TOO HARD for the LORD?

Job 42:1-2, “Then Job answered the Lord and said, I know that thou CANST DO EVERYTHING...”

It can be clearly seen that your situation is not above God. I want you to meditate on and memorize these scriptures for 2023. Pray it out and confess it in the face of opposition and you rise above the storms of your life. If God can’t, then nobody can.

Prayer

Father, forgive me for ever doubting You. God of impossibilities, turn my life around in JESUS NAME!

Confession

I believe that my God can do all things!
