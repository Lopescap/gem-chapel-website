How to Remain at the Top

In short: Never care for accolades; for entitlements are not in titles. Never seek for rewards. Never be bothered about who appreciates or do not appreciate you or your works.

1 ¶ Now the LORD had said unto Abram, Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and from thy father’s house, unto a land that I will shew thee: 2 And I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and make thy name great; and thou shalt be a blessing:
— Genesis 12:1-2

Never care for accolades; for entitlements are not in titles. Never seek for rewards. Never be bothered about who appreciates or do not appreciate you or your works.

Just keep reaching out and touching lives. Your reward is sure to come. The lives you touch are the ones that will keep spreading the values of your life, career and ministry

Leadership is about people and not position. A leader’s heartbeat is how to bless people.

You lose nothing blessing people especially the upcoming ones or the next generation. You will never go down raising others. You don’t get erased raising others. You rather get elevated doing so. Blessing others is sowing seeds into your future. In life what you do for others today is a seed; what is done to you by others tomorrow is your harvest.

You will find it there waiting for you as you gravitate towards the future. Serve as a leader who keeps elevating others and you will never find yourself going down. Be a person who keeps blessing and helping others and God will never stop blessing and helping you.

Be a linkage to the lifting of others and you will become an unforgettable reference point to them. God doesn’t just want to bless you; He wants you more importantly to become a blessing to your generation.

Prayer

O Lord, give me a generous heart in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am a blessing to my generation!
