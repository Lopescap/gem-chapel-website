Experiencing the Abundant Life

In short: The abundant life is the heritage and the redemptive right of every child of God (John 10:10). God wants to bless us to a level that we will not have enough room to contain it.

5 You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies; You anoint my head with oil; My cup runs over. 6 Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me All the days of my life; And I will dwell in the house of the LORD Forever.

— Psalm 23:4-5

The abundant life is the heritage and the redemptive right of every child of God (John 10:10). God wants to bless us to a level that we will not have enough room to contain it. Instead of material shortages, God wants you enjoy financial packages; instead of poverty, He wants to give you prosperity; instead of scarcity, He wants to give you plenty; instead of begging and borrowing, God wants you to be lending out to the nations of the world. However, to terminate the roots of material shortage, you must deal with its roots. At the root of material shortage is spiritual dryness; when your spirit is dry, your pocket will automatically be dry.

Running over blessing is an outcome of anointing.“...thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over” – Psalm 23:4 Your problem is not an enemy but the absence of oil. Ps.23:5. This is because in spite of the devil, you can live in abundance. Those who seek the outpouring of the spirit are never in short of blessing. Is.44:3 says, ‘For I will pour water on him who is thirsty, And floods on the dry ground; I will pour My Spirit on your descendants, And My blessing on your offspring;’

Freshness of the oil attracts favour for continuous supplies. In 1 Sam.10:1, 3-4, after Saul was anointed, he met people who liberally provided for his needs.

Spirituality was platform for material abundance in the first church - Act.4:29-34. This year, don’t joke with your spiritual life; pray more, feed on the word, fast regularly, attend every Church service, win souls and see how you will swim in the ocean of God’s blessings.

Prayer

O Lord, let me be rooted and grounded in you in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am rooted in God, I cannot lack!
