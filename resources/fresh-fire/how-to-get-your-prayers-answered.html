How to Get Your Prayers Answered!

In short: Soul wining is the heart beat of God. Do you want 100% answered prayer? Do you know why some of the things you have asked God have not come or have delayed? That’s the reason for the morning’s devotion.

Vrs.16 “Ye have not chosen me; but I have chosen you, and ordained you, that ye should go and bring forth fruit, and that your fruit should remain that whatsoever ye shall ask of the father in My name, He may give it to you
— John 15:16

Soul wining is the heart beat of God. Do you want 100% answered prayer? Do you know why some of the things you have asked God have not come or have delayed? That’s the reason for the morning’s devotion.

The Great Commission has now become the Great Omission. God has chosen us to win souls and make disciples of them, thus our first fruit must remain. When our first fruit remains, we have an open cheque; whatsoever we ask of the Father shall be given to us. God loves man that why He sent His only beloved Son to die to set man free.

So the reason why some of our prayers have not been answered is because we haven’t borne our first fruits to remain.

If you can get a soul won and a disciple made, you will get a prayer answered. Therefore make soul winning your daily activity and you will not lack anything. As you make heaven happy, you will be happy. God bless you for your obedience .

Prayer

Lord, make me love and win souls everyday.

Confession

I bear fruit and my fruit remains!
