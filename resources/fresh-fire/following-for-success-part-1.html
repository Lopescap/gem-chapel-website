Following for Success — Part 1

In short: Man is never created to be self-dependent; he is created to be a follower. When he ceases to follow he will certainly head for doom. A Disciple is a follower i.e. one called to follow.

Be ye therefore followers of God, as dear children;
— Ephesians 5:1

Man is never created to be self-dependent; he is created to be a follower. When he ceases to follow he will certainly head for doom. A Disciple is a follower i.e. one called to follow. He is one who has resigned from his personal interest with an absolute sense of abandonment. If you really want to make impact in life, you must be a life-long follower.

Stages of his following includes

1. God - for direction, divine guidance and fulfillment. It is in

following God that he derives his satisfaction. Outside of God, he remains empty, Eph 5:1. God is the porter; man is the clay. As you follow Him, He will bring out the best in you. Matthew 4:19 says, “And he saith unto them, Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.”

2. Good leaders - for inspiration, reference, examples & models,

human guidance and mentorship. You must however follow a leader who is following Christ,.Heb 6:12 says, “ That ye be not slothful, but followers of them who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

Himself - for inner witness and personal conviction, conscience

& personal principles.

Who you follow determines what follows you – Ps 23:1-6. Who you follow gives you access into the spirit of the one you follow, Num 27:15-20.

When Joshua followed Moses, he ended up operating like Moses. No man is an island. Everyone learns from someone. Anyone who is being referred to today had a reference point. Follow seriously to end gloriously.

Prayer

O Lord, help me to follow You unto the end.

Confession

I am a good follower!
