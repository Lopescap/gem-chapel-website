Prayer Works Wonders — Part 2

In short: No matter the challenge you face this year, take it to God in prayer. Our heavenly Father’s ears are always opened to hear the cry of His children on earth. However, nothing sustains intercession like revelation. Hos.14:2.

Take words with you, And return to the LORD. Say to Him, “Take away all iniquity; Receive us graciously, For we will offer the sacrifices of our lips.
— Hosea 14:2

No matter the challenge you face this year, take it to God in prayer. Our heavenly Father’s ears are always opened to hear the cry of His children on earth. However, nothing sustains intercession like revelation. Hos.14:2. Word content is what supplies us with strength in prayer. Word content also sustains length of prayer – you never get tired praying the word. Angels come for our words not our noise or cry in prayer . Dan.10:12, ‘Then he said to me, “Do not fear, Daniel, for from the first day that you set your heart to understand, and to humble yourself before your God, your words were heard; and I have come because of your words.’ When you speak the Word in prayer, angels will pick for God to fix.

God’s word is wood that we cast into prayer fire so as to sustain its burning. Mic.6:9; 7:14; Hag.1:8; Lev.6:12 -13. Just as fire comes up with new wood cast into it so also is prayer with the injection of fresh revelation.

Men of the word are never prayer wearied. Efficiency in prayer is a function of effectiveness in the use of scriptures. The skilful application of scriptures is what makes prayer useful.

Our cry will at best attract attention and sympathy; it is our words that obtains result.

The word enriches our prayer time.

What God responded to was prayer of Hezekiah not His cry. Is.38:1- 5. Right now, rise up, take the Word and bombard Heaven with your prayer arrows.

Proverbs 28:9, ‘He that turneth away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer shall be abomination’. In this second half of the year, always pray on the basis of God’s Word.

Prayer

O Lord, make me a Word-addict this year in Jesus Name!

Confession

My prayer will produce wonders!
