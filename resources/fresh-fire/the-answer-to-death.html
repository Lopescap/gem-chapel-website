The Answer to Death

In short: Death is the ultimate that the devil can do and resurrection is the answer to death. The devil thought that the demise of Jesus Christ was permanent and that He will be in the grave forever.

But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwell in you, he that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal bodies by his Spirit that dwelleth in you.

— Romans 8:11

Death is the ultimate that the devil can do and resurrection is the answer to death. The devil thought that the demise of Jesus Christ was permanent and that He will be in the grave forever. What they did not know was that the grave was too small and too weak to contain Jesus – 1 Corinthians 2:8 says, ‘Which none of the princes of this world knew: for had they known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.’

After the resurrection, Jesus declared that all power on earth and in Heaven has been given unto Him. This means that when you are connected to Jesus Christ, you are connected to immeasurable POWER! Your days of powerlessness are over in Jesus Name!

The resurrection power expresses itself in the following ways;

The power of life – Because Jesus lives, you will also

live; nothing is permitted to die in your life – John 14:9, Rom. 8:11

2. The power of Hope – Eccl. 4:9 says that, ‘For to him that

is joined to all the living there is HOPE...’

The power over sin – We are not just saved from sin, we

are also given power over sin – II Cor. 5:15, Acts 4:26

4. The Power over the devil and all his works – Col. 2:15,

Heb. 2:14

By the resurrection power, sickness, poverty, sorrow, affliction are destroyed in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord, envelope me with Your resurrection power in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am walking in the resurrection power!
