The Value-System of Heaven

In short: The measuring Scale of God is different from that of men because the values of God are completely different from those of men. The value-system of Heaven is different from that of men.

But the LORD said unto Samuel, Look not on his countenance, or on the height of his stature; because I have refused him: for the LORD seeth not as man seeth; for man looketh on the outward appearance, but the LORD looketh on the heart” 2Co 10:7, “Do ye look on things after the outward appearance?

— 1 Samuel 16:7

The measuring Scale of God is different from that of men because the values of God are completely different from those of men. The value-system of Heaven is different from that of men. Man judges the actions of others but God judges the motives behind those actions. God places premium on man’s heart than His act. As a matter of fact, the number one demand he places on man is for his heart. Proverbs 23:26 says, “My son, give me thine heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways”.

Men commend us when they see our acts but God approves us when He sees our heart. Worldly values and priorities often clash with God’s Perspective. Luke 16:15 says, “What is highly esteemed among men is an abomination in the sight of God.” Our acts, no matter the number can never weigh as much as our heart before God. It is intention of heart that gives value to the extension of hands. The question is “Why did you do what you did and why are you doing what you are doing?” Is it for selfish reasons, the praise of men or fame?

David earned God’s approval not for His act but for his heart. I Sam. 13:14. God sees our hearts before he notices our acts (I Sam. 16:7, II Chron. 16:9). God weighs your heart to measure your part (Acts 8:21). God demands another heart to deliver your signs (I Sam. 10:9). God values services that come from genuine hearts than excellent acts (Col. 3:23). The state of your heart determines your place on earth. May God give you a right heart in Jesus Name! One day, when you stand before God, may you receive the Master’s well-done.

Prayer

O Lord, give me a new heart in Jesus Name!

Confession

I only seek the affirmation of Heaven!
