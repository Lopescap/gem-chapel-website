The Faith That Wins — Part 5

In short: Faith is an overcoming force; it subdues the enemies of our destinies. Today, we want to learn the last attribute of the winning faith.

For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.

— James 2:26

Faith is an overcoming force; it subdues the enemies of our destinies. Today, we want to learn the last attribute of the winning faith.

Winning faith is an acting faith

Faith without action is fake. Faith is not just a fact but more importantly an act. Positive action is a clear evidence of faith at work. The Bible tells us that “Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet ... prepared an ark to the saving of his house...” Heb. 11:7. Also, when Abraham was called of God, by faith he obeyed and departed. Gen. 12:1-4. Furthermore, the Bible says, “Above all, taking the shield of faith, where with ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked.” Eph. 6:16. So, faith is a weapon of war. In order to obtain the breakthrough you desire, you have to put the weapon to good use. Until you fight, you can’t win! The kingdom of God suffereth violence, and the violent takes it by force. That force is the force of faith. When you engage in a faith battle, it is a good fight, because the end will be favourable to you. 1 Jn. 5:4.

James 2:20 also says But wilt thou know, O vain man, that faith without works is dead?

Faith must not just be spoken but also acted. Jesus Christ did not just hear about the faith of the friends of the paralytic but He also SAW THEIR FAITH THROUGH THEIR ACTION.

Mark 1:1-5, ‘1 ¶ And again He entered Capernaum after some days, and it was heard that He was in the house. 2 Immediately many gathered together, so that there was no longer room to receive them, not even near the door. And He preached the word to them. 3 Then they came to Him, bringing a paralytic who was carried by four men.4 And when they could not come near Him because of the crowd, they uncovered the roof where He was. So when they had broken through, they let down the bed on which the paralytic was lying. 5 When Jesus saw their faith, He said to the paralytic, “Son, your sins are forgiven you.” For instance, if you want a car, go to the driving school and acquire a drivers’ licence and very soon your car will come; when you need a baby, get a baby court and very soon you will hear the cry of a baby in your house. THE MORE FAITH STEPS YOU TAKE, THE CLOSER YOU WILL BE TO YOUR DESIRED MIRACLE.

Prayer

O Lord, help me never to give-up in life in Jesus Name!

Confession

I always take giant steps of faith!
