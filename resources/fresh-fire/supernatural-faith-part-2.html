Supernatural Faith — Part 2

In short: possible, it means it is feasible, attainable, within reach, within bounds and can be fulfilled.

1 ¶ Then Job answered the LORD, and said, 2 I know that thou canst do every thing, and that no thought can be withholden from thee
— Job 42:1-2

GOD DWELLS IN A REALM WHERE IMPOSSIBILITIES

CANNOT EXIST. In fact, the word impossibility is a creation

of man and can only be found in the dictionary of man. In the

dictionary of God, there is no word like, ‘impossible.’ Therefore, when

you place a limit on God, you place a limit on your life because there

are certain things that only God can handle! When a thing is said to be

possible, it means it is feasible, attainable, within reach, within bounds and can be fulfilled.

Now let’s read I Kings 17:2-6,

I Kings 17: 2 ¶ Andword of the LORD came unto him, saying, 3 Get thee hence, and turn thee eastward, and hide thyself by the brook Cherith, that is before Jordan. 4 And it shall be, that thou shalt drink of the brook; and I have commanded the ravens to feed thee there. 5 So he went and did according unto the word of the LORD: for he went and dwelt by the brook Cherith, that is before Jordan.6 And the ravens brought him bread and flesh in the morning, and bread and flesh in the evening; and he drank of the brook.

There are two major principles that stand out from the above passage;

EVERYTHING HAS EARS FOR GOD

God commanded the RAVENS

This means that God understands the language of birds. He had communications with the ravens and they complied. God can speak to your bank account, He can speak to your car; He can command anything under Heaven and they will obey.

GOD CAN MAKE THE UNWILLING DO HIS WILL

Ravens are birds with unquenchable appetite. It is said that a raven eats more than a vulture. It swallows up its young ones it gives birth to because of hunger. It is from raven that we get the word, ‘ravenous’ which means voracious. YET THOSE WERE THE MESSENGERS GOD SENT WITH FOOD FOR ELIJAH. The ones that do not give food to anybody, who are permanently looking for food, were the ones God sent to give food to His prophet. GOD SUSPENDED THE RAVENS APPETITE and they simply could not eat the food because they were under a command. This means that, God can use anyone to favour you and to bless you. Indeed, we serve a God of possibilities! From today, may you never doubt God in your life.

Prayer

O Lord, let everything become possible in my life in Jesus Name!

Confession

Nothing shall be impossible for me!
