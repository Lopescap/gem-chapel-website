Persevere No Matter What!

In short: Most people fail in life not because they lack knowledge or talent but because they quit . When problems seem insurmountable, quitting may look like the easiest way out. It is true for every marriage, ministry, job and relationship.

1 ¶ Therefore we also, since we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses, let us lay aside every weight, and the sin which so easily ensnares us, and let us run with endurance the race that is set before us, 2 looking unto Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith, who for the joy that was set before Him endured the cross, despising the shame, and has sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.

— Hebrews 12:1-2

Most people fail in life not because they lack knowledge or talent but because they quit . When problems seem insurmountable, quitting may look like the easiest way out. It is true for every marriage, ministry, job and relationship. The lack of persistence is the trait of losers. Life is a race and in the race of life we run with endurance. Even Jesus Christ had to endure the pains of the cross in order that He might obtain the joy that was set before Him (Hebrews 12:1-2).

The Apostle Paul was a man who run with endurance. In spite of all the challenges He faced in life, he still did not give up. In II Corinthians 4:8-9, he wrote, ‘8 ¶ We are hard pressed on every side, yet not crushed; we are perplexed, but not in despair; 9 persecuted, but not forsaken; struck down, but not destroyed’

At the end of the journey of his life; the Apostle Paul emerged victorious in spite of all the odds he faced - In II Timothy 4:7-8, he said triumphantly ‘ I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.8 Finally, there is laid up for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will give to me on that Day, and not to me only but also to all who have loved His appearing.’

One man said, ‘A man is a hero not because he is braver than anyone else, but because he is brave for ten minutes longer’.

The Holy Spirit wants me to tell a Fresh Fire user today to hold on a little longer; never give up because you are at the edge of your miracle. Your night will be over soon; there is light waiting for you at the end of the tunnel. Run with endurance! Persevere no matter what!

Prayer

O Lord, give me the spirit of persistence in Jesus Name!

Confession

Giving up is not an option for me!
