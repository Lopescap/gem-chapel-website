You Must Always Be Thankful

In short: Small things matter to God. We must not wait to see big things before we thank God. There is nothing too small to thank God for. There should be no difference between our thanksgiving for seemingly little things and big things.

In everything give thanks; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you.
— 1 Thessalonians 5:18

Small things matter to God. We must not wait to see big things before we thank God. There is nothing too small to thank God for. There should be no difference between our thanksgiving for seemingly little things and big things. For instance, when God delivers you from a minor headache, your thanksgiving and appreciation must be the same if He had delivered you from a major asthma attack.

Great men don’t make assumptions; they mind details .A fool keeps waiting for ‘big bang’ to give big thanks. The difference between the one and nine lepers is that he saw what others referred to as little and insignificant, and he magnified it. It was this same leper who later on experienced wholeness in his body. All the other nine lepers experienced a fraction of the healing virtue but only the thankful leper experienced his total and permanent deliverance from leprosy. Luke 17: 17-19, ’17 And Jesus answering said, Were there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine? 18 There are not found that returned to give glory to God, save this stranger. 19 And he said unto him, Arise, go thy way: thy faith hath made thee whole.’

To qualify for big things, give thanks to God for small things. Do not slight His acts if you don’t want to be slighted by Him. Micah 2:1-2 says, ‘1 ¶ “And now, O priests, this commandment is for you. 2 If you will not hear, And if you will not take it to heart, To give glory to My name,” Says the LORD of hosts, “I will send a curse upon you, And I will curse your blessings. Yes, I have cursed them already, Because you do not take it to heart.’ Don’t ignore small things because it is small things that make a big thing to happen. God doesn’t have problem blessing you with big things if you will only acknowledge him for ‘small’ things. Those who regard God’s act as insignificant will be reduced to insignificance in his eyes. Learn to ALWAYS THANK HIM FOR EVERYTHING; THE SMALL AS WELL AS THE BIG THINGS HE DOES FOR YOU!

Prayer

O Lord, give me a heart of gratitude in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am always thankful!
