The Leading Grace

In short: There is a Grace that will cause you to take the lead in all you do. It causes you to emerge as the head even among heads and to shine brighter even among stars (Deut. 28:13). In life, you go ahead by Grace and you also become the head by Grace.

Who art thou, O great mountain? before Zerubbabel thou shalt become a plain: and he shall bring forth the headstone thereof with shoutings, crying, Grace, grace unto it.

— Zechariah 4:7

There is a Grace that will cause you to take the lead in all you do. It causes you to emerge as the head even among heads and to shine brighter even among stars (Deut. 28:13). In life, you go ahead by Grace and you also become the head by Grace. Zechariah 4:7, ‘Who art thou, O great mountain? before Zerubbabel thou shalt become a plain: and he shall bring forth the headstone thereof with shoutings, crying, Grace, grace unto it.’

Esther - Esther 2:17, ‘And the king loved Esther above all the women, and she obtained grace and favour in his sight more than all the virgins; so that he set the royal crown upon her head, and made her queen instead of Vashti’ Joseph – AMONG STARS HE WAS THE STAR – Gen. 37:9, ‘And he dreamed yet another dream, and told it his brethren, and said, Behold, I have dreamed a dream more; and, behold, the sun and the moon and the eleven stars made obeisance to me.’ This Grace that was upon Joseph was so strong that, in the house of Portiphar, he was the head; in the prison, he was the head and in the palace of Pharaoh, he was the head. Daniel – AMONG PRESIDENTS, HE WAS THE PRESIDENT Daniel 12:1-3, ‘1 ¶ It pleased Darius to set over the kingdom an hundred and twenty princes, which should be over the whole kingdom 2 And over these three presidents; of whom Daniel was first: that the princes might give accounts unto them, and the king should have no damage. 3 Then this Daniel was preferred above the presidents and princes, because an excellent spirit was in him; and the king thought to set him over the whole realm.’

Prayer

O Lord, empower me shine no matter what in Jesus Name!
