Be Strong in the Lord!

In short: Those who dominate in the spiritual realm will automatically dominate in the physical realm. This is because everything is a spiritual situation before it becomes a physical condition. In this New Year, you must be fervent in spirit.

Not slothful in business; fervent in spirit; serving the Lord;

— Romans 12:11

Those who dominate in the spiritual realm will automatically dominate in the physical realm. This is because everything is a spiritual situation before it becomes a physical condition. In this New Year, you must be fervent in spirit. This simply means, you must not joke with your spiritual life. Never allow the activities and the busy-ness of life to kill your relationship and intimacy with God. To be fervent in spirit, you must engage in the following spiritual exercises

1. Be Persistent in Prayer: To be persistent means to hold firm or

steadfastly to something. Despite the odds, obstacles, defeats and set-backs, you stick to it. A classic example in the Bible is blind Bartimaeus. He screamed, ‘Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me’ People in the crowd said, ‘Shut up and die in your blindness’. He ignored them and simply kept on crying to Jesus until he was called out of the crowd. This year, ignore all the anti-prayer agents in your life (TV, too much conversation, too much sleep, etc) and continue to scream to God in prayer if you want to make it.

Study the Bible : Joshua 1:8 says ‘This Book of the Law shall

not depart out of thy mouth but thou shall meditate on it day and night...’. You must be addicted to the Word. Always carry your Bible around you and empty its deposits into your heart. Always live by the standards of the Word of God.

3. Wait on God in fasting: Fasting is the accelerator pedal of prayer.

As you learn the art of waiting on God, you will end as a heavy- weight in the spirit. Paul was a fervent in the spirit because he loved to fast. 2 Corinthians 11:27, ‘ In weariness and painfulness, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often , in cold and nakedness.’

Prayer

O Lord increase my zeal for spiritual things in this New Year in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am strong in the Lord!

Scriptures Referenced

  • Romans
  • Joshua
  • 2 Corinthians
