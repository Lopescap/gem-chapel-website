The Faith That Wins — Part 1

In short: Faith is the principal instrument of breakthrough in every endeavour of life. It is what beautifies destinies and adds colour to the dignity of believers. It is the critical factor that influences every man’s status.

For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith.

— 1 John 5:4

Faith is the principal instrument of breakthrough in every endeavour of life. It is what beautifies destinies and adds colour to the dignity of believers. It is the critical factor that influences every man’s status. Faith is not just a mere confession; it is a great profession. It is the wisdom of God you need to get to your place of glory and honour in Christ. Everything about God and your destiny begins with faith. Furthermore, the foundation of every vision and its fulfilment anchor on faith.

Without faith it is impossible to move God, and when you cannot move Him, it becomes absolutely impossible to receive anything from Him. Heb. 11:6. So, without faith there is no future in your future.

Moreover, victory becomes a way of life when faith is actively at work in you . Contrary to the world’s belief system which places emphasis on dreams, goals and self which in turn subjected man to various destructive tendencies; absolute faith in God is what makes the journey of life great. The Bible makes this clear in Jeremiah 17:5- 8, “... Cursed be the man that trusteth in man, and maketh flesh his arm, and whose heart departeth from the Lord. For he shall be like the heath in the desert, and shall not see when good cometh... Blessed is the man that trusteth in the Lord, and whose hope the Lord is...” Beloved, without faith you are bound to fail. Jesus said to Peter, “I’ve prayed for you that thy faith faileth not.” Lk. 22:32. That means you and I don’t have any future without faith. There is a kind of faith that gives you an upper hand in the battles of life and ensures all-round victory no matter what we face in life. Faith is the key that opens the door of victory. Victory becomes a way of life when faith is actively at work in you.

Ephesians 6:16 says, ‘16 Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked.’

Prayer

O Lord, baptize me with a fresh Spirit of faith in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am winning by faith!
