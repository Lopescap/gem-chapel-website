The Faith That Wins — Part 2

In short: Faith is a winning force; it is the highest exponent in the equation of victory. The following are the attributes of the winning faith;

But let us, who are of the day, be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love; and for an helmet, the hope of salvation
— 1 Thessalonians 5:8

Faith is a winning force; it is the highest exponent in the equation of victory. The following are the attributes of the winning faith;

Winning faith is a holding faith

Sometimes, the arrows of the enemy may move towards your direction in quick succession and you may feel like giving up but what gives us victory at the end of the day is our ability to hold on to the Word. Hebrews 10:35 says, ‘ Cast not away therefore your confidence, which hath great recompence of reward.’

David said in Psalm 119:49, ‘Remember the word unto thy servant, upon which thou hast caused me to hope’

You can simply not separate mighty men of faith from their Bibles. It doesn’t matter what they face, they use the Word to achieve the needed victory. They hold on to the Word even when everything and everyone is against them.

One of the mighty men of David was called Eleazar. One day in the midst of a battle against the Philistines he grew weary but he held on to the sword until he achieved a great victory for Israel - II Samuel 23:8-10, ‘8 ¶ These are the names of the mighty men whom David had: Josheb-Basshebeth the Tachmonite, chief among the captains. He was called Adino the Eznite, because he had killed eight hundred men at one time. 9 And after him was Eleazar the son of Dodo, the Ahohite, one of the three mighty men with David when they defied the Philistines who were gathered there for battle, and the men of Israel had retreated.10 He arose and attacked the Philistines until his hand was weary, and his hand stuck to the sword. The LORD brought about a great victory that day; and the people returned after him only to plunder.’

Like Eleazer, Christians who want to win by faith must learn to hold on to their swords (the Word of God) until the needed victory is achieved. There is a great victory awaiting you if you can hold on to God’s Word to the end!

Prayer

O Lord, uproot every seed of discouragement from my heart in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am enjoying great victories!
