Never Stop Going to Church

In short: This year, you must decide to always be present in Church whenever there is a meeting. You must say like David, ‘I was glad when they said unto me, let us go into the House of the Lord- Psalm 122:1.

But you have come to Mount Zion and to the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem, to an innumerable company of angels

— Hebrews 12:22

This year, you must decide to always be present in Church whenever there is a meeting. You must say like David, ‘I was glad when they said unto me, let us go into the House of the Lord- Psalm 122:1. Another name for the Church is Zion, a place where God strengthens and helps His children.

What’s your reason for not going to Church? Before you answer, read 9 reasons why I never wash!

I was forced to wash as a child

People who wash are all hypocrites; they think they are cleaner

than everyone else

There are so many different kinds of soap; I just can’t decide

which one is best for me.

4. I used to wash but I got bored and stopped doing it.

I only wash on special occasions like Christmas and Easter

None of my friends wash

I will start washing when I get older and dirtier

8. I don’t have time to wash.

The people who make soap are only after your money! Sound

familiar?

There are truths taught in God’s House that you won’t hear anywhere else. There you will find a spiritual family to belong to, a faith to live by and a focus that gets your eyes where they should be; on Christ. From today, may you never forsake the assembling of the saints. The best place to always be is the Church!

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to always love Your House in Jesus Name!

Confession

I love the House of God!
