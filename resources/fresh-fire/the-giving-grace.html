The Giving Grace

In short: The Grace of God is the maker of men; no glorious destiny can be constructed without the ingredient of Grace.

Unto me, who am less than the least of all saints, is this grace given, that I should preach among the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ

— Ephesians 3:8

The Grace of God is the maker of men; no glorious destiny can be constructed without the ingredient of Grace. So, when Paul was giving a summary of his exploits in the ministry, he stated emphatically without no apology ‘I am what I am by the Grace of God....’ – I Cor. 15:10. That is why the Apostle Paul in his letter to his spiritual son Timothy admonished him to be strong in the Grace of God - 2Timothy 2:1, ‘¶ Thou therefore, my son, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus. ‘

There is a Sacrificial or Giving dimension of Grace

When this Grace comes upon you, you are joyful to spend and be spent for the Kingdom of God. You will do everything, give everything and go anywhere for God.

Paul - Ephesians 3:8 Unto me, who am less than the least of all saints, is this grace given, that I should preach among the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ;

The Macedonian Church

II Corinthians 8:1-4, ‘1 ¶ Moreover, brethren, we make known to you the grace of God bestowed on the churches of Macedonia:2 that in a great trial of affliction the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty abounded in the riches of their liberality. 3 For I bear witness that according to their ability, yes, and beyond their ability, they were freely willing, 4 imploring us with much urgency that we would receive the gift and the fellowship of the ministering to the saints. 5 And not only as we had hoped, but they first gave themselves to the Lord, and then to us by the will of God. 6 So we urged Titus, that as he had begun, so he would also complete this grace in you as well. 7 ¶ But as you abound in everything--in faith, in speech, in knowledge, in all diligence, and in your love for us--see that you abound in this grace also.’

Prayer

O Lord, let the giving Grace come strongly upon me in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am ready to sacrifice my all for the Kingdom!
