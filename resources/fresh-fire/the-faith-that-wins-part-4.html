The Faith That Wins — Part 4

In short: When faith stands up, every negative storm in your life goes down. The other attributes of the winning faith are;

11 ¶ Moreover the word of the LORD came unto me, saying, Jeremiah, what seest thou? And I said, I see a rod of an almond tree. 12 Then said the LORD unto me, Thou hast well seen: for I will hasten my word to perform it.
— Jeremiah 1:11-12

When faith stands up, every negative storm in your life goes down. The other attributes of the winning faith are;

Winning faith is a seeing faith

When it comes to the school of faith, what you must understand is that your sight will determine your size. IF YOU CAN SEE WELL, YOU WILL END WELL. That is why when God wanted to strengthen the faith of Abraham, he re-ordered his focus by giving him new pictures. He wanted him to see the future in the present. Abraham was given the vision of his children as numerous as the dust and the stars of Heaven. God made him meditate on the vision that He was implanting into his spirit day and night. In the daytime he meditated on the dust. In the night- time he meditated on the stars. Day and night God caused him to meditate and visualize on what He wanted to do in his life. No wonder in the long run what was a mere promise became a fulfilment - Gen. 13:16 “I will make your descendants as the dust of the earth; so that if one can count the dust of the earth, your descendants also can be counted. In Gen. 15:5 Look toward heaven, and number the stars, if you are able to number them.” Then He said to him, “So shall your descendants be.” 4. Winning faith is speaking faith

Heb. 3:1 Therefore, holy brethren, who share in a heavenly call, consider Jesus, the apostle and high priest of our confession. Heb. 4:14 Since then we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus, the Son of God, he lets us hold fast our confession. See every time Jesus is mentioned as a high priest, our confession is mentioned. Jesus takes what you confess and say it out to the Father. Your words can truly change your world. Three times in Mark 11: 23, when Jesus was teaching on faith, He emphasized on what you say. Verse 23 Truly, I say to you, whoever says (that is the first time the word say comes) to this mountain, “Be taken up and cast into the sea,” and does not doubt in his heart, but believe that that what he says (second time) will come to pass, it will be done for him (he will have whatever he says {the third time} in place of it will be done for him.)

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to see and speak only the right things in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will surely end well!
