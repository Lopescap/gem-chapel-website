Be God-Focused — Part 2

In short: In the journey of Christianity, many start but few are able to finish. Like any other race, if you lose focus, you will not emerge victorious at the end of the day. In the race of Christianity, our FOCUS IS JESUS CHRIST.

¶ Therefore we also, since we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses, let us lay aside every weight, and the sin which so easily ensnares us, and let us run with endurance the race that is set before us, 2 looking unto Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith, who for the joy that was set before Him endured the cross, despising the shame, and has sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.
— Hebrews 12:1-2, 1

In the journey of Christianity, many start but few are able to finish. Like any other race, if you lose focus, you will not emerge victorious at the end of the day. In the race of Christianity, our FOCUS IS JESUS CHRIST. If we fail to look unto Him we will not be able to finish the race at the end of the day.

In our generation, many people are seeking the peripherals and have neglected the main thing. Instead of the Blesser, we just seek the blessing; instead of the Giver of the gifts, we just seek after the gifts; instead of the Benefactor, we just seek the benefits; instead of the Manufacturer, we just seek the products; instead of the Director, we just seek direction; instead of the Presence of God, we just settle for the promises of God; and instead of the FACE OF GOD, we just seek the Hand of God.

But there was something about Moses, he was not satisfied with anything but the FACE OF GOD. Moses knew that God’s favour flows where His face shows. Exodus 33:18-19, ‘18 And he said, “Please, show me Your glory.” 19 Then He said, “I will make all My goodness pass before you, and I will proclaim the name of the LORD before you. I will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and I will have compassion on whom I will have compassion.”

From today, let the Presence of God be your daily delight.

Prayer

O Lord, show me Your face in Jesus Name!

Confession

I desire only God!
