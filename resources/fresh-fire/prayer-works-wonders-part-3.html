Prayer Works Wonders — Part 3

In short: If there is a man to pray, then there is surely a God to answer! He is the prayer answering God and that’s why everyone must run to Him – Psalm 65:2.

28 ¶ Now it came to pass, about eight days after these sayings, that He took Peter, John, and James and went up on the mountain to pray. 29 As He prayed, the appearance of His face was altered, and His robe became white and glistening.
— Luke 9:28-29

If there is a man to pray, then there is surely a God to answer! He is the prayer answering God and that’s why everyone must run to Him – Psalm 65:2.

Sanctification is the mother of manifestations. You can therefore only see the hand of God in your life if you are living holy. Hebrews 12:14 says, ‘Follow peace with all men and holiness without which no man can see God’

However, one of the greatest exponents in the equation of holiness is prayer. Prayer enhances sanctification. Prayer room is a “holy” room because it is the room of the “Holy” God.

So, prayer will make you resistant to sin. In prayer we meet with the holy God where defilement gives way to divinity. In prayer we are purified, refined and sanctified. One day, Jesus took three of His disciples to the mountain to pray. In the midst of prayer, the garment of Jesus became white which is symbolic of the impartation of the Spirit of holiness - Lk.9:28-30; Is.6:1-7. The Bible says in Eccl. 9:8, ‘Let thy garments always be white and let not your head lack ointment’

You can’t be truly prayerful and enjoy sin. In prayer our taste for sin is destroyed and our taste for righteousness is activated.

Get a man to pray and you will succeed at getting him out of sin. When a person gives himself continually to prayer, the negative addictions of sin fade away. In prayer we look at God and thereafter look like God. In this second half of the year, give yourself to prayer if you desire to live holy. Have a daily prayer schedule. Watch and pray so that you will not enter into temptation. YOU CANNOT REMAIN SINFUL IF YOU ARE GENUINELY PRAYERFUL. You can be sanctified through prayer. Prayer works wonders.

Prayer

O Lord, uproot every trace of sin from my life in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am seriously addicted to prayer!
