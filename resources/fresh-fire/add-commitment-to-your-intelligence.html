Add Commitment to Your Intelligence

In short: The best of life always demands the best of you. Commitment! It is what separates doers from dreamers; successful people from failures and great men from the mediocre. In all probabilities, commitment is superior to intelligence.

Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest.
— Ecclesiastes 9:10

The best of life always demands the best of you. Commitment! It is what separates doers from dreamers; successful people from failures and great men from the mediocre. In all probabilities, commitment is superior to intelligence. This is because, not all who are intelligent excel in life but all who are committed live above average.

Whereas intelligence is a plus, commitment is the foundation of a glorious destiny. Commitment first lays the foundation while intelligence builds as a pillar upon it. Intelligence alone will never be equal to success any day. Intelligence can be deceptive in the sense that it creates a sense of achievement in the mind of individuals who are in possession of it. It can actually create a sense of arrival and kill the desire for more.

On the contrary, commitment compels continuous crave for something new. It drives towards excellence at all times. No matter what you know, without commitment it will end in failure. That is why even the wisest man who has ever lived (Solomon) said in Ecclesiastes 9:10, “Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest.”

May God fortify your life with the virtue of commitment in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to be committed in all I do in Jesus Name!

Confession

I give my best at all times!
