How to Change Your Life for the Better

In short: The imagination of man is the limit of man. This is because you are a direct product of your thoughts; YOU EITHER RISE OR FALL BASED ON THE CONDITION OF YOUR MIND.

And the LORD said, “Indeed the people are one and they all have one language, and this is what they begin to do; now nothing that they propose to do will be withheld from them.
— Genesis 11:6

The imagination of man is the limit of man. This is because you are a direct product of your thoughts; YOU EITHER RISE OR FALL BASED ON THE CONDITION OF YOUR MIND.

Things come to us only as we imagine them hence they can never be restrained from us. Genesis 11:6 says , ’And the LORD said, “Indeed the people are one and they all have one language, and this is what they begin to do; now nothing that they propose to do will be withheld from them.’ God said He can do nothing to man’s imagination. He reckons, respects and honors your imagination.

So, imagine broadly. Nurture great thoughts; NOBODY WILL CHARGE YOU FOR THINKING BIG! Let your imagination soar beyond your self- imposed limitations. Envision unrestrictedly. Think as if nothing can stop it from coming to pass and indeed with God on your side He will do exceedingly and abundantly above all that you ask or think (Ephesians 3:20)

Your thoughts have power – Proverbs 23:7. They have power over your body. They have power over your abilities. They have power over your life. If you want to make small incremental improvements in your life, change your behaviour. But if you want to make dramatic, quantum leaps in your life, change your beliefs. Your money problems for instance are mind problems and the first mind problem is your core beliefs about yourself. The poor have a mind-set of smallness. God wants to give them more but their minds can’t handle the blessings.

“People do not attract that which they want, but that which they think they are.” Your picture of yourself isn’t just a picture. It’s a photograph of the future. Get a new picture of who you are by actually changing your thought life. John Johnson said, ‘Men and women are not limited by the place of their birth, nor by the colour of their skin, but by the size of their hope.’

Prayer

O Lord, fill my mind with great and positive thoughts in Jesus Name!

Confession

I have a mind-set of greatness!
