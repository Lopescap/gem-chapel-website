Think About the Effect of Your Actions

And herein do I exercise myself, to have always a conscience void of offence toward God, and toward men.
— Acts 24:16

One of the things you must always guard is your conscience. There are people who simply don’t have conscience - 1Ti 4:2 Speaking lies in hypocrisy; having their conscience seared with a hot iron; . Such people act carelessly and are heartless. They also don’t think about the effect of their speech and deeds on other people.

We need people of integrity in our world today; people whose yes is their yes and their no is their no. The world openly and hypocritically speaks against men of integrity but they admire the virtues and seek such men to work for them. Even thieves want to employ honest men and engage them as staff.

A man of integrity bother less about the praise and applause of men. He focuses on his unseen friend who lives in his inner most being – His Conscience. He seeks to maintain inner peace with himself. That is the most valuable thing to him than any other thing or position he may be given by any man on earth.

From today, decide to have a conscience void of offence toward God and men. Do not follow the crowd to do evil. Follow your inner compass that points in the same direction not minding the fashion or trend in the society! Maintain your integrity no matter what. What you do today is a seed; what you experience tomorrow is your harvest. Think about the effect of your actions.

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to have a clear conscience in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will not follow the crowd to do evil!
