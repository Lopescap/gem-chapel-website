My God, the Sanctifier!

In short: The greatest limitation to God’s manifestation is a lack of sanctification (Joshua 3:5). God wants pure and holy vessels in order to manifest His power in our world today.

Sanctify yourselves therefore, and be ye holy: for I am the Lord your God. And ye shall keep me statutes, and do them: I am the Lord which sanctify you.

— Leviticus 20:7-8

The greatest limitation to God’s manifestation is a lack of sanctification (Joshua 3:5). God wants pure and holy vessels in order to manifest His power in our world today. You automatically become a stranger to God’s power when you are familiar with sin. We live in a sin-soaked and an evil contaminated world that is why God must become our “Jehovah M’kaddesh” (the Lord our sanctifier).

When God becomes our sanctifier, He takes us out of sin into holiness, out of bondage into liberty, out of filth into righteousness. Some Christians think that after their salvation, what they do with their lives do not matter. Such people will be surprised when they hear the Master on the Judgment Day saying, “He does not know them”. God is a thrice holy God; for the angels carry out “holy, holy, holy.” Therefore, without holiness no man (including Pastors, Bishops, Elders, Deacons, General Overseers etc) cannot see God. You will be judged according to your works (Rev. 20:12-13). That is why you and I must choose to follow God’s example of holiness so that we become more like Him.

If you are truly sanctified by God, the old sins that you once indulged in will no longer appeal to you. You will hate fornication, pornography, lies, masturbation, anger, theft, adultery, homosexuality or lesbianism, pride etc. all the days of your life. You will not tolerate for even a second any unforgiveness, bitterness and hatred. From today, allow “Jehovah M’kaddesh” to do a complete work in your life. May you truly know God as your sanctifier in Jesus Name.

Prayer

O Lord, deliver me from any sin that easily entices me in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am a vessel of holiness!

Scriptures Referenced

  • Leviticus
