Practicing God’s Presence

In short: Godly living is living with God in our lives. In other words, recognizing and practicing God’s presence, His life in us. God also wants us to follow His instructions and celebrate His blessings.

Vr. 10 & 11 “For he who has entered His rest has himself also ceased from his works as God did from His; Let us therefore be diligent to enter that rest, lest anyone fall according to the same example of disobedience.

— Hebrews 4:10

Godly living is living with God in our lives. In other words, recognizing and practicing God’s presence, His life in us. God also wants us to follow His instructions and celebrate His blessings. He desires that we dwell in and esteem His presence, because doing so distinguishes us from everyone else. His presence in us and with us makes the difference.

In Exodus 3:3, God appeared to Moses in a burning bush, which was a dramatic way to get someone’s attention in this instance. But now it should not take anything dramatic for us to be aware of God’s presence in our lives because of the presence of the Holy Spirit. Jesus said He will be in us and abide with us as He goes to the Father - John 14: 26. He is the abiding presence. At the same time, we have to be alert to seek out God’s presence and working in everyday circumstances, because it often comes in ways we don’t expect.

To abide in His presence daily we have to do the following: Continually praise, pray, study the Word, obey God and live in holiness.

Prayer

Oh Lord, help me to practice and be in your presence. In Jesus Name. Amen.

Confession

I practice God’s Presence daily!
