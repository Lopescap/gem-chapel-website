The More You Know Him, the More You Become Like Him

In short: Beloved, the only way to know God is by having a revelation in your spirit. This is because, God is a spirit and we can really contact Him with our spirits (John 4:24).

That I may know Him, and the power of His resurrection, and the fellowship of His sufferings, being made conformable unto His death;
— Philippians 3:10

Beloved, the only way to know God is by having a revelation in your spirit. This is because, God is a spirit and we can really contact Him with our spirits (John 4:24). The level of your intimacy with Him will determine the extent of His presence and power in your life . You must not be at the same level of Christianity each and everyday of your life. You must be growing from one level of spirituality to another. You must have diverse spiritual experiences throughout your Christian life. Every passing day must move you closer to God.

When Jesus came to the world, various people had varying levels of relationship with Him. There was the crowd or the multitude who also knew Jesus for His ability to give them bread and other miracles. There was also the seventy disciples who also knew Jesus to some extent. Again, there was “the twelve” disciples who also had a higher level of intimacy with Jesus than the “seventy”. Even among the 12 disciples, Jesus had inner circles of three disciples (Peter, James and John) who were always with Him during his prayer times. Finally among the three, John the beloved was also the closest disciple to Jesus signifying yet another higher level of intimacy. John was also learning on the bosom (heart region) of Jesus and therefore knew what was on the heart of his master.

You must also make a decision to draw closer to God from today. Your daily prayer and desire must be to know Him more and more. The more you know Him, the more you become like Him.(Phil. 3:10). To be far from God is to be far from good.

Prayer

Father, I refuse to be at the same level in my Christian life. Take me higher and deeper with You in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am going deeper with God!
