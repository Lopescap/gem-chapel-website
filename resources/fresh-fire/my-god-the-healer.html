My God, the Healer?

In short: There are many strange sicknesses and diseases that attack mortal men in this world That’s why as a child of God, you must have a revelation of your heavenly Father as a Healer.

And said, If thou wilt diligently hearken to the voice of the Lord thy God, and wilt do that which is right in his sight, and wilt give ear to his commandments, and keep all his statutes, I will put none of these diseases upon thee, which I have brought upon the Egyptians: for I am the Lord that healeth thee.

— Exodus 15:26

There are many strange sicknesses and diseases that attack mortal men in this world That’s why as a child of God, you must have a revelation of your heavenly Father as a Healer. Our God is Jehovah “Rophe”meaning healer, repairer and restorer, the doctor. This revelation came through Moses to the children of Israel as they were freed from the 430 years bondage of Egypt. When they came to the waters of Marah, the Israelites found that the water was bitter and unsuitable for drinking. God showed Moses a tree and when he cast it into the water, it was made sweet, (Exodus 15:25-26).

It is the will of God for you to be healed (3 John 2). God has the power to heal all lands of sicknesses and infirmities. Although certain diseases can be cured medically, others go beyond physical cure. May be your sickness is emotional physical or spiritual. God is still able to heal or cure you. No matter how long you have been carrying that sickness, God can heal you today. Christ bore your pains and took your sicknesses on the cross and by His stripes you were healed. (Matt. 8:17, Isaiah 53:3-5). The enemy can no longer oppress you; from today, you will see God as “Jehovah Rophe” in your life!!

If any part of your body is damaged, God will repair it. However, if it is beyond repairs, Jehovah the Healer will replace it with a new part in Jesus Name! May you live a sickness-free life as you encounter the Doctor of Doctors.

Prayer

O Lord, heal me and I shall be healed in Jesus Name!

Confession

I walk in divine health all the days of my life!
