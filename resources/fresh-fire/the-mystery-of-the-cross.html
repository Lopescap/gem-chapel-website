The Mystery of the Cross

In short: Welcome to another devotional encounter with the loving God who gave His own Son for the salvation of mankind. In recent times, the message of the cross has been relegated to the background in many churches.

For the preaching of the cross is to them that perish foolishness, but unto us which are saved it is the POWER OF GOD.
— 1 Corinthians 1:18

Welcome to another devotional encounter with the loving God who gave His own Son for the salvation of mankind. In recent times, the message of the cross has been relegated to the background in many churches. This accounts for the lack of power in the power of Christ today.

For Christians, the most beloved memorial is the cross. The cross of Christ reaches into the heaven and spans the distance from the beginning of the time to eternity. It’s foundation is sunk into the bed rock of God’s uncommon love and mercy (Romans 5:8). It can withstand the fiercest demonic attacks and sway the most hard hearted sinners.

There is room for an infinite number of people to visit the cross and come away changed forever. Simply put, the cross is the greatest instrument of power for sanctified believers.

Sometimes the cross is used as a cultural icon or a fashion accessory and even warns those who oppose what it stands for.

Christ’s agonizing death, which opened up the way for us to be accepted by God.

We need to visit there daily and preach about it consistently. The best view is not found at the top of some earthy monument but at the foot of the cross.

Prayer

O Lord, bring me to an understanding of the mystery of the cross in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am grateful for the Cross!

Scriptures Referenced

  • 1 Corinthians
  • Romans
