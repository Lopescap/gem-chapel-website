How to Experience Kingdom Prosperity

In short: Fresh Fire for today is the best gift you can give to someone. Most Christians are struggling financially nowadays because they have refused to use simple Bible-keys.

Vrs. 27 “... go thou to the sea, and cast on hook, and take up the fish that first cometh up; and when thou has opened his mouth, thou shall find a piece of MONEY...
— Matthew 17:27

Fresh Fire for today is the best gift you can give to someone. Most Christians are struggling financially nowadays because they have refused to use simple Bible-keys. They have not discovered that there is prosperity in evangelism, but we must know that it is also a by-product.

From the above Scripture, ( Matthew 17:27), “the sea” stands for multitudes of people, and the “hook” stands for the boldness and zeal for evangelism. The “fish” stands for souls (fishers of men). And the “money” stands for prosperity. The fish they caught had money in its mouth. Some soul you will win carries your prosperity (money in the mouth).

Many Christians have however neglected this Great Commission. The Greatest Commission has now become the greatest omission. Each day, know the joy of sharing Christ wherever you go. There is no richer joy on earth, nor is there any higher joy in heaven, than that of winning souls to Christ. Luke 15:7-10.

Go out boldly and preach the gospel. Go from house to house in your neighbourhood and move from street to Street in your community. Plunder hell to populate Heaven; make Heaven glad and hell sad by embarking on an aggressive Evangelism campaign. There is joy and blessing in evangelism. May God bless and prosper you as you obey the Great Commission.

Prayer

I pray for more zeal to work for you O God in Jesus Name!

Confession

I prosper because I win souls!
