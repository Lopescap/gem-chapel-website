What Are You Living For?

In short: Life without a cause will end under a cloud. In other words, when you don’t live according to a specific purpose, you will die in obscurity. Living without a cause is like living under a curse. Cause is the cure to fainting.

Pilate therefore said unto him, Art thou a king then? Jesus answered, Thou sayest that I am a king. To this end was I born, and for this cause came I into the world, that I should bear witness unto the truth. Every one that is of the truth heareth my voice.

— John 18:37

Life without a cause will end under a cloud. In other words, when you don’t live according to a specific purpose, you will die in obscurity. Living without a cause is like living under a curse. Cause is the cure to fainting. With a cause you cannot faint. Strong people are those motivated by their cause. It is the greatest motivation to life. It makes one tireless and ever energized. A man with a cause is usually self-encouraging.

I want to ask you an important question today, ‘What are you living for?” Are you just moving through life marking time and whiling the time away?” When Jesus Christ was brought before Pilate, He emphatically answered this question by saying, ‘ To this end was I born, and for this cause came I into the world, that I should bear witness unto the truth. Every one that is of the truth heareth my voice.’ – John 18:37. When death was close and the agony was lurking around, Jesus would have given up, if He had no cause. He however persisted because He had a cause. John 12:27 says, ‘¶ Now is my soul troubled; and what shall I say? Father, save me from this hour: but for this cause came I unto this hour.’

David had a cause to live for. When he got to the battlefield and heard the threatening of Goliath, he knew that he had been born for this. He therefore said to his brother Eliab, ‘Is there not a cause?’ That was how David graduated from an unknown shepherd boy to become a renowned king.

Beloved, you must stand for something. If you don’t stand for something; you will fall for anything . Live for a cause!

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to fulfill my mission on earth in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am living a life of purpose!
