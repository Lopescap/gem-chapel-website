The Winning Frequency!

In short: In our world today, there are fears within and battles without. Invisible battles are on the ascendancy in order to stop God’s children from maximizing their destinies.

And they overcame him by the BLOOD OF THE LAMB, and by the word of their testimony..........

— Revelation 12:11

In our world today, there are fears within and battles without. Invisible battles are on the ascendancy in order to stop God’s children from maximizing their destinies. The devil has a short time here on earth, (Revelation 12:12) and therefore he is out to demonstrate the greatest wickedness ever. So expect the most wicked manifestation from him in these last days, and at the same time, the brightest triumph for you, if you know the weapons against his strategies.

Beloved, the Blood of Jesus is the secret of all-round victory in every conflict of life. The Cross is what disarmed and devil and his host. Colossians 2:15 says, “And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.” Every time you turn to the blood, you become invincible; too defended to be defeated and too surrounded to surrender. The Bible says in Rev. 12 that there was a fierce battle in Heaven. Nothing worked until the Blood of the Lamb started working and gave Heaven victory against Satan. If they overcame by the Blood, then we shall and will always overcome by the Blood. If the Blood was relevant for victory in Heaven, then it must be super relevant on the earth.

In order for you to silence the devil in your affairs, lift up the banner of the Blood. The Blood of Jesus can break the chains of fear, the yoke of poverty, the burden of sickness and any curse in your life. THE WINNING FREQUENCY OF LIFE IS THE BLOOD OF JESUS. All-round victory is yours through the Blood!

Prayer

O Lord, I claim victory over the challenges of my life on the basis of the Blood of Jesus!

Confession

I am WINNING on every side!
