The Cure for Depression

In short: Depression is the gateway to oppression. One of the most dangerous parties to attend is a ‘pity party’. Pity parties cause you to live in a realm of anxiety and worry.

says, ‘6 Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God; 7 and the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

— Philippians 4:6-7

Depression is the gateway to oppression. One of the most dangerous parties to attend is a ‘pity party’. Pity parties cause you to live in a realm of anxiety and worry. However, what you must know is that easiest route to worry, depression and apprehension is to attempt to care for yourself and to fight your own battles.

Jesus already warned us to take no thought about what to eat or drink. Matt.6: 25 says “Therefore I say to you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink; nor about your body, what you will put on. Is not life more than food and the body more than clothing?’ The cares of this life choke destiny and destroy potentials. Live your life absolutely depending on the One who knew you before He made you (Jeremiah 1:5). Your parents knew you after you were born but God knew you even before you were born.

Roll your care upon Him (I Peter 5:7) because you can simply not handle them. Life is too heavy to carry, release it to the Owner and the Manager of it. He has provided for your total well being in it.He cannot mismanage your life for you. Leave it absolutely to Him and it will amaze you how He will turn it back to you.Be rest assured that no matter the circumstance and situation God will certainly take care of you. Do not exchange your peace for anxiety. Phil.4:6-7 says, ‘6 Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God; 7 and the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus .

Take your rest in God and He will take care of the rest for you. Whenever you feel like worrying; don’t because you have a God who has your whole destiny in His hands. Stop the pity party because you are not a person to be pitied but rather a person to be envied!

Prayer

O Lord, I put my life into Your mighty Hands in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am far from depression!
