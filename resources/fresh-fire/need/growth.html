When you want to grow

Readings for anyone who wants to go further — maturity, increase, and the disciplines that move you forward.

  • How to Increase Your Performance
    Performance in any duty or assignment is enhanced when you consider the task as a privilege. When you take any assignment for granted, you start failing at it. Every man is at his peak performance whenever it is seen as a privilege to serve.
  • You Need Good and Proper Feeding
    In life, what you eat will determine whether you grow well or not. That is why when you take in a lot of protein-based foods and you begin to put on some weight people will begin to ask you the common rhetorical question, ‘What have you been eating’?
  • How to Change Your Life for the Better
    The imagination of man is the limit of man. This is because you are a direct product of your thoughts; YOU EITHER RISE OR FALL BASED ON THE CONDITION OF YOUR MIND.
  • How to Move from Strength to Strength
    You must develop an unquenchable love for the House of God if your desire is to grow in the spirit. In life, it is your company that determines your accomplishments and your companions direct your opinions.
  • How Things Grow Worse
    Laziness is a common trait among many people especially the youth. You must however be a hardworker if you want to be a highflier in life. It is better to wear out working than to rust off idling.
  • Be God-Focused! — Part 1
    To grow in success in life, you must be God focused and not work or career focused. You must do everything to develop an unbreakable relationship with God if you really desire to have a lasting success.
  • The Necessity of Spiritual Growth
    Beloved, spiritual stagnation is the greatest killer of physical advancement. God does not manifest children; He only show forth His matured sons (Rom. 8:19). Growth in Christianity is not an option but a necessity.
  • How to Live Well
    Good and proper feeding is essential for your survival. In as much as our bodies need to feed well in order to grow, our spirits also need the Word of God in order to live and function well.
  • How to Move to the Next Level
    Everyone avoided going to take care of the flock of Jesse, except David. On that duty post, the anointing located him. King Saul, his soldiers and every Israelite avoided Goliath.
