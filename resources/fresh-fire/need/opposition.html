When you are under attack

Readings for seasons of resistance: hostility, spiritual conflict, and the temptation to withdraw when the battle line is drawn.

  • Do You Know God as the Healer?
    There are many strange sicknesses and diseases that attack mortal men in this world That’s why as a child of God, you must have a revelation of your heavenly Father as a Healer.
  • Do You Know God as the Conqueror?
    Battles are satan’s reaction to the advancement and progress of men. In fact, the brighter your destiny, the harsher your attackers.
  • The Power to Start
    Most people think that their greatest challenge is the oppositions and confrontations they are facing in their destiny. Others also think it their background that is their impediment.
  • The Faith That Wins — Part 2
    Faith is a winning force; it is the highest exponent in the equation of victory. The following are the attributes of the winning faith;
  • Don’t Withdraw When the Battle-Line Is Drawn
    Most people fail in life not because they lack knowledge or talent but because they quit. When problems seem insurmountable, quitting may look like the easiest way out. It is true for every marriage, ministry, job and relationship.
  • The Sleeping Giant
    Some people are concerned about the current darkness the world finds itself in-and I am concerned too.
  • My God, the Healer?
    There are many strange sicknesses and diseases that attack mortal men in this world That’s why as a child of God, you must have a revelation of your heavenly Father as a Healer.
  • My God, the Conqueror?
    That is why you must know God as Jehovah Nissi which means the “Lord our Banner” or THE LORD OUR CONQUEROR signifying our victory. We serve a victorious God.
  • The Road to Total Protection!
    The arrows of the enemy and his cohorts are flying all around. Fear and panic are the order of the day now. With all the technological advancement in our world today, human lives still remain the most vulnerable.
  • The Winning Frequency!
    In our world today, there are fears within and battles without. Invisible battles are on the ascendancy in order to stop God’s children from maximizing their destinies.
  • The Healing Grace
    Sickness and weakness are all satanic afflictions. They are arrows sent by the enemy to slow you down in the journey of life. You can be made strong out of your weakness.
  • Overcoming Evil Decrees
    As a Christian, you must never be ignorant of evil or demonic decrees. One of the major weapons the enemy uses to keep people in bondage is evil decrees.
