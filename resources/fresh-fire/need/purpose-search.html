When you are questioning your purpose

Readings for the question of why you are here: calling, uniqueness, and the fear of a life spent on the wrong things.

  • You Must Always Be Thankful
    Small things matter to God. We must not wait to see big things before we thank God. There is nothing too small to thank God for. There should be no difference between our thanksgiving for seemingly little things and big things.
  • The Ultimate Purpose for Man’s Existence
    What is God like? Who is God? If you were asked to describe God today, how would you respond?
  • Why Most People Are Not Happy
    Nobody can be truly happy when he is outside the will of God . One of the most horrible things that could happen to a person is when the person is outside where God wants him to be and he does not realize it until he gets to the grave.
  • Why Most Destinies Are Wasted
    The devil is already wasting people. In fact, the greatest demon harassing many people today is the demon of wastage. Human beings are wasted. Many things are wasted. A person could decide to waste his own life.
  • The Ultimate Purpose of Study
    The primary purpose of study is to get information but the ultimate is to press for transformation. The whole essence of knowledge is transformation. If it is not applicable, then it is not profitable.
  • What Are You Living For?
    Life without a cause will end under a cloud. In other words, when you don’t live according to a specific purpose, you will die in obscurity. Living without a cause is like living under a curse. Cause is the cure to fainting.
  • How to Position Yourself for Big Things
    Small things matter to God, we must not wait to see big things before we thank God. There is nothing too small to thank God for. There should be no difference between our thanksgiving for seemingly little things and big things.
  • The Purpose of the Anointing
    Jesus the “Anointed one” (the Messiah) in Isaiah 61:1, spoke of His Messianic task in Luke 4:18-19.
