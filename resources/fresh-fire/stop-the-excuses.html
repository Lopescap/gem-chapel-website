Stop the Excuses!

In short: Losers always have a book full of excuses to tell you why they could not.

But they all with one accord began to make excuses. The first said to him, ‘I have bought a piece of ground, and I must go and see it. I ask you to have me excused.

— Luke 14:18

Losers always have a book full of excuses to tell you why they could not. We normally hear excuses like, ‘I am unlucky, I’m too young, I’m too old, the economy is bad, I’m handicapped, I’m not good looking, I’m not smart, I don’t have enough money and the list goes on....

In India, they have a particular strategy that they use in catching monkeys. Monkey-hunters use a box with an opening at the top, big enough for the monkey to slide its hand into. Inside the box are nuts. The monkey grabs the nuts and now its hand becomes a fist. The monkey tries to get its hand out but the opening is big enough for the hand to slide into but too small for the fist to come out of. Now the monkey has a choice, either to let go off the nuts and be free forever or hang on to the nuts and get caught. Guess what it p picks every time? You guessed it. He hangs on to the nuts and gets caught.

Most people are no different from monkeys. They hang on to the nuts that keep them from going forward in life. Such people keep on rationalizing by saying, ‘I can’t do this because...’ and whatever comes after ‘because’ are the nuts that they are hanging onto that are holding us back.

Those who were invited for the feast in Luke 14 began to MAKE EXCUSES. Excuses are made; they are not real. Never hold on to your excuses if you really want to excel in life. THE EASIEST WAY TO TRAP YOUR DESTINY IS TO HOLD ON TO YOUR EXCUSES! Your background should never be the grounds for your failure. You can make it no matter what!

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to overcome the things that are limiting my destiny in Jesus Name!

Confession

I let go of any thing holding me back!
