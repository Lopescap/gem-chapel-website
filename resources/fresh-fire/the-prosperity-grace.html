The Prosperity Grace

In short: Very shortly, you will become an object of argument. Your hand will no longer be empty. Your pocket will no longer be empty. Your hand will not touch the bottom of your pocket or bag again.

And God is able to make all grace abound toward you; that ye, always having all sufficiency in all things, may abound to every good work:

— 2 Corinthians 9:8

Very shortly, you will become an object of argument. Your hand will no longer be empty. Your pocket will no longer be empty. Your hand will not touch the bottom of your pocket or bag again. The era of begging and borrowing is over in your life in Jesus Name! - Isaiah 62:10, “10 ¶ Go through, go through the gates; prepare ye the way of the people; cast up, cast up the highway; gather out the stones; lift up a standard for the people.”

There is a wealth whose source is not traceable to the earth (Heaven made wealth). Financial explosion is a reality, the economic instability notwithstanding.

Abraham walked in financial explosion, as well as Isaac and all our covenant fathers.

Genesis 13:2, “ And Abram was very rich in cattle, in silver, and in gold.

Genesis 24:1, “ And Abraham was old, and well stricken in age: and the LORD had blessed Abraham in all things.”

However, you must understand that there is a Grace that can take you from the realm of financial struggles into a realm of financial rest; in spite of the prevailing harsh economic climate; you can still prosper. At this level, people cannot see your sweat but they simply cannot deny your effect. This is because there is SOMETHING WORKING FOR YOU THAT IS NOT WORKING FOR THEM (GRACE) - 2Co 9:8, ‘And God is able to make all grace abound toward you; that ye, always having all sufficiency in all things, may abound to every good work:’

Prayer

O Lord, connect me to the Prosperity Grace in Jesus Name!

Confession

Everything I do shall prosper!

  • Isaiah
  • Genesis
