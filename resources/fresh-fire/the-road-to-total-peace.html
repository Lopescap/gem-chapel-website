The Road to Total Peace!

In short: We live in a world full of turmoil and chaos. Anxiety and worry are common occurrences in the lives of most people. In spite of all these, God can give us total peace. The currency of the Blood of Jesus purchased for us among other things our peace.

Verse 20, “And having made peace through the BLOOD OF HIS CROSS, by Him to reconcile all things unto Himself; by Him, I say, whether they be things in earth or things in heaven”.

— Colossians 1:20-22

We live in a world full of turmoil and chaos. Anxiety and worry are common occurrences in the lives of most people. In spite of all these, God can give us total peace. The currency of the Blood of Jesus purchased for us among other things our peace.

Peace simply means freedom from war; a state of mental calm and serenity, with no anxiety. The covenant of peace between man and God has its roots in the Blood of Jesus. This means that without the Cross, there could be no access by a sinful man to a holy God. Through the fall of Adam, man was constantly at war with God. There was a gap between man and God. Sin created a partition between divinity and humanity but the cross became that bridge that reconnected man to God.

Ephesians 2:14 says of Jesus Christ, “For He is our peace, who hath made both one, and hath broken down the MIDDLE WALL OF PARTITION between us”, that is why you can now have true fellowship with your Father in heaven. Hebrews 10:19 also says, “Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holiest by the BLOOD of Jesus............”

Friend, do you really know Jesus? Are you a partaker of the peace in His Blood? Are you enjoying that peace that the Cross has given you? It is yours. Begin to enjoy absolute peace from today on the basis of the Blood of Jesus. The Blood of Christ is your only true source of peace in this chaotic world. Isaiah 57:21 says, ‘There is no peace, saith my God, to the wicked.’

Prayer

Father, on the basis of the pure Blood of Jesus, I claim total peace in every department of my life in Jesus Name!

Confession

Total peace is my portion!
