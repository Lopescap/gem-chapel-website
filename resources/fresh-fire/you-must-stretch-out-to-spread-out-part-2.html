You Must Stretch Out to Spread Out — Part 2

In short: Don’t give up! You are advancing and exceling no matter what in Jesus Name! Yesterday, we learnt about the importance of goals in a man’s life. Today, we want to learn about the process of setting and re-defining your goals.

Then the LORD answered me and said: “Write the vision And make it plain on tablets, That he may run who reads it.
— Habakkuk 2:2

Don’t give up! You are advancing and exceling no matter what in Jesus Name! Yesterday, we learnt about the importance of goals in a man’s life. Today, we want to learn about the process of setting and re-defining your goals.

The goal setting process includes;

1. Decide exactly what you want in a specific area of your life.

(Spiritual Goals, Academic Goals, Career goals, Financial Goals, Health Goals, Marriage Goals).

2. Write it down. Habakkuk 2:1-2

A goal that is not in writing is not in writing is merely a wish or fantasy

3. Set a deadline for your goal. Habakkuk 2:3-4, Eccl. 3:1-2

A goal without a deadline has no urgency. We have Immediate goals (within 1 year), short-term goals ( 1-3 years), long-term goals (4-10 years).

4. Identify the obstacles that you would have to overcome in order to

achieve your goals .

Internal or personal obstacles: Laziness, too much leisure (TV etc.), dislike for certain subjects etc.

External obstacles: Wrong associations – Psalm 119:163

5. Make a list of everything you have to do in order to achieve your goals

and organize the list into a comprehensive plan. Prov. 16:1-3; Prov. 16:9

6. Take action on your plan immediately. An average plan vigorously

executed is far better than a brilliant plan on which nothing is done. Eph. 5:16, Col. 4:5

7. Decide to do something every day that will move you towards the

achievement of your goals.

You must be self-disciplined. You must submit yourself under rules if you want to be a ruler. There are two kinds of pain: the Pain of Discipline & the Pain of Regrets.

8. Depend on God’s Grace. GRACE= (G) God in the Race. When you have

God in the race of your life; you will eventually emerge as a winner. I Cor. 15:10

Prayer

O Lord, inject into me the spirit of self-discipline in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will not miss my crown of glory!
