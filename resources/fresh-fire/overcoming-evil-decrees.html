Overcoming Evil Decrees

In short: As a Christian, you must never be ignorant of evil or demonic decrees. One of the major weapons the enemy uses to keep people in bondage is evil decrees.

No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the LORD, and their righteousness is of me, saith the LORD.

— Isaiah 54:17

As a Christian, you must never be ignorant of evil or demonic decrees. One of the major weapons the enemy uses to keep people in bondage is evil decrees. These evil decrees have caused many people to suffer failure, poverty, sickness, disease, untimely death etc. That is why David prayed a particular prayer unto God in Psalm 31:20, “Thou shalt hide them in the secret of thy presence from the pride of man: thou shalt keep them secretly in a pavilion from the strife of tongues.” The devil and his cohorts are found of making evil pronouncements in the demonic world to destroy the precious destiny of God’s people.

Evil decrees include curses – the greatest headache associated with a curse is that it goes to the roots of a person’s life. That is why when Jesus cursed the fig tree, it died from the roots. There are also incantations (an invitation of wicked demonic spirits to punish a person), Enchantments (by occultists and members of secret societies) and spells as well as demonic prophecies placed on people by herbalists, jujumen, mallams and false prophets.

In order to overcome these evil decrees, you have to do three things;

1. Reject it . I Samuel 2:1

Such evil decrees do not belong to you.

2. Reverse it. Isaiah 54:17

What you don’t want to remain, don’t watch, speak against it authoritatively.

3. Return it.

Revelations 13:10 says, “He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints.”

Prayer

I fire back every demonic arrow back to the senders in Jesus Name!

Confession

No satanic arrow of the enemy will locate my destiny!
