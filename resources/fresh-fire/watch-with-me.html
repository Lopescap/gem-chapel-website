Watch with Me!

In short: Jesus asked His disciples to keep watch with Him, for His soul was crushed with grief. He was in deep agony and pain, yet His faithful few (Peter, James and John who were part of His inner circle) could not stay awake.

Then he returned to his disciples and found them sleeping. ‘Could you men not keep watch with me for one hour?’ he asked Peter.

— Matthew 26:40

Jesus asked His disciples to keep watch with Him, for His soul was crushed with grief. He was in deep agony and pain, yet His faithful few (Peter, James and John who were part of His inner circle) could not stay awake. They were tired and fell asleep, leaving Christ to watch and pray alone.

Today, many believers are slumbering and sleeping when they should be watching and praying. There is a lack of spiritual heat and fire. Cold embers now reside where fiery souls once burned. Yet Christ is still asking us to “stay here and keep watch” with Him, because we do not know the day or the hour he will return (Matthew 26:38).

Everything going on around us points to one fact, ‘WE ARE IN TURBULENT TIMES’. Turbulent times calls for turbulent and violent prayers from the saints. That is why we cannot turn deaf ears to the clarion call for us to watch and pray. Many Christians are like Jonah who slept soundly and refused to watch when the ship he was traveling in was going through a storm. The other mariners were hopelessly calling on strange gods who couldn’t save them but Jonah was sleeping although he knew the true God who could calm the storm. Jonah 1:4-6 says, ‘4 ¶ But the LORD sent out a great wind on the sea, and there was a mighty tempest on the sea, so that the ship was about to be broken up. 5 Then the mariners were afraid; and every man cried out to his god, and threw the cargo that was in the ship into the sea, to lighten the load. But Jonah had gone down into the lowest parts of the ship, had lain down, and was fast asleep. 6 So the captain came to him, and said to him, “What do you mean, sleeper? Arise, call on your God; perhaps your God will consider us, so that we may not perish.”

Are you willing to watch? Are you willing to pray? Are your prayers in line with the express will of God, or are they in accordance to your own will and desires? Are you willing to set aside time to press on in prayer? Or are other things more important? Christ expects that His Church will be one that watches and prays. It is time to re-dig the well of prayer and re-ignite the fire of intercession. Will you watch and pray?

Prayer

O Lord, revive my prayer life from today in Jesus Name!

Confession

I have a strong prayer altar!
