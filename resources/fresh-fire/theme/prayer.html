Prayer

Petition, intercession, fasting, and getting prayers answered.

  • You Are at Your Best When You Are in the Spirit!
    The believer’s habitat is the Holy Spirit just as water is to fish, hole is to foxes and nest is to birds. Therefore, as a child of God, YOU ARE AT YOUR BEST WHEN YOU ARE IN THE SPIRIT.
  • You Must Always Be in Touch with Heaven
    Those who are in touch with Heaven are empowered to change their world. As a child of God, don’t just pray; learn to listen for His voice. Have you ever wondered why Jesus never made a single misstep?
  • Prayer Works Wonders — Part 1
    There are many prayerless Christians around today. They know the power and importance of prayer but they never pray. Prayerlessness is the greatest sickness of the contemporary Church. We schedule everything except our prayer time.
  • Prayer Works Wonders — Part 2
    No matter the challenge you face this year, take it to God in prayer. Our heavenly Father’s ears are always opened to hear the cry of His children on earth. However, nothing sustains intercession like revelation. Hos.14:2.
  • Prayer Works Wonders — Part 3
    If there is a man to pray, then there is surely a God to answer! He is the prayer answering God and that’s why everyone must run to Him – Psalm 65:2.
  • Developing Spiritual Toughness
    Body fasting is the platform for spiritual fattening. Physical leanness is what births spiritual toughness. If you must get fatter spiritually then you must engage more in fasting. The body must “perish” for the inner man to be renewed. II Cor.
  • How to Get Your Prayers Answered!
    Soul wining is the heart beat of God. Do you want 100% answered prayer? Do you know why some of the things you have asked God have not come or have delayed? That’s the reason for the morning’s devotion.
  • The Faith That Wins — Part 3
    Prayer is to men of faith as oxygen is to their bodies. They know that they cannot survive in this journey of life without the strength and help of God and they secure these through the medium of prayer.
  • God-Addiction
    Today, as we end our journey of developing a closer walk with God, we want to learn how to develop a closer walk with God.
  • You Need Mercy!
    The altar of prayer is the altar of Grace but the gate to the altar is MERCY. You must pass through mercy to access Grace.
  • The Sleeping Giant
    Some people are concerned about the current darkness the world finds itself in-and I am concerned too.
  • Watch with Me!
    Jesus asked His disciples to keep watch with Him, for His soul was crushed with grief. He was in deep agony and pain, yet His faithful few (Peter, James and John who were part of His inner circle) could not stay awake.
  • Hear from God!
    Many people make a mistake of running ahead of God. They take hasty steps and at the end of the day life frustrated lives. It is those who hear from Heaven who eventually impact their world.
  • You Shall Finish What You Have Started!
    In the journey of life, many people start a lot of great things but few are able to finish what they started. It is not enough to start well, you must also receive Grace to finish strong.
  • Supernatural Proofs!
    On the Cross Jesus Christ made a public show of the devil in a triumphant procession - Col 2:15 And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.
  • A Day Without Prayer
    Prayer is not what we choose to do but what we must do; it is not what we like to do but what we are mandated to do.
  • How to Access Grace
    2Co 9:14, ‘And by their prayer for you, which long after you for the exceeding grace of God in you.’
  • Be Strong in the Lord!
    Those who dominate in the spiritual realm will automatically dominate in the physical realm. This is because everything is a spiritual situation before it becomes a physical condition. In this New Year, you must be fervent in spirit.
  • The Most Important Prayer
    Friend, the most dangerous place to be is to be outside the will of God for your life. It is only God who knows the ending from the beginning. Our parents got to know us after we were born, but God knew us even before we were born.
  • How to Make a Big Difference
    What difference are we making? Big or small, it does not matter. If everyone made a small difference, we would end up with a big difference, wouldn’t we?
  • The Footprints of God
    Several years ago, a great French scientist was crossing the Arabian desert under the influence of an Arab (Christian) tourist guide. Along the way, the sat down to take a rest and the guide started praying.
  • The Mystery of Sacrifice
    One of the greatest mysteries you must understand is the mystery of altars. An altar is a place where spirits meet; IT IS A PLACE OF SACRIFICE.
