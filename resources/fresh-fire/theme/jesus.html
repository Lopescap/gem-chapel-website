Jesus and the Cross

The person and work of Christ; salvation and mercy.

  • The Mystery of the Cross
    Welcome to another devotional encounter with the loving God who gave His own Son for the salvation of mankind. In recent times, the message of the cross has been relegated to the background in many churches.
  • Be God-Focused! — Part 3
    Beloved, you can seek after things and miss God at the end of the day but when you seek after God, the things will follow. You must only be satisfied when you wake up in the likeness of God; all of God must be on you.
  • You Need Mercy!
    The altar of prayer is the altar of Grace but the gate to the altar is MERCY. You must pass through mercy to access Grace.
  • The Road to Total Peace!
    We live in a world full of turmoil and chaos. Anxiety and worry are common occurrences in the lives of most people. In spite of all these, God can give us total peace. The currency of the Blood of Jesus purchased for us among other things our peace.
  • The Road to Long Life!
    All over the world most people wish others long life and prosperity and not short life and poverty. As a child of God, you must believe God for long life. The life of everything is extended when the Blood of Jesus is invoked.
  • The Road to Total Protection!
    The arrows of the enemy and his cohorts are flying all around. Fear and panic are the order of the day now. With all the technological advancement in our world today, human lives still remain the most vulnerable.
  • The Road to Total Freedom!
    Most people in our world today are bound in one area of their lives or the other. However, Every bondage terminates at the Blood of Jesus!
  • Pay the Price for the Prize!
    You are born-again to be a high-flier – Prov. 4:18. Life is like a cafeteria. You take your tray, select your food and pay at the other end. You can get anything you want as long as you are willing to pay for the price.
  • The Saving Grace
    Grace simply means undeserved or unmerited favour. It simply means when God does for you what you don’t deserve.
  • The Giving Grace
    The Grace of God is the maker of men; no glorious destiny can be constructed without the ingredient of Grace.
  • The Long Road
    Ayoung girl gave her teacher a very special gift. It was a beautiful seashell. “Where did you get this?” she asked. The child told her that such shells are found only on a certain faraway beach.
  • How to Put a Smile on the Face of God
    May the love of God that drove Christ to the cross now drive us to the lost. If soul-winning is not your priority then you are out of touch with Heaven.
