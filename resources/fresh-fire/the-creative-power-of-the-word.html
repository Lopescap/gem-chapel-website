The Creative Power of the Word

In short: God’s Word has creative power and calls into existence the very thing of which it speaks. If you are sick, the Word can create your healing; if you are poor, the Word can bring into existence your victory.

By the Word of the Lord were the heavens made; and it was done; He commanded, and it stood fast”.

— Psalm 33:6-9

God’s Word has creative power and calls into existence the very thing of which it speaks. If you are sick, the Word can create your healing; if you are poor, the Word can bring into existence your victory.

The whole universe was created by the word of the Lord. He spoke the planets, the sun, the moon, the stars, plants, etc into existence. I love the way Psalm 33:9 put it, “FOR HE SPAKE, AND IT WAS DONE; HE COMMANDED AND IT STOOD FAST”.

Friend, is there anything missing in your life? If yes then I want to assure you that there is a creative power inherent in God’s Word. As you release God’s Word into your situation, there shall be a restoration. I don’t know what you are lacking but my God shall supply all your needs according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus. (Phil. 4:19)

Prayer

O Lord, do something new in my life today in Jesus Name.

Confession

God is doing new things in my life!
