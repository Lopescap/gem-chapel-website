Do You Know God as the Creator?

In short: The name “Elohim” describes God as the creator. In other words, our God is the creator of the world and everything that exist in it.

In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.
— Genesis 1:1

The name “Elohim” describes God as the creator. In other words, our God is the creator of the world and everything that exist in it. Many scientists have propounded many theories to explain how the world came into being and even the existence of human beings. In spite of theories like the “Big Bang” and the “Evolution”, it is an undisputable fact that God created the world. Atheists may claim that there is no God but the fact still holds that there is a God in Heaven that rules over the affairs of men(Psalm 8:3, Psalm 102:25, Col. 1;16-17). Daniel 4:17 says, “This matter is by the decree of the watchers, and the demand by the word of the holy ones: to the intent that the living may know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, and giveth it to whomsoever he will, and setteth up over it the basest of men.”

Creativity is an aspect of God’s character and nature. His creative nature is portrayed in two ways. Firstly, through the spoken word (Psalm 33:6, Hebrews 11:3). Secondly, through wisdom (Jer. 10:15, 51:15). When man was created in the image of God, he was blessed with God’s creative nature. Man’s creative abilities and power of invention are from God.

Ideas rule the world and they are the most expensive commodities on the earth. One creative idea from God can turn things around for you. If you are a true Christian, your words carry creative powers. (Eccl. 8:4). You can see what you say. Again, you have the mind of Christ (creative wisdom of Gods) and you can use it at any time. From today, begin to walk in your creative abilities in Jesus Name!!

Prayer

O Lord, begin to do new things in my life in Jesus Name. Amen.

Confession

I operate with supernatural creativity!
