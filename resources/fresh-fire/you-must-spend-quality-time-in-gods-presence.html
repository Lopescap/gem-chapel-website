You Must Spend Quality Time in God’s Presence

In short: Beloved, you must do everything possible to walk in the power of God. This is because, the only language the devil and his cohorts understand is power. However, no one can gain access to a thing from a man unless he first gains access to his person.

1 O God, You are my God; Early will I seek You; My soul thirsts for You; My flesh longs for You In a dry and thirsty land Where there is no water. 8 My soul follows close behind You; Your right hand upholds me.

— Psalm 63:1, 8

Beloved, you must do everything possible to walk in the power of God. This is because, the only language the devil and his cohorts understand is power. However, no one can gain access to a thing from a man unless he first gains access to his person.

Therefore, to access the power of God you must know the God of the power. It is the custodian of a treasure that determines the measure you can access. Know him little and you will have access to little of His power; know him much and you will have access to much of His power. Dan. 11:32.

In order for you to know, you must spend much time with Him. Qualitative time in the Presence of God produces quantitative power. It is only those who seek His face that can move his hand. II Chr. 7:11, 12. Do not just seek His power, seek his face; His power flows where His face shows. Ps. 63:1, 8, 105:4.

It is the longing of your soul for Him that commands the flow of His power. Until you have power with God you cannot draw power from God. Gen. 32:28; 33:4.

Knowledge of God is the fountain of power. Fullness of God brings about fullness of power (grace) that results in fullness of blessings. John 1:16, Is.44:3, II Cor.9:8.

Men will unconditionally become eager to meet and help you when you have met with God. Gen. 33:4. When you long to see the face of God, men will also long to see your face. From today, pursue God like a predator pursues its prey and by so doing you will be connecting to the greatest and the most authentic source of power in the entire universe.

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to know you more in Jesus Name!

Confession

God is my obsession!
