It’s All About Jesus!

In short: On the Mount of Transfiguration, Moses and Elijah appeared to Jesus. However at the end of the day only JESUS remained standing (Luke 9). Your goal in life must be to keep Jesus at the centre of all you do.

Therefore I take pleasure in infirmities, in reproaches, in needs, in persecutions, in distresses, for Christ’s sake. For when I am weak, then I am strong.
— 2 Corinthians 12:10

On the Mount of Transfiguration, Moses and Elijah appeared to Jesus. However at the end of the day only JESUS remained standing (Luke 9). Your goal in life must be to keep Jesus at the centre of all you do.

Do not seek fame if you don’t want to end in flames. You are not called to be famous but to be faithful. You are not called to known by the world but to show Jesus to the world to see. You are not called to project yourself but to project Christ.

Seek to be full of God at all times and you will continue to flow for God. Fame seeking is what leads people to comparison and unhealthy competition. Every man is called to pursue a purpose and to follow a path. II Cor.12:10; 1 Cor.7:14, 21. Therefore run your own race and fight your own battles.

Don’t speak for yourself. Let your work speak for you. Your work has voice that cannot be silenced, feet that cannot be stopped, face that cannot be shielded, and hand that cannot be hindered. Your work is your address; people will locate you through your works. Your work has the key to all closed doors, to high favour, to attracting all that belongs to you. Rev.3:8 says, ‘“I know your works. See, I have set before you an open door, and no one can shut it; for you have a little strength, have kept My word, and have not denied My name.’

Rather than showing your face and seeking for fame, project your work and be diligent in all you do and very soon you will stand before kings! Then the world will see Jesus in all you do!

Prayer

O Lord, kill any seed of pride in me in Jesus Name!

Confession

I only project Jesus Christ and His Kingdom!
