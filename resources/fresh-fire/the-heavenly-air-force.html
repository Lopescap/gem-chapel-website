The Heavenly Air-Force

In short: You are born as a high flier, you cannot remain as a low crawler. You are part of a “heavenly air force”. You can’t enroll in an “earthly ground troop”. Every Christian is a candidate for progress.

Vrs.13 “And the Lord shall make thee the head, and not the tail, and thou shall be above only, and thou shall not be beneath, if that thou hearken unto the commandments of the Lord thy God, which I command thee this day, to observe and to do them”.
— Deuteronomy 28:10-14

You are born as a high flier, you cannot remain as a low crawler. You are part of a “heavenly air force”. You can’t enroll in an “earthly ground troop”. Every Christian is a candidate for progress.

Whatever you are doing now should not make you get satisfied with yourself.

Candidates for the top do not stop climbing. They keep going ahead in spite of the challenges of life. Those who want to live at the top use stopping stones as stepping stones, and see obstacles as miracles. When they see an end, they turn it into a bend, They don’t compare themselves with others and think the have arrived. They keep moving. What are your limitations? In spite of such limitations, you can still stand out in life this year. It is very crowded at the bottom, it is very crowded at the valley; go to the mountain top of life. The bible says that if you are obedient to God, then you will be the head and not the tail, and you shall be ABOVE ONLY AND NOT BENEATH...(Deut. 28:13) You can’t be stranded in life this year. To rule and to reign in life, you must get out of the common to become outstanding. It takes the daring to become the darling of all and sundry. From today, you must be determined to STEP OUT in order for you to STAND-OUT. You are part of the Heavenly Air force; you shall surely go up and stay up in Jesus Name!

Be a blessing to someone today by giving them a copy of fresh fire.

Prayer

Oh Lord, give me the strength to live life at the top in JESUS NAME! Amen.

Confession

I will go up and stay up!
