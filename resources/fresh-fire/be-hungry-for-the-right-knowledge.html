Be Hungry for the Right Knowledge

In short: Are you opened to new ideas? Are you ready to receive other people’s thoughts and ideas? Do you listen more than you talk? Are you willing to change your opinion based on new information? Do you readily admit when you are wrong?

A wise man will hear and increase learning, And a man of understanding will attain wise counsel

— Proverbs 1:5

Are you opened to new ideas? Are you ready to receive other people’s thoughts and ideas? Do you listen more than you talk? Are you willing to change your opinion based on new information? Do you readily admit when you are wrong? Do you read impactful books and listen to anointed tapes? Do you think and observe before acting on a situation? Do you ask questions concerning things you are ignorant of? Are you opened to doing things in a way you haven’t done them before? Do you act defensively when criticized, or do you listen openly for the truth? Are you willing to ask for directions?

The Bible says, ‘The way of a fool seems right to him, but a wise man listens to advice (Prov. 12:15).

In this second half of the year, you must be opened to new things. The definition for mediocrity is doing things in the same old way. This is because anything you are doing now can be accomplished in a better and faster way. Most of the things we know, we learned from someone else, including the stuff we claim credit for! It may be new to us but it is not new.

Thomas Edison, the renowned scientist was a life-long learner. He had over 1000 patents to his credit. He stayed open, hungry for knowledge and teachable and to succeed, you must too. Knowledge is scattered here and there. You must therefore be an avid and a life-long learner to make the most of your destiny. This year invest in good books and be hungry for knowledge. A wise man must always hear and increase in learning – Prov. 1:5.

Prayer

O Lord, give me a teachable spirit in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am hungry for the right knowledge!
