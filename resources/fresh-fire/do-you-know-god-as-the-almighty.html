Do You Know God as the Almighty?

In short: There are many mighty men but there is only one Almighty God. “El-Shaddai” is another name of God which means the Almighty or the all-sufficient one.

And when Abram was ninety years old and nine, the Lord appeared to Abram, and said unto him, I am the almighty God: walk before me, and be thou perfect.
— Genesis 17:1

There are many mighty men but there is only one Almighty God. “El-Shaddai” is another name of God which means the Almighty or the all-sufficient one. It also means “the mighty one with many breasts.” As a child of God, you must know that if God can’t do it, then nobody can. This is because there is none higher than the Most high. In these hard times, only God can satisfy you!

Lets us critically examine the case of Abraham and Sarah. When Abraham was an old man and Sarah’s womb was dead (menopause, God said, “In one year when you’re a hundred years old and you’re not good for anything, I’ll perform the miracle” (Gen. 17:4-6). In the sight of men this was closed case; there was no way out. However the “El-Shaddai” God stepped into Abraham’s situation and impossibility became a possibility. There is no closed-cause with God. The harder it is for man; the better it is for God! Your case is not yet over. I don’t care your condition now; the Almighty God can change your life for the better.

He is the “mighty breasted one” and the breast meets every need of the baby. It is the baby’s milk, porridge, water, diet etc. The breast is all a baby needs to survive. In the same way, God is all you need to make it in life.

Hopeless situations do not make God helpless. I don’t care how mighty your obstacle is, the Almighty God is sending help to you today.

Prayer

Almighty God, change all my obstacles into miracles in Jesus Name: Amen!!

Confession

God is all I need to make it in life!
