It’s Time to Make a Turn!

In short: There comes a time in our lives we find it very necessary to make a TURN. This is because in life, until you make a turn, it is never your turn.

To open their eyes, and to turn them from darkness to light, and from the power of Satan unto God, that they may receive forgiveness of sins, and inheritance among them which are sanctified by faith which is in me.

— Acts 26:18

There comes a time in our lives we find it very necessary to make a TURN. This is because in life, until you make a turn, it is never your turn. We must make definite and desperate decisions to turn from many things; from darkness to light and from the power of satan unto God. Turn to God and He will turn your darkness to light. Turn away from some things, some habits, sins, and hindrances, to receive God’s richest blessing. Turn to God in prayer and you will receive beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning; the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness.

When you make this turn, you will receive forgiveness of sins, and an inheritance among them which are sanctified by faith that is in Christ Jesus our Lord. When you turn God is also ready to turn some things for your good. Psalm 30:11 says: “Thou hast turned for me my mourning into dancing; Thou hast put off my sackcloth, and girded me with gladness. “ He will turn your mourning into dancing; He will put off your sackcloth and gird you with gladness. Turn to faith in God. Turn to holiness without which no man shall see God.

Your walk with God should be continuous. Turn to God in weariness. When you turn to Him you will see His glory. God will surely show up. He will bring you answers to your problems. Turn and experience peace and joy in the Holy Ghost. Turn towards the light. He will overturn, overrule, and accelerate your spiritual and life’s progress. He will bring order to your life. Turn from following vanities to serve the Living God, which made Heaven and earth, the sea and all things therein - Acts 14:15

Prayer

Lord, help me to turn from every sin and weight that easily beset me, and turn to You always, in Jesus Name, Amen.

Confession

I will follow Jesus all the days of my life!
