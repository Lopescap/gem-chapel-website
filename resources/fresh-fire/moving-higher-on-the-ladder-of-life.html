Moving Higher on the Ladder of Life

In short: In life, some crawl, others walk, some run and others fly. As a child of God, you must decide to be a highflier in life.

Who are these that fly as a cloud, and as the doves to their windows?

— Isaiah 60:8

In life, some crawl, others walk, some run and others fly. As a child of God, you must decide to be a highflier in life. One man said, “ Destiny is not a matter of chance, it is a matter of choice; it is not a thing to be waited for, it is a thing to be achieved.”

Of all the creatures in the world, human beings are physically the most ill-equipped. A human cannot fly like a bird, outrun a leopard, swim like an alligator nor climb like a monkey. A human does not have the eye of an eagle nor the claws and teeth of a wild cat. Physically humans are helpless and defenceless. However, God is reasonable and kind. God’s greatest gift to humankind is the ability to think and make the right choices in life. Humans can therefore create their own environment, whereas animals adapt to their own environment. Sadly, very few people use the greatest gift- the ability to think – to its full potential. Failures are of two kinds: those who did and never thought and those who thought and never did.

Life is full of choices and choices have consequences. Today, you are a result of the choices you made yesterday; tomorrow, you will be a picture of the choices you are making today. That is why you must think through your decisions and make the right choices if you really desire to gain new heights this New Year. When you eat too much, you make a choice to be overweight; when you decide to take alcohol, you make a choice to destroy your liver and break your home; when you don’t care for other people, you choose not to be cared for by other people. Always be guided in your thinking and choices by the Word of God. By so doing, you will know how to refuse the evil, and choose the good as Jesus did (Isaiah 7:14-15).

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to make the right choices in life in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am flying higher and higher!
