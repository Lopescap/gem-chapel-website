See the Future!

In short: It takes a cause not to faint – II Cor.4:16-17.If you don’t have a cause you are pursuing you will be wearied and faint in no time. If you are in pursuit of inner renewal, will be least bordered that the outward man is perishing.

16 For which cause we faint not; but though our outward man perish, yet the inward man is renewed day by day.17 For our light affliction, which is but for a moment, worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory;
— 2 Corinthians 4:16-17

It takes a cause not to faint – II Cor.4:16-17.If you don’t have a cause you are pursuing you will be wearied and faint in no time. If you are in pursuit of inner renewal, will be least bordered that the outward man is perishing.

When you know what awaits you in the future, you are

never wearied of doing what is right in the present. Future

consciousness is motivation to present action.

Keeping in view what awaits you is the strength of your waiting. Understanding what awaits you will naturally motivate you to keep waiting. Consciousness of gains awaiting you is what keeps your eyes off pending pains. If you see the gain you don’t feel the pains. This is because, you are sure the pains will soon culminate in gain. Pain doesn’t last; it is gain that endures.

Pain soon fades but gain will make you shine. Your renewal stops when you get wearied. If you faint, your harvest will certainly fail. The Bible says of Jesus in Hebrews 12:2, ‘Who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross despising the shame.’

In this year, keep your focus on the prize; don’t be distracted by trivialities. There is no price too high to pay for your vision and mission. Refuse to give up; never faint!

Prayer

O Lord, give me a fighting spirit in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will never lose heart; I see a bright future ahead of me!
