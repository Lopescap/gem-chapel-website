The Mystery of Sacrifice

In short: One of the greatest mysteries you must understand is the mystery of altars. An altar is a place where spirits meet; IT IS A PLACE OF SACRIFICE.

And the LORD appeared unto Abram, and said, Unto thy seed will I give this land: and there builded he an altar unto the LORD, who appeared unto him.

— Genesis 12:7

One of the greatest mysteries you must understand is the mystery of altars. An altar is a place where spirits meet; IT IS A PLACE OF SACRIFICE.

There are two kinds of altars; Divine altars (altars that Christians build in honour of God) and there are demonic altars (altars that the unbelievers build in honour of the devil). Life can be defined in simple terms as a battle between altars. This means that, if a Christian will lose in life, it will be because the altars of his enemies are stronger and greater than his.

You will realize that Abraham, one of the greatest fathers of faith always built altars for God. That is why he had a close relationship with God and became great in life. He knew that divine words need divine altars for them to be fulfilled. On the other hand, Lot was not accustomed to the building of altars unto God and suffered greatly for it.

Ge 12:7 says, ; And the LORD appeared unto Abram, and said, Unto thy seed will I give this land: and there builded he an altar unto the LORD, who appeared unto him. Ge 13:18 also says, ‘Then Abram removed his tent, and came and dwelt in the plain of Mamre, which is in Hebron, and built there an altar unto the LORD.’

Never forget that there are demonic altars that are being built on a daily basis against our lives as children of God and we must do well to build counter divine altars. That is why as a Christian you must sacrifice your PERSON – Romans 12:1-2, your PRAYER – Col. 4:12, your PRAISE (Heb. 13:15) and your POSSESSIONS – Exodus 20:24 on a daily basis to God.

Prayer

O Lord, let my divine altars be stronger than those of my enemies in Jesus Name!

Confession

I operate with a strong spirit of sacrifice!
