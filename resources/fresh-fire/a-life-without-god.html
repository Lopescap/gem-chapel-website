A Life Without God

In short: As a plant must be rooted in a soil for it to flourish, so a man can only succeed when he is rooted and grounded in God.

And he said, let me go , for the day breaketh, And he said , I will not let you go, except you bless me

— Genesis 32:26

As a plant must be rooted in a soil for it to flourish, so a man can only succeed when he is rooted and grounded in God. It was said of Abraham Lincoln that one of the most his important prayers he prayed as the President of the United States of America was ‘Lord, without You I MUST FAIL.’ Yes! Without God, you are programmed to fail and condemned to lose! Man was created as a vessel to contain God and without God, you are empty.

One day, Jacob was left alone and he wrestled with an angel. During the wrestling, his thigh joint was dislocated. Immediately Jacob’s thigh was dislocated, he knew he had lost the strength to run after blessings, business enterprises and all other activities. He had come to the place where he had no option but to lean on God.

His only means of ever getting blessings was for that ‘Personality’ to bless him. That is exactly what God wants. God wants to bring you to a point and time where you cannot achieve anything apart from Him. Jacob lost his strength; he needed another to enrich and bless him. His life was useless to him if the man didn’t bless him.

When God overcomes you completely, you will naturally lean on Him. You will hang on to Him as the one and only source of your life and blessings. Those who can still do a lot of things for themselves whether God helps them or not are very far from God’s demand. God intentionally reduced Gideon’s army from 32,000 to 300 soldiers so that he will know that the battle was the Lord’s.

You must be weak to become strong. You must be helpless to receive help. You must be broken if you want to breakthrough. For His strength can be perfected in weakness. You must totally lean on God because you will be a colossal failure without Him.

Prayer

O Lord, let every unbroken area in my life be broken in Jesus Name.

Confession

I am nothing without God!
