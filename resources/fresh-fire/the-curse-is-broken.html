The Curse Is Broken!

In short: Praise the Lord! We are so excited that when Jesus Christ died on the Cross and was buried, He didn’t remain in the grave but rose up triumphantly on the third day.

13 Christ has redeemed us from the curse of the law, having become a curse for us (for it is written, “Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree”), 14 that the blessing of Abraham might come upon the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith

— Galatians 3:13-14

Praise the Lord! We are so excited that when Jesus Christ died on the Cross and was buried, He didn’t remain in the grave but rose up triumphantly on the third day. The death and the resurrection of Jesus Christ broke the curse that was hanging over the human race and set in motion a string of unending blessings. That is why Jesus wore the Crown of thorns...By that act, He was saying, I have carried your curses - Mt 27:29 And when they had platted a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand: and they bowed the knee before him, and mocked him, saying, Hail, King of the Jews!

When Jesus resurrected, He placed us under the canopy of God’s blessings - Acts 3:26 Unto you first God, having raised up his Son Jesus, sent him to bless you, in turning away every one of you from his iniquities. Galatians 3: 13-14, ‘ 13 Christ has redeemed us from the curse of the law, having become a curse for us (for it is written, “Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree”), 14 that the blessing of Abraham might come upon the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith’

A curse is a declaration that empowers a man to fail by all means. A person operating under a curse is easy to recognize, because he will be struggling against something, and will never be able to master the thing or overcome it. At the point of success, frustration comes in because the curse is limiting that person. They don’t succeed in education, marriage, career and other areas of their lives. Therefore, from today receive Jesus Christ into your heart and walk in the consciousness of the fact that every curse in your life and family is broken. Yes! You are blessed beyond a curse! Where others fail, you will prevail and where other fall, you will rise in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord, break every curse by Your Resurrection power in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am under the canopy of God’s blessings!
