Check Who You Are Following

In short: It is very surprising that most people pay attention to the quality of water they drink, the quality of food they eat, the quality of dress they wear, the quality of house they build and the quality of air they breathe BUT THEY RARELY PAY ATTENTION TO THE QUALITY & CALIBRE OF PEOPLE THEY FOLLOW!

But thou, O man of God, flee these things; and follow after righteousness, godliness, faith, love, patience, meekness.

— 1 Timothy 6:11

It is very surprising that most people pay attention to the quality of water they drink, the quality of food they eat, the quality of dress they wear, the quality of house they build and the quality of air they breathe BUT THEY RARELY PAY ATTENTION TO THE QUALITY & CALIBRE OF PEOPLE THEY FOLLOW!

In life, who you follow, will determine what will follow you. If you follow the wrong company, your life will attract only wrong things! Who you walk with determines what walks into your life or walks out of your life!

The kind of people you follow can either lead to your speedy destruction or continuous preservation.

The doom of Samson was sponsored by his association with Delilah and the premature death of Amnon was due to his connection with Jonadab. On the other hand, King David was constantly saved from death through his friendship with Jonathan. Your companions will direct your opinions; your company will determine your accomplishment and your association will determine your manifestations.

1Co 15:33 says, ‘Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners.’

Popularity is not equal to relevance; it is an exemplary life-style that makes a person relevant enough for you to follow him or her. Therefore, before you call somebody a role model, the person must be a good example to you in all things. Worldly people should never be your role models! -II Cor. 6:18

Follow those who uphold godliness and spirituality and not those who are given to worldliness, profanity and insanity. - 1Ti 6:11 But thou, O man of God, flee these things; and follow after righteousness, godliness, faith, love, patience, meekness.

Always remember that, Eagles don’t flock with turkeys.

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to always follow the right people in Jesus Name!

Confession

I only follow the right people!
