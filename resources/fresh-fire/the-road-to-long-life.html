The Road to Long Life!

In short: All over the world most people wish others long life and prosperity and not short life and poverty. As a child of God, you must believe God for long life. The life of everything is extended when the Blood of Jesus is invoked.

For it is the life of all flesh; the blood of it is for the life therefore: therefore I said unto the children of Israel, Ye shall eateth blood of no manner of flesh; for the life of all flesh is the blood therefore: Whosoever eateth it shall be cut off”.

— Leviticus 17:14

All over the world most people wish others long life and prosperity and not short life and poverty. As a child of God, you must believe God for long life. The life of everything is extended when the Blood of Jesus is invoked. God commanded the Israelites not to eat the blood of any animal. The reason being that the life of animals was in their blood. By this statement, God was establishing an eternal truth that the “blood is the custodian of life.” When you lose your blood, you will surely lose your life. Therefore, to be far away from the Blood of Jesus is to be far away from the abundant life that is in Christ Jesus.

That is why Jesus said in John 6:53, “Most assuredly, I say to you, unless you eat the flesh of the son of Man and drink his blood, you have NO LIFE in you. ” The Blood of Jesus Christ has power to give life. Every time you say, “The Blood of Jesus”, you are bringing God’s very life on the scene. Therefore, the Blood can bring life to any dead situation and extend the life of everything and everyone.

Are you going through a life-threatening sickness? Is your marriage at the point of death? Is your spiritual life dying? Is your ministry or business dying? Don’t give up!! The Bible says of Jesus Christ in Acts 2:24, “Whom God hath raised up, having loosed the pains of death: because it was not possible that he should be holden of it.” There is still hope for you because of the Resurrection power of Jesus. Just plead the Blood in faith over every area of your life or any situation that is dying and it shall surely jump back to life.

Prayer

Father, let the Blood of Jesus inject life into anything dying situation in my life now, in Jesus Name. Amen!

Confession

My life is extended through the Blood of Jesus!
