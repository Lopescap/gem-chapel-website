How to Sweat Less in Life

In short: Acombination of Holy Spirit and human Spirit is what makes an excellent destiny. What man can do for himself; God will never do for him. Planning is as potent as prayer.

The preparations of the heart in man, and the answer of the tongue, is from the LORD.

— Proverbs 16:1

Acombination of Holy Spirit and human Spirit is what makes an excellent destiny. What man can do for himself; God will never do for him. Planning is as potent as prayer. This is because God does not only act according to your prayers but He equally acts according to your thinking. Ephesians 3:20 says “Now unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ASK OR THINK, according to the power that worketh in us,” ‘Asking’ is praying but ‘thinking’ is planning and God answers them both.

Failure to plan is planning to fail. Every successful venture in life must be preceded by proper planning otherwise it will end as a bitter adventure. It is success in planning that leads to success in life. Listen to Jesus Christ Himself in Luke 14:28-30, “For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it? Lest haply, after he hath laid the foundation, and is not able to finish it, all that behold it begin to mock him, Saying, This man began to build, and was not able to finish.”

You will sweat less when you plan well. Every great builder is first and foremost a great planner .Every vision works (unfolds) by planning .The secret trade mark of an enterprise is strategy, planning. Effect is not in noise but planning. There is no accident success; you plan for it.

Planning comes via thinking and writing and the major raw material for planning is the right knowledge. Begin to plan well if you really want to end well in life.

Prayer

O Lord, help me to plan my life very well in Jesus name!

Confession

I am a serious planner; my destiny shall be a glorious one!
