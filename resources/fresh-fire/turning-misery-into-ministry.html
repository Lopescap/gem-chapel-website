Turning Misery into Ministry

In short: God often uses sorrow in our lives to open our eyes to the pain of others and to equip us to comfort them. Something good happens when we get our eyes off our own pain and minister out of it to hurting people.

6 Even when we are weighed down with troubles, it is for your comfort and salvation! For when we ourselves are comforted, we will certainly comfort you. Then you can patiently endure the same things we suffer. 7 We are confident that as you share in our sufferings, you will also share in the comfort God gives us.

— 2 Corinthians 1:6-7

God often uses sorrow in our lives to open our eyes to the pain of others and to equip us to comfort them. Something good happens when we get our eyes off our own pain and minister out of it to hurting people. In the midst of comforting others through the power of the Holy Spirit, we find the comfort we crave.

Look at the life of the Patriarch Joseph. He started having a lot of wonderful dreams at a tender age and he was the favourite of his father Jacob. However, he started experiencing misery when out of hatred, his own brothers sold him into slavery. His troubles continued when he was falsely accused by the wife of Potiphar and finally landed in the prison.

However, Joseph while in prison turned his misery into ministry. He was a great source of hope and inspiration to other prisoners. He interpreted the dreams of the butler and the baker. Joseph finally landed in the palace because he made a concrete decision to always turn his misery into ministry.

The Apostle Paul always turned his misery into ministry. Listen to him in II Corinthians 1:6-7, ‘6 Even when we are weighed down with troubles, it is for your comfort and salvation! For when we ourselves are comforted, we will certainly comfort you. Then you can patiently endure the same things we suffer. 7 We are confident that as you share in our sufferings, you will also share in the comfort God gives us.’

This was how although he was a latecomer, he eventually became a frontliner in the early Church.

Receive Grace to always turn your misery into ministry in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord, open my eyes to the hurts of those around me and make me a blessing to them in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am a blessing to many!
