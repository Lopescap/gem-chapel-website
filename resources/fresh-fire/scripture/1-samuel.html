1 Samuel

Devotionals referencing 1 Samuel

  • The Covering of His Glory — Part 1
    Every Christian has a glorious destiny. Your faith in God guarantees a life of unending glory.
  • How to Increase Your Performance
    Performance in any duty or assignment is enhanced when you consider the task as a privilege. When you take any assignment for granted, you start failing at it. Every man is at his peak performance whenever it is seen as a privilege to serve.
  • Supernatural Proofs!
    On the Cross Jesus Christ made a public show of the devil in a triumphant procession - Col 2:15 And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.
  • The Best Way to Deal with Challenges
    Challenges come the way of all men and women, young and old, learned and unlearned, rich and poor – just to all kinds of people.
  • A Day Without Prayer
    Prayer is not what we choose to do but what we must do; it is not what we like to do but what we are mandated to do.
  • The Value-System of Heaven
    The measuring Scale of God is different from that of men because the values of God are completely different from those of men. The value-system of Heaven is different from that of men.
  • Overcoming Evil Decrees
    As a Christian, you must never be ignorant of evil or demonic decrees. One of the major weapons the enemy uses to keep people in bondage is evil decrees.
