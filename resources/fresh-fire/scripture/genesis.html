Genesis

Devotionals referencing Genesis

  • How to Produce Great Results
    The easiest way to multiply your results is to walk with God. Walking with the great invisible is what produces great and visible results. Every Christian must work for God in his or her life-time.
  • Supernatural Faith — Part 1
    Beloved, the Christian walk is a walk of faith. we only believe what we see with our physical eyes, then what would a blind man believe. The Christian walk is impossible without the indispensable nugget of faith.
  • Your Case Is Different
    When you encounter God, you become a candidate for good things. When other men who do not know Your God are saying there is a casting down, you will be rising continually . Your case will simply be different.
  • Do You Know God as the Creator?
    The name “Elohim” describes God as the creator. In other words, our God is the creator of the world and everything that exist in it.
  • Do You Know God as the Almighty?
    There are many mighty men but there is only one Almighty God. “El-Shaddai” is another name of God which means the Almighty or the all-sufficient one.
  • Do You Know God as the Master?
    Jesus Christ is not only your Saviour, He is also your Lord.
  • Do You Know God as the Provider?
    Every true father provides for his children. Therefore God, who is our heavenly Father is always ready to provide for us His children.
  • Supernatural Makeover!
    One of these days, you will wake up in the robes of a king in Jesus Name! My dear brother/sister, in every pain, there is a hidden gain. The challenges of life must never cause you to give up.
  • Don’t Barter Away Your Destiny
    In our world today, most people major in the minors and minor in the majors. This generation usually values the trivial and treats the important with contempt. As Isaac’s firstborn son, Esau was entitled to the birthright and inheritance.
  • How to Change Your Life for the Better
    The imagination of man is the limit of man. This is because you are a direct product of your thoughts; YOU EITHER RISE OR FALL BASED ON THE CONDITION OF YOUR MIND.
  • How to Remain at the Top
    Never care for accolades; for entitlements are not in titles. Never seek for rewards. Never be bothered about who appreciates or do not appreciate you or your works.
  • God-Addiction
    Today, as we end our journey of developing a closer walk with God, we want to learn how to develop a closer walk with God.
  • Fruitfulness Is Your Destiny
    Fruitfulness is God’s original design for every person. Genesis 1:27-28 says, “So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.
  • Be God-Focused! — Part 1
    To grow in success in life, you must be God focused and not work or career focused. You must do everything to develop an unbreakable relationship with God if you really desire to have a lasting success.
