1 Corinthians

Devotionals referencing 1 Corinthians

  • Loving God
    This generation really need people who genuinely love God with all their hearts. We don’t just need Church members; we need God lovers. We don’t just need Men of God; we need lovers of God.
  • You Must Stretch Out to Spread Out — Part 2
    Don’t give up! You are advancing and exceling no matter what in Jesus Name! Yesterday, we learnt about the importance of goals in a man’s life. Today, we want to learn about the process of setting and re-defining your goals.
  • It’s All About Jesus!
    On the Mount of Transfiguration, Moses and Elijah appeared to Jesus. However at the end of the day only JESUS remained standing (Luke 9). Your goal in life must be to keep Jesus at the centre of all you do.
  • Connecting to Greater Glory
    The Bible says that the glory of the latter house shall be greater than the former. God has so many wonderful plans for you but the question is, ‘Are you ready?’. Everything starts and ends with the heart.
  • Do You Know God as the Conqueror?
    Battles are satan’s reaction to the advancement and progress of men. In fact, the brighter your destiny, the harsher your attackers.
  • The Mystery of the Cross
    Welcome to another devotional encounter with the loving God who gave His own Son for the salvation of mankind. In recent times, the message of the cross has been relegated to the background in many churches.
  • Developing Spiritual Toughness
    Body fasting is the platform for spiritual fattening. Physical leanness is what births spiritual toughness. If you must get fatter spiritually then you must engage more in fasting. The body must “perish” for the inner man to be renewed. II Cor.
  • How to Move from Strength to Strength
    You must develop an unquenchable love for the House of God if your desire is to grow in the spirit. In life, it is your company that determines your accomplishments and your companions direct your opinions.
  • The Greatest Threat to Christianity
    The greatest threat to Christianity is Christians trying to make it to Heaven without doing anything for God. In life, we are owners of nothing but stewards of everything. Stewardship a call to labour.
