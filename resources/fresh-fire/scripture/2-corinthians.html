2 Corinthians

Devotionals referencing 2 Corinthians

  • You Must Spend Quality Time in God’s Presence
    Beloved, you must do everything possible to walk in the power of God. This is because, the only language the devil and his cohorts understand is power. However, no one can gain access to a thing from a man unless he first gains access to his person.
  • Your Point of Uniqueness
    In life, you must understand that although you don’t have everything, you definitely have something. In fact, there is something within you that the world cannot do without.
  • It’s All About Jesus!
    On the Mount of Transfiguration, Moses and Elijah appeared to Jesus. However at the end of the day only JESUS remained standing (Luke 9). Your goal in life must be to keep Jesus at the centre of all you do.
  • Miracle-Producing Faith — Part II
    Every word of Scripture is a more sure Word of Prophecy. This means, it can’t change, it is established, it is bound to come to pass. However, what delays or eventually stops the manifestation of God’s Word in our lives is unbelief.
  • Developing Spiritual Toughness
    Body fasting is the platform for spiritual fattening. Physical leanness is what births spiritual toughness. If you must get fatter spiritually then you must engage more in fasting. The body must “perish” for the inner man to be renewed. II Cor.
  • The Ultimate Purpose of Study
    The primary purpose of study is to get information but the ultimate is to press for transformation. The whole essence of knowledge is transformation. If it is not applicable, then it is not profitable.
  • Don’t Withdraw When the Battle-Line Is Drawn
    Most people fail in life not because they lack knowledge or talent but because they quit. When problems seem insurmountable, quitting may look like the easiest way out. It is true for every marriage, ministry, job and relationship.
  • See the Future!
    It takes a cause not to faint – II Cor.4:16-17.If you don’t have a cause you are pursuing you will be wearied and faint in no time. If you are in pursuit of inner renewal, will be least bordered that the outward man is perishing.
  • The Answer to Death
    Death is the ultimate that the devil can do and resurrection is the answer to death. The devil thought that the demise of Jesus Christ was permanent and that He will be in the grave forever.
  • The Giving Grace
    The Grace of God is the maker of men; no glorious destiny can be constructed without the ingredient of Grace.
  • The Healing Grace
    Sickness and weakness are all satanic afflictions. They are arrows sent by the enemy to slow you down in the journey of life. You can be made strong out of your weakness.
  • The Prosperity Grace
    Very shortly, you will become an object of argument. Your hand will no longer be empty. Your pocket will no longer be empty. Your hand will not touch the bottom of your pocket or bag again.
  • How to Access Grace
    2Co 9:14, ‘And by their prayer for you, which long after you for the exceeding grace of God in you.’
  • Be Strong in the Lord!
    Those who dominate in the spiritual realm will automatically dominate in the physical realm. This is because everything is a spiritual situation before it becomes a physical condition. In this New Year, you must be fervent in spirit.
  • What You Have Can Create What You Need!
    In life, you must understand that although you don’t have everything, you definitely have something . In fact, there is something within you that the world cannot do without.
  • Turning Misery into Ministry
    God often uses sorrow in our lives to open our eyes to the pain of others and to equip us to comfort them. Something good happens when we get our eyes off our own pain and minister out of it to hurting people.
  • The Starting Point of Transformation
    Every Samson who’s laid with a Delilah, every David who committed adultery with a Bathsheba and later betrayed his Uriah, every Judas who sold out his Jesus began his downfall with a THOUGHT.
  • Persevere No Matter What!
    Most people fail in life not because they lack knowledge or talent but because they quit . When problems seem insurmountable, quitting may look like the easiest way out. It is true for every marriage, ministry, job and relationship.
  • Check Who You Are Following
    It is very surprising that most people pay attention to the quality of water they drink, the quality of food they eat, the quality of dress they wear, the quality of house they build and the quality of air they breathe BUT THEY RARELY PAY ATTENTION TO THE QUALITY & CALIBRE OF PEOPLE THEY FOLLOW!
