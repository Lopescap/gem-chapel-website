John

Devotionals referencing John

  • You Must Spend Quality Time in God’s Presence
    Beloved, you must do everything possible to walk in the power of God. This is because, the only language the devil and his cohorts understand is power. However, no one can gain access to a thing from a man unless he first gains access to his person.
  • You Must Always Be in Touch with Heaven
    Those who are in touch with Heaven are empowered to change their world. As a child of God, don’t just pray; learn to listen for His voice. Have you ever wondered why Jesus never made a single misstep?
  • How to Produce Great Results
    The easiest way to multiply your results is to walk with God. Walking with the invisible is what produces visible results. Every Christian must work for God in his or her life- time. However, to effectively work for God, you must first walk with God.
  • Supernatural Faith – Faith 3
    Faith is the engine that makes the impossible to become possible. Wherever faith goes, signs and wonders follow. It is active faith in the God of possibilities that causes you to emerge out of the adversities of life with a testimony.
  • The More You Know Him, the More You Become Like Him
    Beloved, the only way to know God is by having a revelation in your spirit. This is because, God is a spirit and we can really contact Him with our spirits (John 4:24).
  • The Covering of His Glory — Part 2
    Beloved, there is something about you as a Christian. Human mind can’t comprehend it; human words can’t explain it. It is God’s glory.
  • Developing Spiritual Toughness
    Body fasting is the platform for spiritual fattening. Physical leanness is what births spiritual toughness. If you must get fatter spiritually then you must engage more in fasting. The body must “perish” for the inner man to be renewed. II Cor.
  • How to Get Your Prayers Answered!
    Soul wining is the heart beat of God. Do you want 100% answered prayer? Do you know why some of the things you have asked God have not come or have delayed? That’s the reason for the morning’s devotion.
  • Experiencing the Abundant Life
    The abundant life is the heritage and the redemptive right of every child of God (John 10:10). God wants to bless us to a level that we will not have enough room to contain it.
  • What Are You Living For?
    Life without a cause will end under a cloud. In other words, when you don’t live according to a specific purpose, you will die in obscurity. Living without a cause is like living under a curse. Cause is the cure to fainting.
  • The Road to Long Life!
    All over the world most people wish others long life and prosperity and not short life and poverty. As a child of God, you must believe God for long life. The life of everything is extended when the Blood of Jesus is invoked.
  • The Answer to Death
    Death is the ultimate that the devil can do and resurrection is the answer to death. The devil thought that the demise of Jesus Christ was permanent and that He will be in the grave forever.
  • Supernatural Proofs!
    On the Cross Jesus Christ made a public show of the devil in a triumphant procession - Col 2:15 And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.
  • An Encounter with the Living Christ
    The Apostle Paul declared out of holy hunger and desperation in Philippians 3: 10 That I may know him, and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings, being made conformable unto his death;
  • Growing in God
    You cannot help studying God’s Word without concluding that Relationships are a high priority with God. He cares about His creatures! Love permeates His very Nature and every aspect of His Person.
  • Practicing God’s Presence
    Godly living is living with God in our lives. In other words, recognizing and practicing God’s presence, His life in us. God also wants us to follow His instructions and celebrate His blessings.
  • The Purpose of the Anointing
    Jesus the “Anointed one” (the Messiah) in Isaiah 61:1, spoke of His Messianic task in Luke 4:18-19.
  • The Saving Grace
    Grace simply means undeserved or unmerited favour. It simply means when God does for you what you don’t deserve.
  • The Long Road
    Ayoung girl gave her teacher a very special gift. It was a beautiful seashell. “Where did you get this?” she asked. The child told her that such shells are found only on a certain faraway beach.
  • The Footprints of God
    Several years ago, a great French scientist was crossing the Arabian desert under the influence of an Arab (Christian) tourist guide. Along the way, the sat down to take a rest and the guide started praying.
