Matthew

Devotionals referencing Matthew

  • You Must Always Act on His Instructions
    Most modern day Christians are revelation rich and application poor. Whenever you pray, you must first listen to the voice of God and after that act on the instructions you received.
  • The Cure for Depression
    Depression is the gateway to oppression. One of the most dangerous parties to attend is a ‘pity party’. Pity parties cause you to live in a realm of anxiety and worry.
  • Do You Know God as the Healer?
    There are many strange sicknesses and diseases that attack mortal men in this world That’s why as a child of God, you must have a revelation of your heavenly Father as a Healer.
  • The Realm of God!
    In the realm of God, there is nothing which is impossible. Men can fail, but God will always prevail. True faith does not look at the intensity/gravity of a problem, but the immensity of God’s power.
  • How to Experience Kingdom Prosperity
    Fresh Fire for today is the best gift you can give to someone. Most Christians are struggling financially nowadays because they have refused to use simple Bible-keys.
  • Following for Success — Part 1
    Man is never created to be self-dependent; he is created to be a follower. When he ceases to follow he will certainly head for doom. A Disciple is a follower i.e. one called to follow.
  • Following for Success — Part 2
    He must always strive to catch up with the spirit of his leader. Joshua and Caleb followed Moses until they imbibe the spirit of Moses in them. Num.27:18 – Joshua/Num.14:24 – Caleb.
  • The Greatest Threat to Christianity
    The greatest threat to Christianity is Christians trying to make it to Heaven without doing anything for God. In life, we are owners of nothing but stewards of everything. Stewardship a call to labour.
