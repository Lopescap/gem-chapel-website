The Way of Purity

In short: The most authentic source of spiritual authority is purity. Sin is a destroyer, a blocker and a sinker. It blocks the ears of God and shortens the hands of God. Reuben and Samson due to sexual immorality missed their greatness.

Many times did he deliver them; but they provoked him with their counsel, and were brought low for their iniquity.
— Psalm 106:43

The most authentic source of spiritual authority is purity. Sin is a destroyer, a blocker and a sinker. It blocks the ears of God and shortens the hands of God. Reuben and Samson due to sexual immorality missed their greatness.

Sin will bring you low - Ps 106:43 Many times did he deliver them; but they provoked him with their counsel, and were brought low for their iniquity. However, righteousness will take you up - Pr 14:34 ¶ Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin is a reproach to any people.

Never allow sin to contaminate your destiny. You must learn to deal with sin the right way by confessing and forsaking it - Pr 28:13 ¶ He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy. At that point, you must believe that the Blood of Jesus Christ has cleansed you from all sins; then get up and go on with God; don’t wallow in self- condemnation.

If there is something rotten in your refrigerator, you know it every time you open the door. You may not know exactly where it is located, but the smell tells you it’s in there. Our lives are like that. If there is something wrong within us, those we come into contact with are going to sense it, whether or not they know where it is or what it is. It gives off an odour that is detectable. That’s why we must continually open ourselves to God and allow the Holy Spirit to come in, cleanse our hearts and remove whatever and remove whatever is hindering us in our walk with Him. From today there will be no more contamination in your life in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord, by Your mercies, let the Blood of Jesus cleanse me from all sins in Jesus Name!

Confession

Purity is my way of life!
