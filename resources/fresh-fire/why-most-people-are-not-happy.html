Why Most People Are Not Happy

In short: Nobody can be truly happy when he is outside the will of God . One of the most horrible things that could happen to a person is when the person is outside where God wants him to be and he does not realize it until he gets to the grave.

Are not Abana and Pharpar, rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel? may I not wash in them, and be clean? So he turned and went away in a rage.
— 2 Kings 5:12

Nobody can be truly happy when he is outside the will of God . One of the most horrible things that could happen to a person is when the person is outside where God wants him to be and he does not realize it until he gets to the grave. Such a person would end up living a wasted life. You can never please God anywhere else but where He wants you to be . You may be popular amongst people and anytime you are around, they praise you, but if you were not where He wanted you to be, you would have chosen the second best for your life. Whatever our achievements, we must not miss God’s purpose for our lives. If you feel too big to be where God wants you to be, you would be cheating yourself. But when you are in a place of divine appointment, then you are in the flow of divine miracle. If somebody had told the little boy whose fish and bread the Lord used to feed the multitude when he was living home his provision would feed 5,000 people that day, he would have said, “No. This one is just for me. How can 5,000 feed on this?” When you are in a place of God’s purpose for your life, you are in a place of power. A person may be working very hard on a very good thing but if he is in the wrong place, he would be wasting his time; and it could be said that he is rebellious. So, the question is: Are you in the right place and in the right condition? Is your present spiritual level where God wants it to be? Namaan wanted to go to Abana and Pharpar, rivers of Damascus, but God had no appointment with Abana and Pharpar. Although they were nice rivers, the divine appointment was with the dirty river Jordan. It was in the River Jordan that he received His healing. Many of us are heading for Abana and Pharpar when we should go to Jordan.

May God help you to always be in the right place in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord, always direct my steps in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will enjoy life to the fullest!
