Learn to Celebrate Others

In short: King Saul instead of providing quality leadership for Israel vacated his throne and went into a chasing spree after an ordinary citizen of the land and a teenage shepherd called David.

Wrath is cruel, and anger is outrageous; but who is able to stand before JEALOUSY?

— Proverbs 27:4

King Saul instead of providing quality leadership for Israel vacated his throne and went into a chasing spree after an ordinary citizen of the land and a teenage shepherd called David. Since his throne became EMPTY, somebody else had to OCCUPY it and in his case, it was no other person than his perceived rival David. It is UNWISE, UNNECESSARY & UNGODLY to compare your success with those of others and at the end of the day become jealous - Proverbs 27:4 says, ‘Wrath is cruel, and anger is outrageous; but who is able to stand before JEALOUSY?’

Jealousy is the CANCER OF THE SPIRIT, the JAUNDICE OF THE SOUL and the BREAKER OF THE BODY! In life, it is not just what you are eating but also WHAT IS EATING YOU UP!

Normal jealousy is a feeling that comes on in an instant, one which we can dismiss on our own.

Unhealthy jealousy happens when we indulge that feeling and engage in acts impulsively from a place of SUSPICION and INSECURITY

Jealousy is a breaker of focus. Instead of running your race and concentrating on your assignment, jealousy will cause you to waste your time trying to undo or outdo someone else. When you are jealous;

- You don’t celebrate anything except it is coming from

you; you always think the whole world should revolve around you. In jealousy there is more self-love than love.

- You are unwilling to accommodate and celebrate the

success as well as the victories of others

From today, learn to celebrate others and genuine appreciate what

others do because what you don’t admire, you can never attract.

Prayer

O Lord, eliminate the spirit of jealousy from my life in Jesus Name!

Confession

I always celebrate others!
