How to Achieve Extrda-Ordinary Results

In short: When confronted with challenges, ask less questions and engage more action. Your action terrorizes opposition and attracts divine helps.

¶ The former account I made, O Theophilus, of all that Jesus began both to do and teach
— Acts 1:1

When confronted with challenges, ask less questions and engage more action. Your action terrorizes opposition and attracts divine helps. Those who ask too much questions may end as question marks and those who make too much analysis may suffer paralysis. All great achievements are born out of action. You don’t know what you can do until you begin to act.

Real leaders act more than they speak. While people may be inspired by your speech they will only follow you for your acts. Act what you say then say what you acted thereafter. You may have more admirers for what you say but you will only command followership only by what you act. Begin with action then end with speech. That was the way of Jesus – Act.1:1. People believed and followed Him because of His great acts of sacrifice and compassion.

People followed Him for His acts. To grow into this realm of leadership, be full of acts and less of words as people will understand you better and follow you for your action. May you receive the Grace to take the relevant steps that will cause a dramatic turnaround in your destiny in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to take the right steps in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am action-oriented!
