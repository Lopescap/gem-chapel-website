Fresh Fire for Today

  • You Are at Your Best When You Are in the Spirit!
    The believer’s habitat is the Holy Spirit just as water is to fish, hole is to foxes and nest is to birds. Therefore, as a child of God, YOU ARE AT YOUR BEST WHEN YOU ARE IN THE SPIRIT.
  • You Must Spend Quality Time in God’s Presence
    Beloved, you must do everything possible to walk in the power of God. This is because, the only language the devil and his cohorts understand is power. However, no one can gain access to a thing from a man unless he first gains access to his person.
  • Loving God
    This generation really need people who genuinely love God with all their hearts. We don’t just need Church members; we need God lovers. We don’t just need Men of God; we need lovers of God.
  • Your Point of Uniqueness
    In life, you must understand that although you don’t have everything, you definitely have something. In fact, there is something within you that the world cannot do without.
  • The Best Is Yet to Come
    As a child of God, the best of life must always answer to your destiny. Eternal life connects you to the best of Heaven. However, one of the modes of operation of the devil is to set a limit on your destiny.
  • You Must Stretch Out to Spread Out — Part 1
    Life without a bearing is a burden. You must have a burning desire if you really want to achieve something worthwhile with your life.
  • You Must Stretch Out to Spread Out — Part 2
    Don’t give up! You are advancing and exceling no matter what in Jesus Name! Yesterday, we learnt about the importance of goals in a man’s life. Today, we want to learn about the process of setting and re-defining your goals.
  • Think About the Effect of Your Actions
    One of the things you must always guard is your conscience. There are people who simply don’t have conscience - 1Ti 4:2 Speaking lies in hypocrisy; having their conscience seared with a hot iron; . Such people act carelessly and are heartless.
  • How to Access Heaven’s Blessings
    The Almighty God has a lot in store for His children. However, prompt and consistent obedience to His commands is what gives us access to Heaven’s blessings.
  • The Spirit of the Overcomer
    There is no war-free zone in life. If there is nothing to overcome how can we become overcomers and eventually emerge as more than conquerors. There is a race to run and a battle to be fought once you become a Christian.
  • You Must Always Be in Touch with Heaven
    Those who are in touch with Heaven are empowered to change their world. As a child of God, don’t just pray; learn to listen for His voice. Have you ever wondered why Jesus never made a single misstep?
  • You Must Always Act on His Instructions
    Most modern day Christians are revelation rich and application poor. Whenever you pray, you must first listen to the voice of God and after that act on the instructions you received.
  • The Way of Purity
    The most authentic source of spiritual authority is purity. Sin is a destroyer, a blocker and a sinker. It blocks the ears of God and shortens the hands of God. Reuben and Samson due to sexual immorality missed their greatness.
  • Your Attitude Is Important to Your Success — Part 1
    In life, you have no control over the actions of others but you have absolute control over your reactions. Your attitude is so important to your success in life. Every day, you choose the clothes you wear, the food you eat, and the attitude to adopt.
  • Your Attitude Is Important to Your Success — Part 2
    Your attitude is your attraction. It is the magnet that attracts or repels the right people into your life. Ruth was a servant, her humble attitude got the attention of Boaz.
  • You Must Believe to Understand
    In the journey of Christianity,you must believe, then later on you will understand. There are things going on in your life today that you don’t understand.
  • Never Stop Going to Church
    This year, you must decide to always be present in Church whenever there is a meeting. You must say like David, ‘I was glad when they said unto me, let us go into the House of the Lord- Psalm 122:1.
  • How to Produce Great Results
    The easiest way to multiply your results is to walk with God. Walking with the great invisible is what produces great and visible results. Every Christian must work for God in his or her life-time.
  • Be Hungry for the Right Knowledge
    Are you opened to new ideas? Are you ready to receive other people’s thoughts and ideas? Do you listen more than you talk? Are you willing to change your opinion based on new information? Do you readily admit when you are wrong?
  • Prayer Works Wonders — Part 1
    There are many prayerless Christians around today. They know the power and importance of prayer but they never pray. Prayerlessness is the greatest sickness of the contemporary Church. We schedule everything except our prayer time.
  • Prayer Works Wonders — Part 2
    No matter the challenge you face this year, take it to God in prayer. Our heavenly Father’s ears are always opened to hear the cry of His children on earth. However, nothing sustains intercession like revelation. Hos.14:2.
  • Prayer Works Wonders — Part 3
    If there is a man to pray, then there is surely a God to answer! He is the prayer answering God and that’s why everyone must run to Him – Psalm 65:2.
  • It’s All About Jesus!
    On the Mount of Transfiguration, Moses and Elijah appeared to Jesus. However at the end of the day only JESUS remained standing (Luke 9). Your goal in life must be to keep Jesus at the centre of all you do.
  • You Must Always Be Thankful
    Small things matter to God. We must not wait to see big things before we thank God. There is nothing too small to thank God for. There should be no difference between our thanksgiving for seemingly little things and big things.
  • The Cure for Depression
    Depression is the gateway to oppression. One of the most dangerous parties to attend is a ‘pity party’. Pity parties cause you to live in a realm of anxiety and worry.
  • Connecting to Greater Glory
    The Bible says that the glory of the latter house shall be greater than the former. God has so many wonderful plans for you but the question is, ‘Are you ready?’. Everything starts and ends with the heart.
  • Choose Rather to Obey God!
    In the journey of life, your only one desire must be to please God in all you do. Rather than struggling to impress people, strive to do what is right. One of the dangers of life is to try to confuse the applause of men with the affirmation of Heaven.
  • Supernatural Faith — Part 1
    Beloved, the Christian walk is a walk of faith. we only believe what we see with our physical eyes, then what would a blind man believe. The Christian walk is impossible without the indispensable nugget of faith.
  • Supernatural Faith — Part 2
    possible, it means it is feasible, attainable, within reach, within bounds and can be fulfilled.
  • Supernatural Faith – Faith 3
    Faith is the engine that makes the impossible to become possible. Wherever faith goes, signs and wonders follow. It is active faith in the God of possibilities that causes you to emerge out of the adversities of life with a testimony.
  • Your Case Is Different
    When you encounter God, you become a candidate for good things. When other men who do not know Your God are saying there is a casting down, you will be rising continually . Your case will simply be different.
  • The Ultimate Purpose for Man’s Existence
    What is God like? Who is God? If you were asked to describe God today, how would you respond?
  • Do You Know God as the Creator?
    The name “Elohim” describes God as the creator. In other words, our God is the creator of the world and everything that exist in it.
  • Do You Know God as the Almighty?
    There are many mighty men but there is only one Almighty God. “El-Shaddai” is another name of God which means the Almighty or the all-sufficient one.
  • Do You Know God as the Master?
    Jesus Christ is not only your Saviour, He is also your Lord.
  • Do You Know God as the Provider?
    Every true father provides for his children. Therefore God, who is our heavenly Father is always ready to provide for us His children.
  • Do You Know God as the Healer?
    There are many strange sicknesses and diseases that attack mortal men in this world That’s why as a child of God, you must have a revelation of your heavenly Father as a Healer.
  • Do You Know God as the Conqueror?
    Battles are satan’s reaction to the advancement and progress of men. In fact, the brighter your destiny, the harsher your attackers.
  • Do You Know God as the Peace Giver?
    Our God is Jehovah Shalom meaning “Our Peace.” One of the greatest assets in life is peace. You may have money, a good shelter, assorted food, academic degrees etc. but if you lack peace, life will be unbearable.
  • Do You Know God as the Sanctifier?
    The greatest limitation to God’s manifestation is a lack of sanctification (Joshua 3:5). God wants pure and holy vessels in order to manifest His power in our world today.
  • Do You Know God as the Shepherd?
    As our Father God feeds us and as a Shepherd, He leads us. “Jehovah Rohi” simply means “ the Lord my shepherd.” As a shepherd, the Lord feeds, directs, leads and cares for us. The Christian is compared to the sheep.
  • The More You Know Him, the More You Become Like Him
    Beloved, the only way to know God is by having a revelation in your spirit. This is because, God is a spirit and we can really contact Him with our spirits (John 4:24).
  • The Covering of His Glory — Part 1
    Every Christian has a glorious destiny. Your faith in God guarantees a life of unending glory.
  • The Covering of His Glory — Part 2
    Beloved, there is something about you as a Christian. Human mind can’t comprehend it; human words can’t explain it. It is God’s glory.
  • Miracle-Producing Faith — Part I
    Faith is the catapult that launches the stone of God’s Word into the destination of fulfilment.
  • Miracle-Producing Faith — Part II
    Every word of Scripture is a more sure Word of Prophecy. This means, it can’t change, it is established, it is bound to come to pass. However, what delays or eventually stops the manifestation of God’s Word in our lives is unbelief.
  • The Greatest Preparation for Your Manifestation
    If you don’t want God to stay off you, stay off sin. If you must keep God with you, then keep off sin. It is true that, in our modern world today, most people are living in sin and are living any how.
  • The Mystery of the Cross
    Welcome to another devotional encounter with the loving God who gave His own Son for the salvation of mankind. In recent times, the message of the cross has been relegated to the background in many churches.
  • The Realm of God!
    In the realm of God, there is nothing which is impossible. Men can fail, but God will always prevail. True faith does not look at the intensity/gravity of a problem, but the immensity of God’s power.
  • The Greatest Treasure in Life
    The greatest treasure in life is a GOOD NAME . When money is put on the right and a good name is placed on the left, which one would you choose? Obviously, most people will opt for the money.
  • Developing Spiritual Toughness
    Body fasting is the platform for spiritual fattening. Physical leanness is what births spiritual toughness. If you must get fatter spiritually then you must engage more in fasting. The body must “perish” for the inner man to be renewed. II Cor.
  • Add Commitment to Your Intelligence
    The best of life always demands the best of you. Commitment! It is what separates doers from dreamers; successful people from failures and great men from the mediocre. In all probabilities, commitment is superior to intelligence.
  • The Heavenly Air-Force
    You are born as a high flier, you cannot remain as a low crawler. You are part of a “heavenly air force”. You can’t enroll in an “earthly ground troop”. Every Christian is a candidate for progress.
  • How to Experience Kingdom Prosperity
    Fresh Fire for today is the best gift you can give to someone. Most Christians are struggling financially nowadays because they have refused to use simple Bible-keys.
  • How to Get Your Prayers Answered!
    Soul wining is the heart beat of God. Do you want 100% answered prayer? Do you know why some of the things you have asked God have not come or have delayed? That’s the reason for the morning’s devotion.
  • How to Achieve Extrda-Ordinary Results
    When confronted with challenges, ask less questions and engage more action. Your action terrorizes opposition and attracts divine helps.
  • Why Most People Are Not Happy
    Nobody can be truly happy when he is outside the will of God . One of the most horrible things that could happen to a person is when the person is outside where God wants him to be and he does not realize it until he gets to the grave.
  • Why Most Destinies Are Wasted
    The devil is already wasting people. In fact, the greatest demon harassing many people today is the demon of wastage. Human beings are wasted. Many things are wasted. A person could decide to waste his own life.
  • The Ultimate Purpose of Study
    The primary purpose of study is to get information but the ultimate is to press for transformation. The whole essence of knowledge is transformation. If it is not applicable, then it is not profitable.
  • Speak Right to Live Right!
    When the 10 spies were saying it was not possible for the children of Israel to possess the promised land, Caleb said, ‘ let us go up at once and possess the land, for we are well able to overcome it (Numbers 13:30)
  • Following for Success — Part 1
    Man is never created to be self-dependent; he is created to be a follower. When he ceases to follow he will certainly head for doom. A Disciple is a follower i.e. one called to follow.
  • Following for Success — Part 2
    He must always strive to catch up with the spirit of his leader. Joshua and Caleb followed Moses until they imbibe the spirit of Moses in them. Num.27:18 – Joshua/Num.14:24 – Caleb.
  • How to Increase Your Performance
    Performance in any duty or assignment is enhanced when you consider the task as a privilege. When you take any assignment for granted, you start failing at it. Every man is at his peak performance whenever it is seen as a privilege to serve.
  • Supernatural Makeover!
    One of these days, you will wake up in the robes of a king in Jesus Name! My dear brother/sister, in every pain, there is a hidden gain. The challenges of life must never cause you to give up.
  • Take Advantage of Your Adversities
    There is one major truth you must embrace in the journey of life; you can put a good man down but you can never keep him down. That is why our Lord and Master Jesus Christ could not be detained by the powers of the grave.
  • Don’t Barter Away Your Destiny
    In our world today, most people major in the minors and minor in the majors. This generation usually values the trivial and treats the important with contempt. As Isaac’s firstborn son, Esau was entitled to the birthright and inheritance.
  • You Need Good and Proper Feeding
    In life, what you eat will determine whether you grow well or not. That is why when you take in a lot of protein-based foods and you begin to put on some weight people will begin to ask you the common rhetorical question, ‘What have you been eating’?
  • The Power to Start
    Most people think that their greatest challenge is the oppositions and confrontations they are facing in their destiny. Others also think it their background that is their impediment.
  • How to Change Your Life for the Better
    The imagination of man is the limit of man. This is because you are a direct product of your thoughts; YOU EITHER RISE OR FALL BASED ON THE CONDITION OF YOUR MIND.
  • The Faith That Wins — Part 1
    Faith is the principal instrument of breakthrough in every endeavour of life. It is what beautifies destinies and adds colour to the dignity of believers. It is the critical factor that influences every man’s status.
  • The Faith That Wins — Part 2
    Faith is a winning force; it is the highest exponent in the equation of victory. The following are the attributes of the winning faith;
  • The Faith That Wins — Part 3
    Prayer is to men of faith as oxygen is to their bodies. They know that they cannot survive in this journey of life without the strength and help of God and they secure these through the medium of prayer.
  • The Faith That Wins — Part 4
    When faith stands up, every negative storm in your life goes down. The other attributes of the winning faith are;
  • The Faith That Wins — Part 5
    Faith is an overcoming force; it subdues the enemies of our destinies. Today, we want to learn the last attribute of the winning faith.
  • How to Remain at the Top
    Never care for accolades; for entitlements are not in titles. Never seek for rewards. Never be bothered about who appreciates or do not appreciate you or your works.
  • How to Experience the Glory of Tomorrow
    Beloved, the past has a great influence on the outcome of your future. Your past can affect your present destiny and imprison your future. YOUR PAST CAN BE LIKE GOALIATH AND STAND IN THE WAY OF YOUR FUTURE.
  • How to Magnetize Good Things
    The excited in life is always an attraction to many. It is only cheerful people who are able to build fruitful relationships in the journey of life. The more excited you are the more an attraction you become.
  • How to Move from Strength to Strength
    You must develop an unquenchable love for the House of God if your desire is to grow in the spirit. In life, it is your company that determines your accomplishments and your companions direct your opinions.
  • The True Value of a Man
    Work moves people forward. Those who work don’t see other peoples’ back because their work makes them second to none. The ant has no guide yet it advances. No one supervises the tiny ant yet he is able to advance its destiny into the land of plenty.
  • God-Addiction
    Today, as we end our journey of developing a closer walk with God, we want to learn how to develop a closer walk with God.
  • Experiencing the Abundant Life
    The abundant life is the heritage and the redemptive right of every child of God (John 10:10). God wants to bless us to a level that we will not have enough room to contain it.
  • The Greatest Threat to Christianity
    The greatest threat to Christianity is Christians trying to make it to Heaven without doing anything for God. In life, we are owners of nothing but stewards of everything. Stewardship a call to labour.
  • Don’t Withdraw When the Battle-Line Is Drawn
    Most people fail in life not because they lack knowledge or talent but because they quit. When problems seem insurmountable, quitting may look like the easiest way out. It is true for every marriage, ministry, job and relationship.
  • See the Future!
    It takes a cause not to faint – II Cor.4:16-17.If you don’t have a cause you are pursuing you will be wearied and faint in no time. If you are in pursuit of inner renewal, will be least bordered that the outward man is perishing.
  • How to Sweat Less in Life
    Acombination of Holy Spirit and human Spirit is what makes an excellent destiny. What man can do for himself; God will never do for him. Planning is as potent as prayer.
  • How Things Grow Worse
    Laziness is a common trait among many people especially the youth. You must however be a hardworker if you want to be a highflier in life. It is better to wear out working than to rust off idling.
  • Fruitfulness Is Your Destiny
    Fruitfulness is God’s original design for every person. Genesis 1:27-28 says, “So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.
  • Be God-Focused! — Part 1
    To grow in success in life, you must be God focused and not work or career focused. You must do everything to develop an unbreakable relationship with God if you really desire to have a lasting success.
  • Be God-Focused — Part 2
    In the journey of Christianity, many start but few are able to finish. Like any other race, if you lose focus, you will not emerge victorious at the end of the day. In the race of Christianity, our FOCUS IS JESUS CHRIST.
  • Be God-Focused! — Part 3
    Beloved, you can seek after things and miss God at the end of the day but when you seek after God, the things will follow. You must only be satisfied when you wake up in the likeness of God; all of God must be on you.
  • The Best Helping Hand
    Winston Churchill said, “Responsibility is the price for greatness”. Therefore unless you begin to take responsibility for the affairs of your life, you will die as a liability.
  • You Need Mercy!
    The altar of prayer is the altar of Grace but the gate to the altar is MERCY. You must pass through mercy to access Grace.
