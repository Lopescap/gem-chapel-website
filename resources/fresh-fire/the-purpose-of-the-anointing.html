The Purpose of the Anointing

In short: Jesus the “Anointed one” (the Messiah) in Isaiah 61:1, spoke of His Messianic task in Luke 4:18-19.

The Spirit of the Lord God is upon me, Because He has anointed Me; to preach the gospel to the poor: He has sent Me to heal the broken hearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and recovery of sight to the blind; To set at liberty those who are oppressed. To proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord.

— Luke 4:18

Jesus the “Anointed one” (the Messiah) in Isaiah 61:1, spoke of His Messianic task in Luke 4:18-19. This was not just a proper name, but a title which identified Jesus of Nazareth as the Messiah (the anointed one of God), which is interpreted in Greek as the “Christ.”

The Spirit of God upon Jesus Christ (the Anointed One) was to;

* Preach the Gospel to the poor (the Great Commission)

* Proclaim liberty to captives (deliverance Ministry)

* Heal the sick and the broken hearted (Healing Ministry)

* Tell of the acceptable year of the Lord (Prophesying)

And as children of God, He wants us to imitate Him and His works. Jesus said John 14:12 that, “if we believe in Him, the works that He did, we will also do and even do greater.”

May you do greater works for God in Jesus Name. Amen.

Prayer

Oh Lord, empower me to do mighty and greater works according to Your Word, in Jesus Name. Amen.

Confession

I do greater works!
