You Need Mercy!

In short: The altar of prayer is the altar of Grace but the gate to the altar is MERCY. You must pass through mercy to access Grace.

Blessed be God, which hath not turned away my prayer, nor his mercy from me.
— Psalm 66:20

The altar of prayer is the altar of Grace but the gate to the altar is MERCY. You must pass through mercy to access Grace. Until you understand mercy, you cannot experience grace - Heb 4:16 says, ‘ Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.’

Beloved, until God looks at you with the eyes of mercy, you can never obtain His favour. It is an attitude of humility in prayer that guarantees answers to prayer. How you prepare before God determines what you will get from Him. Luke 18:9-14 says, ‘9 ¶ And he spake this parable unto certain which trusted in themselves that they were righteous, and despised others: 10 Two men went up into the temple to pray; the one a Pharisee, and the other a publican.11 The Pharisee stood and prayed thus with himself, God, I thank thee, that I am not as other men are, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, or even as this publican. 12 I fast twice in the week, I give tithes of all that I possess. 13 And the publican, standing afar off, would not lift up so much as his eyes unto heaven, but smote upon his breast, saying, God be merciful to me a sinner. 14 I tell you, this man went down to his house justified rather than the other: for every one that exalteth himself shall be abased; and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted.’

Don’t appear as self-righteous, proud and arrogant, holier than thou and superior to others. Always appear in humility as a beneficiary of mercy in God’s righteousness. It is an attitude of humility in prayer that guarantees answers to prayers. This is because a humble person will obtain mercy from God which will in turn cause God to favour him or her.

As we the year 2021 is getting to an end, may the Mercies of God speak for you wherever you turn in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord bombard me with Your manifold mercies in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am under the canopy of the mercies of God!
