How to Multiply Your Impact

In short: To multiply your impact however, you must grow in your leadership. Leadership is to be developed no matter the vocation in which you find yourself. In life everybody leads someone at one point in time or the other.

Neither as being lords over God’s heritage, but being ensamples to the flock.

— 1 Peter 5:3

When you contact the Almighty, you must make impact. Impact is living for others to live.

To multiply your impact however, you must grow in your leadership. Leadership is to be developed no matter the vocation in which you find yourself. In life everybody leads someone at one point in time or the other. Whether you are a parent leading your children, an employer leading your employees or a pastor leading a congregation, you must decide to become an effective leader.

A leader offers direction, sensitizes people and mobilizes them to follow. Every effective leader must lead by example. This simply means they don’t simply show the way; they rather walk alongside the followers in the way. 1 Peter 5:3 says, ‘Neither as being lords over God’s heritage, but being ensamples to the flock.’

A leader teaches and raises others to take his place. He or she is a duplicator and multiplier of grace. Paul entreated Timothy the young leader in II Timothy 2: 2, ‘And the things that you have heard from me among many witnesses, commit these to faithful men who will be able to teach others also.’ For instance, King David the giant killer raised other giant killers who were known as the mighty men of David.

A leader is one who deliberately teaches others all he knows. He does not hide or hoard knowledge. He distributes freely from his bank of knowledge. He understands that the more he shares, the more it multiples. Sharing is developing capacity to accommodate more. Paul said in Acts 20: 27, “for I didn’t shrink from declaring all that God wants you to know.”- NLT

Keep developing yourself to become a leader of worth. Learn from the virtues as well as the mistakes of the leaders in the Bible and from other anointed books. Make a firm decision today to become an effective leader if you desire to multiply your effect and influence.

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to become an effective leader in Jesus Name!

Confession

My life will affect others positively!
