You Must Believe to Understand

In short: In the journey of Christianity,you must believe, then later on you will understand. There are things going on in your life today that you don’t understand.

But now, O LORD, You are our Father; We are the clay, and You our potter; And all we are the work of Your hand.

— 1 Saiah 64:8

In the journey of Christianity,you must believe, then later on you will understand. There are things going on in your life today that you don’t understand. You may be asking, ‘Why am I going through all these storms and challenges?’ Today God wants to give you an answer. Your life is like a clay in the hands of the Potter (God). He wants to make something beautiful out of your destiny but the process of doing so is painful and difficult.

Imagine a beautiful China tea cup saying: ‘I wasn’t always like this’. There was a time when I was just a cold, hard lump of clay. One day the potter picked me up and said, ‘I could do something with this.’ Then he started to put pressure on me and change my shape. I said, ‘What are you doing.? That hurts. Stop! But he said, ‘Not yet.’ Then he put me on a wheel and began to spin me around and around until I shouted, ‘Let me off.’ He replied again, ‘Not yet.’ Then he shaped me into a cup and put me in a hot oven. I cried, ‘Let me out of here, I am suffocating.’ But he looked at me and said, ‘Not yet.’ When he took me out, I thought his work was over, but then he started to paint me. I couldn’t believe what he would do next. He put me back into the oven, and I said, I can’t stand this anymore, please let me out, please let me out! But he said once again, ‘Not yet.’ Finally, he took me out of the oven and set me on a shelf where I thought he has forgotten me. Then one day, he took me off the shelf and held me up in the mirror. I couldn’t believe my eyes. I had become a beautiful teacup that everyone wants to buy.

Yes, all things are working together for your good because you are in the hands of the Potter (God). When you finally get to the place God wants you to be, you will realize that He has been making you into a vessel He can use, bless and use to bless others! So allow God to shape you. From today, when you don’t understand what you are going through, trust His Process.

Prayer

O Lord, shape me into a vessel of glory in Jesus Name!

Confession

My life is in the Hands of God!
