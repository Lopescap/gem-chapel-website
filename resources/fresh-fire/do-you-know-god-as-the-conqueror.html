Do You Know God as the Conqueror?

In short: Battles are satan’s reaction to the advancement and progress of men. In fact, the brighter your destiny, the harsher your attackers.

And Moses built an altar, and called the name of it Jehovah Nissi
— Exodus 17:15

Battles are satan’s reaction to the advancement and progress of men. In fact, the brighter your destiny, the harsher your attackers.

That is why you must know God as Jehovah Nissi which means the “Lord our Banner” or THE LORD OUR CONQUEROR signifying our victory. We serve a victorious God. Defeat and failure can never be associated with God and it should also not be associated with us. No one in life wants to be defeated in any venture. However, you cannot have true victory without the help of God. Divine assistance is the greatest catalyst of human advancement. It was said of King Uzziah in 2 Chronicles 26:15, ”... And his name spread far abroad; for he was marvellously helped, till he was strong.”

According to Exodus 17, there was a conflict between the Israelites and the Amalekites who were the descendants of Esau at Rephidim. Moses instructed Joshua to organize the Israelites for battle on the following day, promising to stand on the mountain top and petition God for the victory while Israel faced Amalek in battle. Joshua did exactly as he was told and Moses stood upon the mountain with the rod of God extended as he prayed for victory. So long as Moses lifted up his hands in prayer, Israel prevailed in the battle. When we grew tired and let down his hands however, Amalek begun to triumph. It took the help of Aaron and Hur to keep His hands up to ensure Israel’s victory in that battle.

God has promised His children victory in all the conflicts of life. The Bible says that whatsoever is born of God overcomes the world and this is the victory that overcomes the world, even our faith (1 John 5:4). The Bible also says that God will always give us victory through our Lord Jesus Christ (1 Cor. 15:57). You must however lift up your hands in prayer to God every time you get into trouble. So long as your hands are up in prayer, you will never be defeated no matter what the enemy brings your way.

Prayer

Dear Lord, make me victorious in every conflict I face in life in Jesus Name!!

Confession

All-round victory is my portion!
