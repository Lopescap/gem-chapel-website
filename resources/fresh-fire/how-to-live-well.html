How to Live Well

In short: Good and proper feeding is essential for your survival. In as much as our bodies need to feed well in order to grow, our spirits also need the Word of God in order to live and function well.

Your words were found, and I ate them, And Your word was to me the joy and rejoicing of my heart; For I am called by Your name, O LORD God of hosts.

— Jeremiah 15:16

Good and proper feeding is essential for your survival. In as much as our bodies need to feed well in order to grow, our spirits also need the Word of God in order to live and function well.

The goodness, fatness and strength of a soul are knowledge determined. That is why III John 2 says ‘Beloved, I wish above all things that you may prosper and be in health even as your soul prospers’. Your physical prosperity is therefore dependent on how well you feed your spirit.

If the spirit is not fed well, it will be lean and famish in the end. Prov.19:2 says, ‘Also it is not good for a soul to be without knowledge...’ Life is enriched by feeding it. Feeding determines weight and weight is what measures the impact one will make.

Feeding is a personal responsibility we must accept. When Jeremiah located the Word, he had to take the responsibility of eating it. Jeremiah 15: 16 says ‘Your words were found, and I ate them, And Your word was to me the joy and rejoicing of my heart; For I am called by Your name, O LORD God of hosts.’

No one can develop your spirit for you . You must make a decision to daily fill yourself with the Word of God through the process of quality meditation. The Words you hide in your heart are what you will give out in times of crisis. Therefore, feed well to live well!

Prayer

O Lord, increase my appetite for Your Word in Jesus Name!

Confession

The Word of God is my favourite food!
