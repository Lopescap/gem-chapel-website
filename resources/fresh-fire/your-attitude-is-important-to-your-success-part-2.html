Your Attitude Is Important to Your Success — Part 2

In short: Your attitude is your attraction. It is the magnet that attracts or repels the right people into your life. Ruth was a servant, her humble attitude got the attention of Boaz.

39 And one of the malefactors which were hanged railed on him, saying, If thou be Christ, save thyself and us.
— Luke 23:39

Your attitude is your attraction. It is the magnet that attracts or repels the right people into your life. Ruth was a servant, her humble attitude got the attention of Boaz. Your attitude determines what comes towards you or moves away of you - Ruth 3:1-5, 9-11

Your attitude can make your life a living hell or a wonderful Heaven on earth

Two thieves were hanging with Jesus Christ ..... One went to Heaven and one went to hell. The major determinant was attitude. One had a sober and right attitude but the other had a harsh and bad attitude - Luke 23:39-43, ‘39 And one of the malefactors which were hanged railed on him, saying, If thou be Christ, save thyself and us. 40 But the other answering rebuked him, saying, Dost not thou fear God, seeing thou art in the same condemnation? 41 And we indeed justly; for we receive the due reward of our deeds: but this man hath done nothing amiss. 42 And he said unto Jesus, Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom. 43 And Jesus said unto him, Verily I say unto thee, To day shalt thou be with me in paradise.

Your attitude can decide the entrance or exit of a miracle in your life Your attitude is affecting every relationship in your life. The woman with the issue of blood had an attitude of expectancy and she received a notable miracle. She refused to give up even after spending all she had on the best doctors - Mark 5:24-29.

Your attitude is more powerful than your gifts and talents.

Many gifted and talented people never taste the fruits of their labour because their attitudes make them undesirable to be near - Eg. Samson. May God give you a new heart and a new spirit in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord, by Your Spirit, create in me a clean heart in Jesus Name!

Confession

I operate with a positive attitude!
