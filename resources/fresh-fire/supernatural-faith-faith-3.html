Supernatural Faith – Faith 3

In short: Faith is the engine that makes the impossible to become possible. Wherever faith goes, signs and wonders follow. It is active faith in the God of possibilities that causes you to emerge out of the adversities of life with a testimony.

Jesus said unto him, If thou canst believe, all things are possible to him that believeth.
— Mark 9:23

Faith is the engine that makes the impossible to become possible. Wherever faith goes, signs and wonders follow. It is active faith in the God of possibilities that causes you to emerge out of the adversities of life with a testimony.

Now let’s carefully read, John 4:46-53, ‘46 So Jesus came again into Cana of Galilee, where he made the water wine. And there was a certain nobleman, whose son was sick at Capernaum. 47 When he heard that Jesus was come out of Judaea into Galilee, he went unto him, and besought him that he would come down, and heal his son: for he was at the point of death. 48 Then said Jesus unto him, Except ye see signs and wonders, ye will not believe. 49 The nobleman saith unto him, Sir, come down ere my child die. 50 Jesus saith unto him, Go thy way; thy son liveth. And the man believed the word that Jesus had spoken unto him, and he went his way. 51 And as he was now going down, his servants met him, and told him, saying, Thy son liveth. 52 Then enquired he of them the hour when he began to amend. And they said unto him, Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him. 53 So the father knew that it was at the same hour, in the which Jesus said unto him, Thy son liveth: and himself believed, and his whole house.

There are two major principles that stands out of this passage;

NOTHING DIES IN THE PRESENCE OF JESUS CHRIST

The noble man’s son was at the point of death but when he met Jesus Christ (the resurrection and the life), he was revived. Acts 3:15 also calls Jesus Christ THE PRINCE OF LIFE. This means that whatever is about to die, dying and dead be it your spiritual life, marriage, business etc, will jump back to life in the presence of Jesus Christ.

MIRACLES FLOW IN THE DIRECTION OF GOD’S WORD

God’s Word is as good as done. That is why, you must believe the Word of God more than any reality. Isaiah 55:10-11 says, ‘10 For as the rain cometh down, and the snow from heaven, and returneth not thither, but watereth the earth, and maketh it bring forth and bud, that it may give seed to the sower, and bread to the eater: 11 So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please, and it shall prosper in the thing whereto I sent it.’

Prayer

O Lord, begin to do the impossible in my life in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am designed for signs and wonders!
