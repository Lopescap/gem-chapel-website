The Mystery of Seeds and Harvest

In short: One major thing you must remember is that what you are doing today whether good or bad is a seed; whatever is done to you tomorrow is your harvest. Your life today is just a reflection of your decisions, choices and actions yesterday.

7 Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap.9 And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season, we shall reap, if we faint not.

— Galatians 6:7

One major thing you must remember is that what you are doing today whether good or bad is a seed; whatever is done to you tomorrow is your harvest. Your life today is just a reflection of your decisions, choices and actions yesterday. Therefore, if you don’t want the harvest you are experiencing, simply change the seeds you are sowing.

Whether it is your thoughts, actions, or behaviour, sooner or later they return and with great accuracy. Treat people with respect on your way up because you will be meeting them on your way down. Instead of a frown, give a smile; instead of pride; give humility; instead of hatred, give love; instead of retaliation, practice forgiveness; instead of discouragement, transmit hope and the list goes on.

Never allow the bad actions of others to destroy the goodness in you. Don’t allow your desire to revenge stop your blessings. Keep sowing good seeds in the journey of your life. What you make happen for others, God will definitely make happen for you. Galatians 6: 7, 9, says ‘7 Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap.9 And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season, we shall reap, if we faint not.

Always remember that your seeds today is your harvest tomorrow!

Prayer

O Lord, help me to always sow good seeds in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will never stop doing good!
