Loving God

In short: This generation really need people who genuinely love God with all their hearts. We don’t just need Church members; we need God lovers. We don’t just need Men of God; we need lovers of God.

But as it is written: Eye has not seen, nor ear heard, Nor have entered into the heart of man The things which God has prepared for those who love Him.
— 1 Corinthians 2:9

This generation really need people who genuinely love God with all their hearts. We don’t just need Church members; we need God lovers. We don’t just need Men of God; we need lovers of God.

Church members and Men of God are not scarce to find but lovers of God are few and not easy to come by. When God is looking for a man to enthrone and promote, he always looks for a person with a heart for Him. That is why, when God wanted a king to replace King Saul, he settled on David a man after His heart – Ac 13:22 And when he had removed him, he raised up unto them David to be their king; to whom also he gave testimony, and said, I have found David the son of Jesse, a man after mine own heart, which shall fulfil all my will.

Love for God will give you exclusive access to God and His secrets- I Corinthians 2:9. Your passion for God will determine your portion from God. Your passion for God will determine your place with God. Your passion moves the might and strength of God in your direction - 2Ch 16:9 For the eyes of the LORD run to and fro throughout the whole earth, to shew himself strong in the behalf of them whose heart is perfect toward him.

Without a heart for God, you can never make a mark on earth. Therefore, in this New Year, always cry for the love of God to be released into your heart by the Holy Spirit (Rom. 5:5). Let nothing separate you from the love of God; simply value God above all else. You will surely see what no eyes have seen and hear what no ears have heard when you become a lover of God!

Prayer

O Lord, give me a heart for You in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will never stop loving God!

  • Romans
