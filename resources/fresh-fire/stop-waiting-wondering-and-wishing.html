Stop Waiting, Wondering and Wishing

In short: Most people have never learnt to take responsibility for their lives. When such people fail, they blame it on everyone apart from themselves. However, those who normally excel in the journey of life always take responsibility for their lives.

But wilt thou know, O vain man, that faith without works is dead?

— James 2:20

Most people have never learnt to take responsibility for their lives. When such people fail, they blame it on everyone apart from themselves. However, those who normally excel in the journey of life always take responsibility for their lives. They know that what man can do, God will never do for them. Such people prepare, plan and take action rather than wait, wonder and wish to accomplish their goals in life.

A flood was threatening a small town and everyone was leaving for safe ground except for one man who said, “God will save me. I have faith.” As the water level rose, a jeep came to rescue him, the man refused, saying God will save me, I have faith.”. As the water level rose further, he went up to the second storey of his house, and a boat came to help him. Again he refused to go saying God will save me. I have faith. The water kept rising and the man climbed on to the roof. A helicopter came to rescue him, but he said, God will save me. I have faith. Well, finally he drowned. When he reached his Maker he angrily questioned, ‘I had complete faith in You. Why did you ignore my prayers and let me down?’ The Lord replied, “Who do you think sent you the jeep, the boat and the helicopter?”

Beloved, your faith must always be coupled with works. Faith is not just a fact but most importantly an act. James 2:20 says, ‘But wilt thou know, O vain man, that faith without works is dead?’ When the prodigal son took steps, he landed in the welcoming arms of his father. As you also take steps from today, you will land in the arms of your miracle in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord, help me to always act on my faith in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am determined to take steps of faith!
