The Spirit of the Overcomer

In short: There is no war-free zone in life. If there is nothing to overcome how can we become overcomers and eventually emerge as more than conquerors. There is a race to run and a battle to be fought once you become a Christian.

I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith:
— 2 Timothy 4:7

There is no war-free zone in life. If there is nothing to overcome how can we become overcomers and eventually emerge as more than conquerors. There is a race to run and a battle to be fought once you become a Christian. The devil who walks about as a roaring line seeking someone to devour will try to put you down and cause you to stay down. You must however get up and run again! Proverbs 24:16 says, ‘For a righteous man may fall seven times And rise again, But the wicked shall fall by calamity.’

Victory belongs to those who keep looking at the goal, not the going; not the process but the prize, not the trial but the treasure that’s promised to those who persevere!

A two-fold theme of Christianity is RISING AGAIN AND GETTING BACK INTO THE RACE! But you can’t win if you don’t run and risk falling. No matter what caused you to fall, the word for you today is, TAKE GOD’S HAND, GET UP & RUN! In the mighty Name of Jesus, I declare over you that you will fight the good fight, you shall finish your course and you shall keep the faith! The Spirit of the overcomer is upon you.

Prayer

O Lord, fortify me with the spirit of endurance in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am winning again!
