You Must Stretch Out to Spread Out — Part 1

In short: Life without a bearing is a burden. You must have a burning desire if you really want to achieve something worthwhile with your life.

But I have a baptism to be baptized with, and how distressed I am till it is accomplished
— Luke 12:50, 50

Life without a bearing is a burden. You must have a burning desire if you really want to achieve something worthwhile with your life.

In life, only goal setters will ever become goal getters. Your goals determine where you reach in life. What you set before your eyes is what you will get with your hands. If you stand for nothing; you will fall for anything.

Goals motivate you; stretches you; releases your potential and compels action. Luke 12:50. A man without a goal will end as a goat. A goat is an animal without aim, objective and purpose and as a result is vulnerable to all kinds of danger and assault. There is no animal that suffers assault more than a goat. In the same way, there is no man that suffers assault like a goal-less man. To be goal-less is to guideless and to be guideless is to run into trouble without knowing. If you lack clear guidance, you will lack defense.

One must therefore always keep defining and redefining his goals in the journey of life. The more you re-define your goal, the clearer, sharper, brighter and finer it becomes. Re-defining your goal brings it closer to your eyes and your heart.

If you don’t make clear definitions, you will suffer many deviations and with deviations begins the journey of detention, devastation, destruction and frustration.

From today , re-define your goal in life if you desire to experience a change of level.

Prayer

O Lord, wherever I am out of your will for my life, re-direct my steps in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will never be diverted from my God-given destiny!
