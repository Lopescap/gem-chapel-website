How to Achieve All Your Dreams

In short: The Bible says in Daniel 11:32 that “... the people that do know their God shall be strong and do exploits”. It means that by our association with divinity, our abilities must take us to a realm of unending exploits.

So built we the wall; and all the wall was joined together unto the half thereof: for the people had a mind to work.

— Nehemiah 4:6

The Bible says in Daniel 11:32 that “... the people that do know their God shall be strong and do exploits”. It means that by our association with divinity, our abilities must take us to a realm of unending exploits. Greatness must be our hallmark and trademark. However, what you must understand is that it takes a willing heart to produce a skillful hand. Willingness and performance are closely intertwined; you can’t separate one from the other. Our competence begins with our willingness. Willingness is the fountain of excellence and the source of all exploits. Success always awaits the willing hearted. That is why it is popularly said, “where there is a will’ there is a way.” A willing man is never clueless and stranded in the journey of life. A willing man can achieve all his dreams. This is because, his willingness can turn the better into sweet, shame to glory, breakdown into breakthroughs, obstacles into miracles and stopping stones into stepping stones”

Just anything is achievable by the hand of a willing man. Nehemiah was just a cup bearer but with a willingness of mind, he led a revolution that built the walls of Jerusalem in a record time of 52 days. It was not just Nehemiah who was willing, the virus of willingness spread like wildfire among the Jews and this accelerated the work greatly. Nehemiah 4:6 says, “So built we the wall; and all the wall was joined together unto the half thereof: for the people had a mind to work.” It is true that you are destined for the top as a child of God but this must be coupled with your willingness to succeed. Ecclesiastes 9:10 says, “Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest.”

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to do things with a willing heart in Jesus Name!

Confession

I shall achieve all my dreams!
