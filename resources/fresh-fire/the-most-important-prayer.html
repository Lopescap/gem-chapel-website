The Most Important Prayer

In short: Friend, the most dangerous place to be is to be outside the will of God for your life. It is only God who knows the ending from the beginning. Our parents got to know us after we were born, but God knew us even before we were born.

Teach me to do Thy will; for Thou art my God: Thy Spirit is good; lead me into the land of uprightness.

— Psalm 143:10

Friend, the most dangerous place to be is to be outside the will of God for your life. It is only God who knows the ending from the beginning. Our parents got to know us after we were born, but God knew us even before we were born. God is the Architect of our lives; He is the Master - designer of our destines. He holds the blue-print to our lives.

That is why Jeremiah 10:23 says, “.....It is not in man to direct his own steps” but the steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord (Psalm 37:23). That is why Jesus even in the Garden of Gethsemane wanted nothing but for the perfect will of God to be done. (Matt. 26:42)

Being in the centre of God’s will for your life is the greatest and the most important prayer.

Being in the centre of God’s will for your life is what makes you a centre of attraction in your generation. Being in the centre of God’s will is your security in the journey of life. The price for being in the centre of God’s will cannot be compared with the loss of being outside of His will.

That is why David prayed desperately in Psalm 40:8, “I delight to Thy will, O my God: yea, Thy law is within my heart.” Many Christians normally pray, “Lord bless what I am doing, but you must rather pray: Lord, let me do what You have blessed.” This prayer places you right the centre of God’s will.

Prayer

O Lord, place me at the centre of Your will for my life in Jesus Name. Amen!

Confession

My joy is to do God’s will!
