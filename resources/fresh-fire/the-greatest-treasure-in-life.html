The Greatest Treasure in Life

In short: The greatest treasure in life is a GOOD NAME . When money is put on the right and a good name is placed on the left, which one would you choose? Obviously, most people will opt for the money.

A good name is rather to be chosen than great riches and loving favour rather than silver and gold
— Proverbs 22:1

The greatest treasure in life is a GOOD NAME . When money is put on the right and a good name is placed on the left, which one would you choose? Obviously, most people will opt for the money. However, although money is good, it is never the best inheritance to leave behind for your children. Money or great riches may bring you fame but can as well lead into flame. It is not so with name; a good name will bring you grace that will take away your shame. A good name will attract to you great favour.

You may loose money and recover the same and even more but if you lose your name, you may never recover it. That is why you must do everything to protect your name. Even Jesus Christ understood and endorsed this assertion in his own ife. After staying with the disciples for three and a half years, He did not leave them money or other material possessions. Jesus left them a NAME. He told them that if they acted in His Name, they will see wonders and miracles (Mark 16:15-20). God the Father also rewarded Jesus Christ with a NAME that is ABOVE EVERY NAME (Phil. 2:9-10)..

Always strive to get a good name. I Chronicles 11: 20 says, “And Abishai the brother of Joab, he was chief of the three: for lifting up his spear against three hundred, he slew them, and had a NAME among the three.” Like Abishai, begin to slay (do away with) any negative character in order for you to have a good name in your generation. Protect your name!

Prayer

O Lord, help me to deal with any behavior that is destroying my name in Jesus Name!

Confession

I have a good name!
