A Day Without Prayer

In short: Prayer is not what we choose to do but what we must do; it is not what we like to do but what we are mandated to do.

And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint

— Luke 18:1

Prayer is not what we choose to do but what we must do; it is not what we like to do but what we are mandated to do. If you are not praying you will be fainting and loosing breath in the journey of life because by strength (physical abilities) shall no man prevail (I Samuel 2:9)

All great men in Scripture were prayerful. Prayer is cure to faint heartedness. Prayerful people are therefore bold people. In prayer, you stand before God and if you can stand before God then you can stand before any man.

There is no alternative to prayer no matter how colourful such appears to be. Jesus prayed always and never fainted once. The difficulties of life did not stop Jesus from praying. It was during such times that He even prayed more seriously. Luke 22:44 says, ‘ And being in an agony he prayed more earnestly: and his sweat was as it were great drops of blood falling down to the ground.’

Peter and others escaped fainting because they prayed always. Acts.6:4. Paul advocated that the believer must pray always and without ceasing. 1 Thes.5:17; Eph.6:16

Prayer is the believer’s spiritual respiratory organ. A day without prayer is a risky day.

If you are not praying, you will be choking, panting, fainting and failing – a church that prays always enjoy ventilation. Nothing dissolves crisis in a church like prayer. Satan attempted to stop the growth of the first church in Acts chapter 5 but the church stopped him by renewing their commitment to prayer in Act 6:4. Because we are tempted and attacked always, we must pray always.

That is the only guarantee for our victory always!

Prayer

O Lord, pour upon me the spirit of prayer in Jesus Name!

Confession

I pray without ceasing!
