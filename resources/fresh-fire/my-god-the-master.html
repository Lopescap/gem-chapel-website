My God, the Master

In short: Jesus Christ is not only your Saviour, He is also your Lord.

And said, My Lord, if now I have found favour in thy sight, pass not away, I pray thee, from thy servant

— Genesis 18:3

As a child of God, you must have a true revelation of God as your Master.

Jesus Christ is not only your Saviour, He is also your Lord. When the Apostle Peter was delivering his first sermon in Acts 3:36, he said, “Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly, that God hath made the same Jesus whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ.”

As Abraham was sitting in the doorway of his tent at noontime with the sun beating down upon him, he saw three men approaching. Bowing himself to the ground, Abraham called out, “My Lord” (meaning Master or wholly thine) – Gen. 18:1-3. In other words, if God becomes your master, it means you are the property of God. You are always at his service. Everything that concerns God, concerns you and because of that, everything that concerns you also concerns God.

In the olden days, servants or slaves were willing to die for their masters. So if God is truly your Lord, you’ll be willing to die for Him. That was why Shadrack, Meshack and Abednago were not afraid of the death threats of King Nebuchadnezzar; God was their Master. They decided to rather burn than turn from the commandments of their Master. It is the Master (Lord) who dictates everything for the servant. The word of the Master is obeyed by the servant.

Beloved, is God your Master? Do you serve Him at all times? Do you obey Him no matter the cost? Are you fulfilling the Great Commission? Are you totally surrendered to Him? If not let Him become your “Adonai” today. God only reveals His will to those who know Him as Adonai (Gen. 18:17).

Prayer

Father, Lord, I want you to become the Lord and Master over my life from today in Jesus Name. Amen!!

Confession

I have only one Master and Lord; the Almighty God!
