The Best Helping Hand

In short: Winston Churchill said, “Responsibility is the price for greatness”. Therefore unless you begin to take responsibility for the affairs of your life, you will die as a liability.

Wherefore I put thee in remembrance that thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee by the putting on of my hands.
— 2 Timothy 1:6

Winston Churchill said, “Responsibility is the price for greatness”. Therefore unless you begin to take responsibility for the affairs of your life, you will die as a liability.

Don’t expect anybody to live your life for you; that is irresponsibility. Somebody may start you up but no one can keep you on. For instance; there are some children who always want to depend on their parents even during their adulthood.

You may be sent forth but you have to go forth. You may be imparted with grace but no one can do the going for you .Accept responsibility and begin to stir up the gift deposited into you. Although the Apostle Paul imparted a lot of gifts into Timothy through the laying on of his hands on him, he encouraged him to make good use of what he had received. That’s responsibility. II Timothy 1:6 says, ‘Wherefore I put thee in remembrance that thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee by the putting on of my hands.’

Someone may hold your hands when you are taking the first step but you have to take the subsequent steps on your own. Elisha got the prophetic power mantle from Elijah but he had to strike the water by himself. The arm is only support to the hand. It cannot and never take the place of the hand. Your hand remains the best helping hand.

Accept responsibility to advance your destiny. Stop blaming others for your failures and inadequacies. In spite of your family background, other family members are making it. In spite of the poor government policies in your country; other citizens are making it. The question is “Why are you not the one making it?” The answer is simple; you are simply refusing to take responsibility for the happenings of your life.

Prayer

O Lord, help me to live a responsible life in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am living a responsible life!
