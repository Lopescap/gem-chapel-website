Do You Know God as the Shepherd?

In short: As our Father God feeds us and as a Shepherd, He leads us. “Jehovah Rohi” simply means “ the Lord my shepherd.” As a shepherd, the Lord feeds, directs, leads and cares for us. The Christian is compared to the sheep.

The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want.

— Psalm 23:1

As our Father God feeds us and as a Shepherd, He leads us. “Jehovah Rohi” simply means “ the Lord my shepherd.” As a shepherd, the Lord feeds, directs, leads and cares for us. The Christian is compared to the sheep. Naturally, sheep are weak, defenceless and foolish. Without a good shepherd therefore, sheep are vulnerable to all sorts of dangers. They can get lost easily. While they are grazing in the pasture, some can wander away from the flock. That is why the Bible says that “All we like sheep have gone astray; we have turned everyone to his own way;” (Isaiah 53:6).

When sheep go astray, it takes a loving and a caring shepherd to go after the one that was lost and bring it back into the fold. In the same way, we were once lost and it took the loving and caring shepherd of our souls (Jesus Christ to bring us back to the fold of God. Again, sheep can experience severe hunger especially when there is no pasture. Such severe hunger can cause death among them. A good shepherd therefore will always lead the sheep to a green pasture so that they don’t starve to death.

In our Christian walk too, spiritual hunger occurs when the Word of God is lacking and we are finding it very difficult to participate in other godly activities. The Bible makes this statement about the Good Shepherd, ‘He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: He leads me beside the still waters” (Psalm 23:2). Finally, Jesus Christ, our Good Shepherd also protects us from all evil and delivers our souls from the hands of the enemy.

It is my prayer for you today, that God will always be your Shepherd. May all-round protection be your portion in Jesus Name as you know God as a Shepherd.

Prayer

O Lord, lead me in this journey of life in Jesus Name!!

Confession

My steps are ordered by the Lord!
