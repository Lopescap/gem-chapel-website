Your Case Is Different

In short: When you encounter God, you become a candidate for good things. When other men who do not know Your God are saying there is a casting down, you will be rising continually . Your case will simply be different.

And Jacob called the name of the place Peniel: for I have seen God face to face, and my life is preserved.
— Genesis 32:30

When you encounter God, you become a candidate for good things. When other men who do not know Your God are saying there is a casting down, you will be rising continually . Your case will simply be different.

Whenever, you meet God, your life is preserved - Ge 32:30 And Jacob called the name of the place Peniel: for I have seen God face to face, and my life is preserved. You are preserved from the heat of the day.

As a Christian, you must be sure of one thing that, what kills others cannot kill you;

The same fire that killed the ones who placed the three Hebrew boys into the fiery furnace could not kill Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego (Dan. 3)

The lions that ate up the enemies of Daniel could not eat him up (Dan. 6:1-19)

James died by the sword of Herod but Peter escaped the same sword (Acts 5:1-12)

Paul could not be killed by the venomous viper. Where there is fire, there is no need to fear the viper because the viper cannot survive the fire (Acts 28:1-6)

God will preserve you from premature death; nobody connected to you will die in an untimely fashion in Jesus Name! - Ps 79:11 Let the sighing of the prisoner come before thee; according to the greatness of thy power preserve thou those that are appointed to die;

SAY BOLDLY TO YOURSELF, ‘MY CASE IS DIFFERENT”

Prayer

O Lord, preserve m by Your mighty power in Jesus Name!

Confession

My case is different!
