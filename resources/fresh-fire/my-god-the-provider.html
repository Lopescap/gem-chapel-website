My God, the Provider!

In short: Every true father provides for his children. Therefore God, who is our heavenly Father is always ready to provide for us His children.

And Abraham said, My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering: so they went both of them together.

— Genesis 22:8

Every true father provides for his children. Therefore God, who is our heavenly Father is always ready to provide for us His children. Our God is Jehovah Jireh which suggests two meanings: “Jehovah shall be seen” and “Provider.” This name therefore speaks about redemption.

Ωbirth of Isaac. I want you to put yourself in the position of Abraham. Imagine the joy and excitement that Abraham experienced as he watched his son grow. Then one day God demanded for the only son of old man Abraham ((Gen. 22:2). Many modern-day people would have disobeyed this command but without hesitation, Abraham took Isaac and they embarked on a three-day journey to Mount Mariah. It was certainly a journey of no return for Isaac. As they walked along Isaac said, “Father I see the fire and the wood, but where is the lamb for the sacrifice? Abraham was so confident in his God that he knew the he could provide even at the last moment and in the most difficult of times. True to his faith, God provided a ram on top of a mountain.

Listen to me today, no matter what is lacking in your life, God can provide. When man ends, God begins . When man says it is over, God takes over and when God takes over, the battle is over. If God is truly your shepherd, you will never want any good thing (Psalm 23:1). Phil. 4:19 says that your God will supply all your needs...” The economic situation notwithstanding, God will always provide for you and your family. May Heaven surprise you today in Jesus Name! God will connect you to good things and open great doors for you. From today, goodness and mercy shall follow you all the days of your life.

Prayer

O Lord, let me experience supernatural provision in all I do in Jesus Name! Amen.

Confession

I will never lack any good thing!
