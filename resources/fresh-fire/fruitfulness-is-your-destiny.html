Fruitfulness Is Your Destiny

In short: Fruitfulness is God’s original design for every person. Genesis 1:27-28 says, “So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.

My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt be no priest to me: seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I will also forget thy children.

— Hosea 4:6

Fruitfulness is God’s original design for every person. Genesis 1:27-28 says, “So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them. And God blessed them, and God said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth, and subdue it: and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over every living thing that moveth upon the earth.”

You are a product of God and he has His signature on you (which is fruitfulness). Therefore by God’s design, your fruitfulness is non- negotiable, it is necessity - It is in your DNA.The ability to be fruitful has been placed inside you but the actualization of your fruitfulness is based on your knowledge - Hos.4:6. Your ability to believe in the manufacturer is based on the capacity of knowledge you have acquired from your “product manual” = The Bible.

Knowledge gives birth to faith. Romans 10:17 says, “So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.” Faith gives birth to results. That is why I John 5:4 also says, “For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith.”

Ignorance is an unaffordable luxury. One great man said, “There is no mountain anywhere; every man’s mountain is his own ignorance. Do you desire victory in any area of your life? Do you want to be fruitful? Do you want your reproach to come to an end? If your answers to the above questions is Yes; then begin to seek knowledge from God’s Word and other anointed and impactful books from today. You will be fruitful in Jesus name!

Prayer

O Lord, increase my appetite for the right knowledge in Jesus name!

Confession

I am fruitful in all I do!
