Do You Know God as the Peace Giver?

In short: Our God is Jehovah Shalom meaning “Our Peace.” One of the greatest assets in life is peace. You may have money, a good shelter, assorted food, academic degrees etc. but if you lack peace, life will be unbearable.

Then Gideon built an altar there unto the Lord, and called it Jehovah-Shalom
— Judges 6:24

Our God is Jehovah Shalom meaning “Our Peace.” One of the greatest assets in life is peace. You may have money, a good shelter, assorted food, academic degrees etc. but if you lack peace, life will be unbearable. That is why I love this scripture in Psalm 29:11, “ The Lord will give strength unto his people; the Lord will bless his people with peace.” Peace is one blessing you should always pray for as a Christian.

How did God get this name? After Israel’s miraculous liberation from Egypt, they soon forgot the Lord. When you forget God, you will also be under oppression. God allowed the Midianites to oppress the Israelites. In the midst of this excruciating torment, the Israelites prayed unto the Lord. God in His own mercy appeared unto Gideon and commanded him to lead the Israelites into battle against the Midianites. Unaware of whom he was in God, the words Gideon spoke revealed the unbelief in his heart when he said, “If I have found grace in thy sight, then show me a sign” (Judges 6:17). When God revealed himself as “Jehovah Shalom” to Gideon, fear and unbelief vanished. Shortly after this experience, Gideon led 300 men to a supernatural victory over the Midianites.

You can also enjoy the peace of God; for He will keep you in perfect peace if your mind is stayed on Him (Isaiah. 26:3). I speak peace into your life and family; I declare peace into your marriage and in your business in Jesus Name!

Prayer

O Lord, let your peace which surpasses all understanding overshadow my life in Jesus Name. Amen!!

Confession

I walk under the canopy of God’s peace!
