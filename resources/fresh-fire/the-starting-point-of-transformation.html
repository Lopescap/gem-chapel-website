The Starting Point of Transformation

In short: Every Samson who’s laid with a Delilah, every David who committed adultery with a Bathsheba and later betrayed his Uriah, every Judas who sold out his Jesus began his downfall with a THOUGHT.

21 For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders, 22 Thefts, covetousness, wickedness, deceit, lasciviousness, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, foolishness: 23 All these evil things come from within, and defile the man.

— Mark 7:21-23

Every Samson who’s laid with a Delilah, every David who committed adultery with a Bathsheba and later betrayed his Uriah, every Judas who sold out his Jesus began his downfall with a THOUGHT. Your thought is the exact measure of your life; no one gets better than his or her THOUGHT LIFE - Proverbs 23:7 says, “...as he thinketh in his heart, so is he”

Purity is demonstrated in the heart’s most sacred thoughts –Mark 7:21-23. When an impure thought is not brought to captivity, it breeds evil imaginations, which breed carnal attitudes that make us vulnerable to temptation – II Cor. 10:5. Never expect to behave differently from the way you think. Your thoughts create a pattern for your character. Your thought is the seed of your life. Therefore, think right to live right.

One wise man said, ‘Sow a thought, reap an act; sow an act, reap a habit; sow a habit, reap a character; sow a character, reap a destiny. From today, decide to align your thoughts with GOD’S WORD! The RENEWAL OF YOUR MIND is the starting point of your TRANSFORMATION –

Romans 12:2 says, ‘And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.’

Prayer

O Lord, transform my mind by Your Spirit in Jesus Name!

Confession

I always think right!
