How to Put a Smile on the Face of God

In short: May the love of God that drove Christ to the cross now drive us to the lost. If soul-winning is not your priority then you are out of touch with Heaven.

And when they found them not, they drew Jason and certain brethren unto the rulers of the city, crying, THESE THAT HAVE TURNED THE WORLD UPSIDE DOWN ARE COME HITHER ALSO “.

— Acts 17:6

May the love of God that drove Christ to the cross now drive us to the lost. If soul-winning is not your priority then you are out of touch with Heaven. This is because what causes Heaven to jubilate and puts a smile on the face of God is when souls come to the glorious knowledge of the Gospel. God is looking for a generation of soul-winners who would rescue this generation of sinners from the greedy mouth of yawning hell. He wants people who will empty hell and fill Heaven; who will make hell sad and the devil mad but make Heaven glad. Every member of the Church must be an evangelism addict.

Consider Paul with a powerful anointing of the Holy Spirit upon him, he went out to ransack Asia Minor, preaching through its markets, stirring its synagogues, penetrating its palaces. Out he went, with the war cry of the Gospel in his heart and on his lips. This generation of Christians are too silent says the Lord. We talk about everything ranging from football, politics, Marriage issues, education, to recreation, but not the Gospel.

Our spirits are not stirred at all. Acts 17:16 says, “Now while Paul waited for them at Athens, his spirit was STIRRED in him, when he saw the CITY WHOLLY GIVEN TO IDOLATRY” . Oh may your spirit be stirred as you are reading this material. May your spirit be stirred when you see your city and nation totally given to fornication, alcoholism, lesbianism, homosexuality, corruption, pornography, lies, etc.

Preach the word in season and out of season from today to bring a change your home school, Job, family and relationships. This is how we change the world!

Prayer

O Lord, make me an incurable soul-winner!

Confession

I am always on the move for God!
