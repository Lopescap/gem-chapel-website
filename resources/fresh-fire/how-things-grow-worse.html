How Things Grow Worse

In short: Laziness is a common trait among many people especially the youth. You must however be a hardworker if you want to be a highflier in life. It is better to wear out working than to rust off idling.

By much slothfulness the building decayeth; and through idleness of the hands the house droppeth through.
— Ecclesiastes 10:18

Laziness is a common trait among many people especially the youth. You must however be a hardworker if you want to be a highflier in life. It is better to wear out working than to rust off idling. Idleness leads to rust, doing nothing is the easiest way to decay. In fact, everything grows worse without work

God commands us to work with our hands and to be diligent workers.

1Th 4:11 And that ye study to be quiet, and to do your own business, and to work with your own hands, as we commanded you;

Eph 4:28 Let him that stole steal no more: but rather let him labour, working with his hands the thing which is good, that he may have to give to him that needeth

Proverbs 22:29 ¶ Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.

Working simply means releasing energy to produce a desired change. Time will remain effectively useful only as long as it is in motion. So don’t stop working if you don’t want to waste off. Abraham was a giver, a tither and a worker and at the end of the day God blessed him in all things (Gen. 24:1). The prosperity covenant contains details; if you don’t mind the details you will be detained on the same spot and you will move in circles in life. Stop idling and start working because God has promised to bless all the work of your hands. From today, WHATSOEVER YOU DO SHALL PROSPER IN JESUS NAME!

Prayer

O Lord, deliver me from the spiri of laziness in Jesus Name!

Confession

I hate idleness!
