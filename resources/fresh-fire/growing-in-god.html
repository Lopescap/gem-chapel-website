Growing in God

In short: You cannot help studying God’s Word without concluding that Relationships are a high priority with God. He cares about His creatures! Love permeates His very Nature and every aspect of His Person.

And this is eternal life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom You have sent.

— John 17:3

You cannot help studying God’s Word without concluding that Relationships are a high priority with God. He cares about His creatures! Love permeates His very Nature and every aspect of His Person. The Christian is one in whom God abides and is manifested. A major part of manifesting God in our lives is the expression of God’s Love in our relationships with others. John 13:35, “By this all will know that you are My disciples, if you have love for one another.”

Every man must develop a relationship with God. To build this kind of strong relationship with God, you must do the following;

Know God

John 17:3, “And this is eternal life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom You have sent.’ Love God

Matthew 22:37-38, “ 37 Jesus said to him,” ‘You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.’ 38 “This is the first and great commandment.” Serve God

Luke 4: 8, ‘ And Jesus answered and said to him, “Get behind Me, Satan! For it is written, ‘You shall worship the LORD your God, and Him only you shall serve.’

“When you come to KNOW God, you will LOVE Him. And when you come to Love Him, you will want to SERVE Him. If you don’t want to Serve God, maybe it is because you don’t really Love Him. And if you don’t Love God, maybe it is because you don’t really Know Him”

Prayer

O Lord, help me to know you more and more in Jesus Name!

Confession

I have a strong relationship with God!
