The Proof of Greatness

In short: When God called Abraham, he was a nobody but by the time God was through with him, greatness had become the signature of his life.

And Abram went up out of Egypt, he, and his wife, and all that he had, and Lot with him, into the south. And Abram was very rich in cattle, in silver, and in gold. And Lot also, which went with Abram, had flocks, and herds, and tents. And Lot also, which went with Abram, had flocks, and herds, and tents

— Genesis 13:1-2

When God called Abraham, he was a nobody but by the time God was through with him, greatness had become the signature of his life.

Abraham’s greatness was expressed and interpreted in Lot’s greatness. Gen.13:1-5. Abraham was not the only one who became great; he also made sure that his nephew Lot also became great. Don’t put everyone down in order for you alone to be at the top. No! To prove your greatness, make others great. To prove your success, make others succeed. This is the ultimate prove of success.

If you don’t want to be erased raise others. If you don’t learn to raise others you will soon erase yourself. Doesn’t just be a container of blessings but also a channel of those same blessings. Never end as a possessor of wealth but also a distributor of wealth. Real greatness is hidden in the system of making other people great. That is impact! From today, may you receive the Grace to carry others along on the journey of your success in Jesus Name!! Always be there to give a helping hand to the vulnerable.

The world measures by how materially rich a person is and the bank balance of a person. However, in the Kingdom of God, a truly great person seeks always, the success of others. He seeks to make others great as himself and even more. So, if no one around you is experiencing greatness then you are not a great person.

Prayer

O Lord, break any trace of self-centeredness in me in Jesus Name!!

Confession

I always help others to succeed!
