The Road to Victory Over Sin!

In short: Sin is a destroyer and sin is a sinker! Sin gives the devil an upper hand in your life. You must know that due to the Adamic nature, all human beings possess, it is easier for people to sin than to do what is right.

And almost all things are by the law purged with the BLOOD; and without the shedding of blood is no remission”.

— Hebrews 9:22

Sin is a destroyer and sin is a sinker! Sin gives the devil an upper hand in your life. You must know that due to the Adamic nature, all human beings possess, it is easier for people to sin than to do what is right. David said in Psalm 51:5, “Behold, I was shapen in iniquity; and in sin did my mother conceive me.” Paul the Apostle also said in Roman 7:19, “For the good that I would I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I do.”

Without the Blood, man’s struggle against sin will not yield any results. Charles Spurgeon once said, “Trust Christ and you shall live. The bloody sacrifice of Calvary is the only hope of sinners”. The Blood of Jesus is a “Master Eraser”. It can erase your sinful past and give you a glorious future today. This Blood will not only deliver you from sin but also empower you to live above sin. Sin chased man out of Eden’s perfection and fruitfulness, and only righteousness can bring him back. God desires to bring us back to Eden if we will let Him cleanse us from all iniquities.

Every sin that is not dealt with has power to deal with the sinner. Stop toying with sin otherwise, you will become a toy in the hands of the devil. There are things you must stop today or you will never have another opportunity to stop them. Stop that sin before it stops you from enjoying the best that God has for you. Is it fornication, masturbation, alcoholism, pornography, pride, anger, theft, unforgiveness, abortion etc? The Blood is powerful enough to cleanse you from every sin. (1 John 1:7). You can win the battle against sin through the Blood of Jesus.

Prayer

O Lord, let the Blood of Jesus cleanse me from all my sins in Jesus Name.

Confession

My Jesus is Lord over death, sin and the grave!
