An Encounter with the Living Christ

In short: The Apostle Paul declared out of holy hunger and desperation in Philippians 3: 10 That I may know him, and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings, being made conformable unto his death;

To whom also he shewed himself alive after his passion by many infallible proofs, being seen of them forty days, and speaking of the things pertaining to the kingdom of God:

— Acts 1:3

The Apostle Paul declared out of holy hunger and desperation in Philippians 3: 10 That I may know him, and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings, being made conformable unto his death;

Like Paul, we must have an insatiable desire to know Jesus more and more. This is because, when you encounter the resurrected Christ, you will experience the following;

Your eyes will be opened to understand the Scriptures –

Luke 24:16-31, 45

You will discover God’s plan and purpose for your life –

John 21:5-6

You will experience notable miracles and breakthroughs –

John 21:5-6

You will discover the deeper things of the Kingdom – Acts

1:3

The storm in your life will be calmed – John 20:21

Sigs and wonders will be unleashed – Acts 1:3

7. Jesus Christ will breathe on you, the breath of life that

quickens our mortal bodies –John 20:22, Rom. 8:11

You must begin to seek the face of God in this season for a genuine encounter with the resurrected Christ!

O Lord, release Your Power into my life in Jesus Name!
