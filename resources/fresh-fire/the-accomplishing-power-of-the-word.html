The Accomplishing Power of the Word

In short: Iwant you to develop a new approach to God’s Word. If you don’t change your negative approach to God’s Word, you will surely end as a reproach. The Bible says that God is not a man that He should lie (Numb. 3:19).

Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done, saying, My counsel shall stand, and I will do all my pleasure. Calling a revenue bird from the east, the man that executeth my counsel from a far county: yea I HAVE PURPOSED IT, I WILL ALSO DO IT.

— Isaiah 46:10-11

Iwant you to develop a new approach to God’s Word. If you don’t change your negative approach to God’s Word, you will surely end as a reproach. The Bible says that God is not a man that He should lie (Numb. 3:19). Again, the Bible says it is IMPOSSIBLE for God to lie (Heb. 6:18). God is therefore too faithful to fail.

In our world today, everything around us has failed. Governments have failed their nations, parents are failing children and vice- versa, the economic figures are not reflecting in the life of the ordinary person etc. That is why your faith and confidence must shift to God’s Word. When all else fail, the Word will always prevail.

God doesn’t make empty promises like some of our politicians do. His Word contains an accomplishment force. (Isaiah 55:10-11). In II Kings 4, there was famine in Samaria and the Word of the Lord came through Elisha that the economy of Samaria will experience an overnight turn around for the better. The right-hand man of the king doubted and missed all of God’s blessings. Those who believed saw the fulfilment of God’s Word in their lives.

May you never miss your blessings in Jesus Name

Prayer

O Lord, it is time for You to work in my life in Jesus Name!

Confession

God’s Word is working wonders in my life!
