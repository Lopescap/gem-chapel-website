Your Time, Your Life

In short: You must master the art of investing your time because time lost can never be recovered. One of the dictionary definitions of investment is ‘to spend time or effort with the expectation of some satisfaction’.

Walk in wisdom toward them that are without, redeeming the time.

— Colossians 4:5

You must master the art of investing your time because time lost can never be recovered. One of the dictionary definitions of investment is ‘to spend time or effort with the expectation of some satisfaction’. To effectively invest your time, you must do the following; 1. Invest time in God

You invest in God by spending time to study the Bible, praying and fellowshipping with the Holy Spirit. The lives of all great men and women in the Bible reveal that they spent time with God. When we really spend time with God, we become transformed, we sense His Presence in our lives more and we receive strength and direction for the journey.

Acts 4:13, ‘Now when they saw the boldness of Peter and John, and perceived that they were unlearned and ignorant men, they marveled; and they took knowledge of them, that they had been with Jesus.’

Invest time in Yourself

The greater your destiny, the more time you must invest in yourself. IN THE GAME OF LIFE, WHAT YOU PUT IN IS WHAT YOU

CAN WITHDRAW.

If you are student and you want to be counted among the achievers in your school, then you must go beyond the minimum. You must be diligent enough to study very hard. If you are in the ministry or in any other business you must develop a studious lifestyle in order to get to the top. Proverbs 22:29 ¶ Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men

Invest time in Eternity

God invested His son in us because of eternity. That is why we must do things that carry eternal rewards. When you win people to Christ, you are investing in something that will bring sure rewards in eternity. When you go out of your way to help a needy person; when you take care of an orphan and visit those in the prisons you are laying up treasures in Heaven. Matthew 6:19-20

Prayer

O Lord, help me to use my time for profitable ventures in Jesus Name!

Confession

I refuse to waste my time!
