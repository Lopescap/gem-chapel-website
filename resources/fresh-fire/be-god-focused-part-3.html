Be God-Focused! — Part 3

In short: Beloved, you can seek after things and miss God at the end of the day but when you seek after God, the things will follow. You must only be satisfied when you wake up in the likeness of God; all of God must be on you.

As for me, I will see Your face in righteousness; I shall be satisfied when I awake in Your likeness.

— Psalm 17:15

Beloved, you can seek after things and miss God at the end of the day but when you seek after God, the things will follow. You must only be satisfied when you wake up in the likeness of God; all of God must be on you.

However, for you to be a carrier of the tangible and the visible Presence of God; you must first die to self. The flesh and all its desires must be crucified. The lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes and the pride of life must die for Christ to live fully in you. When Moses asked God to show him His face; God made a simple but a profound statement in Exodus 33:20, ’ But He said, “You cannot see My face; for no man shall see Me, and live.”

God was simply telling Moses, ‘Before you can see Me, every flesh in you must die because only dead men can see God. The flesh must be crucified. That is why Moses only saw God after death on the Mount of Transfiguration when He saw Jesus face to face (Matthew 17).

Again, the Apostle Paul who had many dumbfounding revelations of God said in Galatians2:20, ‘I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me: and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself for me.’ Beloved, stop flirting with the world if you really want to see Jesus! Surrender your body, soul and spirit to the Almighty God. When you see His face you will always shine in the journey of life and shame will never knock on your door. Psalm 34: 5 says, ‘They looked to Him and were radiant, And their faces were not ashamed.’

Prayer

O Lord, kill my love for the things of the world in Jesus Name!

Confession

God is my obsession!
