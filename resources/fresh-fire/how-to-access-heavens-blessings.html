How to Access Heaven’s Blessings

In short: The Almighty God has a lot in store for His children. However, prompt and consistent obedience to His commands is what gives us access to Heaven’s blessings.

If they obey and serve Him, They shall spend their days in prosperity, And their years in pleasures
— Job 36:11

The Almighty God has a lot in store for His children. However, prompt and consistent obedience to His commands is what gives us access to Heaven’s blessings. In order for you to access your inheritance in Christ, you must develop a quality walk with God. Our walk with God is however made possible by continuous obedience to God’s commandments (Deut. 28:1-14). Abraham practically walked with God by his lifestyle of obedience to God. He promptly obeyed God when he was told to move out of his father’s house, he obeyed God and circumcised the men in his entire household and when God asked him for Isaac, he didn’t hesitate at all.

You cannot walk with God unless you please Him and obedience is the only way to please Him. But obedience is made easy by a deep affection for God. So love Him to obey Him to please Him and to ultimately enter into a quality walk with Him.

From today, may obedience to His instructions be your daily delight. Whatever He tells you to do, DO IT! Don’t succumb to the spirit of disobedience operating in our world today. As you constantly follow God’s instructions in every area of your life, you will continuously access His blessings.

Prayer

O Lord, destroy any trace of the spirit of disobedience from my life in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am always obedient to His commands!
