You Must Always Act on His Instructions

In short: Most modern day Christians are revelation rich and application poor. Whenever you pray, you must first listen to the voice of God and after that act on the instructions you received.

And now, Lord, behold their threatenings: and grant unto thy servants, that with all boldness they may speak thy word
— Acts 4:29

Most modern day Christians are revelation rich and application poor. Whenever you pray, you must first listen to the voice of God and after that act on the instructions you received. Don’t just pray and fold your arms, start taking the right steps that will take you to your next level. Intimidation narrows down destiny. Fear hinders people from faring well. Fear is what makes people get unfair treatment. It takes bravery to be out of slavery, only the daring will become the darling of all around them. The Bible calls the Christian a lion and an eagle. These two creatures are bold and fearless. Proverbs 28:1 says, “The righteous are as bold as the lion...” Boldness will naturally result into step taking which brings a man out of mediocrity and limitation. Bravery will make you step ahead in the place where others are wondering what will happen. Don’t be a prisoner of your past. What you have in your hand today is more important than what you have lost. Stop looking at where you have been and start looking at where you can be. Life’s only limitations are what you set on yourself!

Start moving and begin acting now! When you move, God will also move. Heaven acts after man has acted (Matt. 18:18). A ship in the port is safe but that is not what a ship was built for. Don’t be afraid to take action! While others are wondering you are generating wonders, while they are asking questions you are making exclamations! God has not given you the spirit of fear but of love, of power and of a sound mind (II Tim. 1:7). Always ask for the spirit of boldness. When the disciples were being intimidated, they asked God to grant them boldness. You can do the same thing today.

Don’t just pray; take action! From today, be determined to act on every divine instruction.

Prayer

O Lord, grant me holy boldness to take the right steps this year in Jesus Name!

Confession

I always act on divine instructions!
