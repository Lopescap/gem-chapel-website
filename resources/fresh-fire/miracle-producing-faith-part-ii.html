Miracle-Producing Faith — Part II

In short: Every word of Scripture is a more sure Word of Prophecy. This means, it can’t change, it is established, it is bound to come to pass. However, what delays or eventually stops the manifestation of God’s Word in our lives is unbelief.

We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts:
— 2 Peter 1:19

Every word of Scripture is a more sure Word of Prophecy. This means, it can’t change, it is established, it is bound to come to pass. However, what delays or eventually stops the manifestation of God’s Word in our lives is unbelief. If you can believe, you will see the speedy fulfilments of God’s Word in your life.

To exercise your faith, you must do the following;

Believe everything that is declared from God’s Word

I Timothy 4:13,15, “13 Till I come, give attendance to reading, to exhortation, to doctrine. 15 Meditate upon these things; give thyself wholly to them; that thy profiting may appear to all.

Hebrews 4: 3 says For we which have believed do enter into rest,...”

2. Make specific confessions from the Word.

Faith must be vocalized if the required results are to be actualized. II Corinthians 4:13, “We having the same spirit of faith, according as it is written, I believed, and therefore have I spoken; we also believe, and therefore speak;”

Don’t let your mouth make your life crooked, wretched and miserable

3. Take steps of faith. James 2:19-20

In the school of faith, there is what to say and what to do; there is what to confess and what to profess; there is what to declare and what to act upon. Faith results are waiting for faith action. Faith does not fold his hands, cross his legs and open his mouth. It moves.

a) Act on the demands of the Scriptures

b) Act on the instruction of God’s Servants. II Chron. 20:20 c) Take practical steps

Prayer

O Lord, energize my faith in Jesus Name!

Confession

My faith always produce great Miracles!
