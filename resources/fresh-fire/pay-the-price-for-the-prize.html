Pay the Price for the Prize!

In short: You are born-again to be a high-flier – Prov. 4:18. Life is like a cafeteria. You take your tray, select your food and pay at the other end. You can get anything you want as long as you are willing to pay for the price.

looking unto Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith, who for the joy that was set before Him endured the cross, despising the shame, and has sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.

— Hebrews 12:2

You are born-again to be a high-flier – Prov. 4:18. Life is like a cafeteria. You take your tray, select your food and pay at the other end. You can get anything you want as long as you are willing to pay for the price. In a cafeteria, if you wait for people to serve you, you will wait forever. Life is like that too; you make choices and pay the price to succeed!

Without pain there can never be gain. For every new height to scale; there is a new price to pay. Therefore, those who dodge pain will miss gain. Without pain there cannot be access to gain. While many see pain as intimidating obstacle only very few see it as platform for gaining access to next level. Whenever you feel the pain then see the gain. Hebrews 12: 2 says “looking unto Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith, who for the joy that was set before Him endured the cross, despising the shame, and has sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.”

Never be crushed by pain whenever it comes; rather, count the gain. Refuse to be blinded by pains; behold the gains that come thereafter. It will take a toll on you but it will make you stand tall in life. It will never become your turn to gain new height for as long as you keep turning away from pain and sacrifices. No one ever gets tall without a toll on him. You never get tall without paying a toll. The pain you are willing to bear will determine the crown you will be allowed to wear. Jesus Christ is wearing the greatest crown today because; He experienced the greatest pain on the cross of Calvary.

Your access to rose flower is at the mercy of your ability to endure the thorns that surrounds it. Never mind the “thorns” as you seek access to the rose. Like the Apostle, keep stretching and pressing for the prize of the high calling of Christ.

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to endure my cross in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am advancing no matter what!
