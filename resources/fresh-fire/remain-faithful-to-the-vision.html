Remain Faithful to the Vision

In short: At times, you may be tempted by the devil, to believe that the fulfillment of your God-given vision is an impossibility. It is an illusion.

Vr.28 “And we know that all things work together for good to those who love God, to those who are called according to His purpose.

— Romans 8:28

At times, you may be tempted by the devil, to believe that the fulfillment of your God-given vision is an impossibility. It is an illusion. But remember, the same God who gave you the vision is more than capable and sufficient to give you whatever gifts and abilities you need to realize His purpose for your life. The fact is, you have to be aware of the vision first, write it down, run with it (ie. Do something about it), and never lose focus, stay with it. Hab. 2:2-3.

Remaining faithful to God’s vision for your life assures you of finding favour with God and with others. Joseph never gave up on his vision but waited patiently on God to see all his dreams come true, no matter what he went through he still believed his dreams. (Read Genesis 39:2-9). At the right time God took him from the prison to the palace.

David was also was promised the kingship of Israel but he had to wait patiently for God’s divine timing. (2 Samuel 2:1-11)

God has a perfect vision for your life, so do not lose hope. Stay faithful to Him and you will never be disappointed.

Prayer

Oh Lord help me to stay faithful to the vision you have for me.

Confession

I stay faithful to my vision!
