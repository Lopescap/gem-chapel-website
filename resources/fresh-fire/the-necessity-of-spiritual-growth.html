The Necessity of Spiritual Growth

In short: Beloved, spiritual stagnation is the greatest killer of physical advancement. God does not manifest children; He only show forth His matured sons (Rom. 8:19). Growth in Christianity is not an option but a necessity.

Now I say, that the heir; as long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he lord of all.

— Galatians 4:1

Beloved, spiritual stagnation is the greatest killer of physical advancement. God does not manifest children; He only show forth His matured sons (Rom. 8:19). Growth in Christianity is not an option but a necessity. There are so many baby Christians around today that is why God cannot trust the body of Christ with certain blessings and greater levels of the anointing.

In 1 Corinthians 13:11, Paul said “When I was a child, I spoke like a child, i thought as a child, but when i became a man, I put away childish things.” No wise man will give a gun fully load with bullets to his 4-year-old boy because it can kill him. The only-wise God will never entrust you with certain blessings until you grow in the spirit.

You must grow in the Spirit because of the following reasons;

1. In order to fulfill your purpose in life. Luke 1:80 says “And

the child (John the Baptist) grew and waxed strong in spirit....” Again Luke 2:40 says of Jesus, “And the child grew and waxed strong in the spirit....” No wonder both John and Jesus were able to fulfill their assignments in life.

2. Your spirit is a channel of God’s direction (Prov. 20:27).

Therefore, without a developed spirit, you cannot receive divine direction.

3. Your spirit sustains you in time of pains /sickness. Prov. 18:14 4. Your spirit empowers you to overcome the adversities of life.

Prov. 24:10.

That is why you must carefully develop your spirit from today. In the subsequent days, you will learn exactly how to do that.

Prayer

O Lord, help me to grow strong in the spirit in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am growing strong in the Lord!
