Supernatural Proofs!

In short: On the Cross Jesus Christ made a public show of the devil in a triumphant procession - Col 2:15 And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.

To whom also he shewed himself alive after his passion by many infallible proofs, being seen of them forty days, and speaking of the things pertaining to the kingdom of God:

— Acts 1:3

On the Cross Jesus Christ made a public show of the devil in a triumphant procession - Col 2:15 And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it.

After that, Jesus Christ celebrated His resurrection among His disciples for 40 days - Acts 1: 3 To whom also he shewed himself alive after his passion by many infallible proofs, being seen of them forty days, and speaking of the things pertaining to the kingdom of God:

And because we believe that Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today and forever (Heb. 13:8), we believe that after Easter, we must expect diverse encounters with the resurrected Christ and undeniable proofs in miracles, signs and wonders that will change our destiny forever.

In this season therefore, we must endeavour to be spiritually awake, remain on our watch and set ourselves upon our towers so as to maximize this season of divine visitation- Hab. 2:1-3

You must engage the following mysteries if you want to experience undeniable proofs;

The Altar of Prayer and Fasting – Acts 10:9-24, 29

Praises & Worship – Luke 24:36-40, 53 & Psalm 22:3

A deep study of the Word – I Samuel 3:21

Serving in the House of God – Mark 16:14-20/ John 14:21

5. Constant Church attendance – Psalm 132:13-15, Zeph. 3:17

May God reveal Himself to you in Jesus Name!
