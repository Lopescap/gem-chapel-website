The Greatest Preparation for Your Manifestation

In short: If you don’t want God to stay off you, stay off sin. If you must keep God with you, then keep off sin. It is true that, in our modern world today, most people are living in sin and are living any how.

By mercy and truth iniquity is purged: and by the fear of the Lord, MEN DEPART FROM EVIL “.
— Proverbs 16:6

If you don’t want God to stay off you, stay off sin. If you must keep God with you, then keep off sin. It is true that, in our modern world today, most people are living in sin and are living any how. However, if you don’t withdraw your hand from sin, God will withdraw His hand from your life. Don’t touch sin if you don’t want to be out of touch with God.

You either love sin and hate God or love God and hate sin. James 4:4 says, “... know ye not that friendship of the world enmity with God? Whosoever therefore will be a friend of the world is the enemy of God “. When Samson did not withdraw from sin, God withdrew from Him.

Samson was anointed and a great instrument in the hands of God. An instrument of glory however became a tool of mockery in the hands of the devil, because of the sin of sexual immorality. Never toy with sin if you don’t want to end as a toy in Satan’s hand.

Sanctification is the process of preparation for manifestation. The greater your desire for holiness, the higher you fly. You begin to see God’s hand launching you into higher realms of favour and glory, if you decide to live a holy life through the empowerment of the Spirit. ( Ezek. 36:27)

The Bible says in Rom. 6:14: “And sin shall have no dominion over you......”

Remember, holiness is beautiful and it pleases God for God is holy. He dwells in holiness and in light unapproachable - Ps. 2:9

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to keep away from sin in Jesus Name!

Confession

My life will always please God!
