Developing Spiritual Toughness

In short: Body fasting is the platform for spiritual fattening. Physical leanness is what births spiritual toughness. If you must get fatter spiritually then you must engage more in fasting. The body must “perish” for the inner man to be renewed. II Cor.

Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke?
— 1 Saiah 58:6

Body fasting is the platform for spiritual fattening. Physical leanness is what births spiritual toughness. If you must get fatter spiritually then you must engage more in fasting. The body must “perish” for the inner man to be renewed. II Cor. 4:16, ” Therefore we do not lose heart. Even though our outward man is perishing, yet the inward man is being renewed day by day.” He must increase, but I (the body) must decrease- John 3:30. You must be dead to self to be alive unto God; you must be away from the body to be present with God - 1 Cor.5:8. At fasting time you desire the things of the Spirit more than things of the flesh. Fasting creates for you the platform to develop closeness to God. Denying your body of food is enhancing your spirit. Without a break from the body, you cannot assume strong spirituality. Feeding the body beyond necessary is killing the spirit man without knowing. How long you wait on God determines how much you weigh before men. Therefore wait on the Lord in a fast to enhance your spiritual weight before men. When you fast, you are empowered to prevail over the negative circumstances of life. After Jesus Christ had fasted for 40 days and nights it was said of Him in Luke 4:14 that “He returned in the POWER OF THE SPIRIT”.

Are you getting weak spiritually? Are you finding it difficult to pray? Do you feel some spiritual dryness in your life? Are you experiencing a draught of revelations, visions and dreams? Then enter into fast. You can fast your way into power. Yes you can!

Prayer

O Lord, increase my desire for spiritual things in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am a spiritual heavyweight!
