The Footprints of God

In short: Several years ago, a great French scientist was crossing the Arabian desert under the influence of an Arab (Christian) tourist guide. Along the way, the sat down to take a rest and the guide started praying.

The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God...

— Psalm 14:1

Several years ago, a great French scientist was crossing the Arabian desert under the influence of an Arab (Christian) tourist guide. Along the way, the sat down to take a rest and the guide started praying. When he had finished, the scientist stood looking at him with scorn, and asked what he was doing. He replied, ‘I am praying’. The scientist said, ‘Praying! Praying to who? ‘To God’ replied the guide.

Then the scientist said, ‘Did you see God?’ Did you ever put out your hands and touch and feel God?’ The tourist guide answered ‘No’ to these two questions. Then you are a great fool to believe in a God you never saw, and a God you never put out your hand and touched! The Arab guide said nothing.

They finally retired for the night, rose early the next morning, and a little before sunrise, they went out from the tent. The French scientist said to his guide, ‘There was a camel round this tent last night.’ With a peculiar look in his eyes, the guide said, ‘Did you see the camel?’ ‘Did you hear the camel?’, ‘Did you put out your hand and touch the camel?’ The scientist also answered ‘No” to all these questions. ‘Well,’ said the guide, you are a strange man of science to believe in a camel you never heard, a camel you never put out your hand and touched.” Oh but the scientist said, ‘Here are his footprints all around the tent.’ Just then, the sun rising was rising in all its oriental splendour and with a graceful wave of his hand, the guide said, ‘BEHOLD THE FOOTPRINTS OF THE CREATOR, AND KNOW THAT THERE IS A GOD! “Clearly the untutored Arab had the best of the argument. EVERYWHERE IN THIS MAGNIFICENT UNIVERSE, WE SEE THE FOOTPRINTS OF THE CREATOR!

Man was created as a vessel to contain God. Without God, you are EMPTY! Embarking on the journey of life without God is an exercise in futility – John 15:5, ‘....He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.’ Never conclude in your heart or say with your mouth that there is no God – Psalm 14:1

The life of an orphan is very hard; like the prodigal son, RUN BACK TO YOUR FATHER (GOD) AND HE WILL RECEIVE YOU WITH HIS OPEN ARMS OF LOVE!

Prayer

O Lord, open my eyes to see my Saviour; I want to know Him more and more in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will never forget my God!
