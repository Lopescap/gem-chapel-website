The Greatest Mistake You Can Ever Make

In short: The greatest mistake you can make in your life is to STOP DOING the very things that took you to the level you are today. T he SACRIFICES that changed your story must always be maintained if you don’t want to DIMINISH IN GLORY!

¶ But when he was STRONG, his heart was lifted up to his destruction: for he transgressed against the LORD his God, and went into the temple of the LORD to burn incense upon the altar of incense.

— 2 Chronicles 26:16

The greatest mistake you can make in your life is to STOP DOING the very things that took you to the level you are today. T he SACRIFICES that changed your story must always be maintained if you don’t want to DIMINISH IN GLORY!

When King Uzziah began his journey of leadership, he did a lot of right things and sought God under the Prophet Zechariah and because of that God prospered him. However, at the height of his prosperity and glory, he stopped doing all the right things that he was doing and he became proud. He finally lost his glory and experienced destruction - II Chronicles 26:4-5, 18 The exercises that brought you into power and glory must still be continued in order to maintain the power and glory. Whatever you did to get into a realm is what you must do in order to stay there and even go higher. No matter how far you go and no matter how high you reach, you must never forget where you came from. The higher you go in spiritual authority, the greater should be your spiritual exercises and sacrifices because it TAKES WIDTH to SUSTAIN HEIGHT. If you look at a triangle, the broader the base, the higher the triangle.

Anytime God brings you from the pit to the top; every time He brings you from the back to the front, never forget your history.

- Go back to your attitude of honour and humility

- Go back to fasting and prayers

- Go back to your lifestyle of deep praises and worship

- Go back to your lifestyle of meditating on God’s Word and the

reading of anointed books

- Start living a holy life again etc.

If you forget your ROOT, you automatically stop your FRUITS; if you forget your FOUNDATION, you will truncate your ELEVATION.

Prayer

O Lord, empower me to keep doing the right things in Jesus Name!

Confession

I will never lose my throne of glory!
