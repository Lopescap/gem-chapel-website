The Biggest Problem of Every Person

In short: When the 10 spies were saying it was not possible for the children of Israel to possess the promised land, Caleb said, ‘let us go up at once and possess the land, for we are well able to overcome it (Numbers 13:30)

How forcible are right words! but what doth your arguing reprove?

— Job 6:25

In life, you must speak right to live right; you must speak life to go up.

When the 10 spies were saying it was not possible for the children of Israel to possess the promised land, Caleb said, ‘let us go up at once and possess the land, for we are well able to overcome it (Numbers 13:30)

In life, never declare your condition but rather speak out your expectation

You were designed to have what you say and not to say what you have

Mr 11:23 For verily I say unto you, That whosoever shall say unto this mountain, Be thou removed, and be thou cast into the sea; and shall not doubt in his heart, but shall believe that those things which he saith shall come to pass; he shall have whatsoever he saith.

Your declarations are your prescriptions for God’s

manifestations in your life.

Numbers 14: 28 Say unto them, As truly as I live, saith the LORD, as ye have spoken in mine ears, so will I do to you: Your life turns in the direction of your tongue because your words are prophetic arrows into your future

I Peter 3:10 For he that will love life, and see good days, let him refrain his tongue from evil, and his lips that they speak no guile:

A person’s biggest problem is not the problem itself but rather what they say about the problem. Therefore, speak the right words to create the right world around you; change your language to change your luggage in life!

Prayer

O Lord, touch my lips with your coals of fire in Jesus Name!

Confession

Everything is turning around for my good!
