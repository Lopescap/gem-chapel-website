The True Value of a Man

In short: Work moves people forward. Those who work don’t see other peoples’ back because their work makes them second to none. The ant has no guide yet it advances. No one supervises the tiny ant yet he is able to advance its destiny into the land of plenty.

6 ¶ Go to the ant, you sluggard! Consider her ways and be wise, 7 Which, having no captain, Overseer or ruler, 8 Provides her supplies in the summer, And gathers her food in the harvest
— Proverbs 6:6-8

Work moves people forward. Those who work don’t see other peoples’ back because their work makes them second to none. The ant has no guide yet it advances. No one supervises the tiny ant yet he is able to advance its destiny into the land of plenty. Your work is your guide. Instead of looking for a guide, work your way to become one. If you are a worker, people will locate you via your work. Without work you are not traceable. Your work is your address. Work is your true and authentic identity. Idle people always line up behind workers, awaiting orders. Workers are leaders! Stop giving excuses and find something to do.

The true value of a man’s life is defined by the work of his hand . Your life is in your hand. Don’t blame anyone for your outcome, blame your hand. Speak to your hand to work hard. You are worthless without your work. If you don’t want to lose your worth, love your work. The worth of Joseph in Potiphar’s house was preceded by his work; his work gave him worth. It is your contribution that determines your relevance.

Real workers don’t think of what to take but what to contribute. A man’s real worth is not his salary but his contribution. Your contribution will earn you far more than your salary. Do not seek recognition because it is a natural sequence to contribution. Mordecai was a contributive gateman whose work spoke for him in the end. He became recognized as a result of his contribution. ( Est.2:21-23; 6:1-14; 10:1). Contribution is the passage to recognition. Proverbs 22:29 says, ‘Do you see a man who excels in his work? He will stand before kings; He will not stand before unknown men.’ Work your way forward!

Prayer

O Lord, help me to become a diligent worker in Jesus Name!

Confession

I diligently do my work!
