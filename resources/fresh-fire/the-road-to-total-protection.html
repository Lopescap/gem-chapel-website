The Road to Total Protection!

In short: The arrows of the enemy and his cohorts are flying all around. Fear and panic are the order of the day now. With all the technological advancement in our world today, human lives still remain the most vulnerable.

And the blood shall be to you for a token upon the houses where you are: and when I see the blood, I will pass over you, and the plaque shall not be upon you to destroy you, when I smite the land of Egypt”.

— Exodus 12:13

The arrows of the enemy and his cohorts are flying all around. Fear and panic are the order of the day now. With all the technological advancement in our world today, human lives still remain the most vulnerable. There is a multiplication of violence and evil in our world today. Armed robbery activities, murder, car – accidents, plane crashes are not uncommon in our world. People are living in fear of the unexpected. However, Proverbs 3:25 says, “Be not afraid of sudden fear, neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it cometh.”

The All-powerful Blood of Jesus, can give you sound sleep in the midst of a turbulent world. An obstinate and stubborn Pharaoh still refused to give up after nine deadly plaques from the All – powerful God. God had to stop Pharaoh once and for – all and therefore He sent an angel of death into the land of Egypt to slay all the firstborns in the lands. God however gave the Israelites a specific instruction. He told them to kill a lamb and apply the blood upon their door posts. The children of Israel obeyed this instruction and when the angel of death saw the Blood, he passed over them and they were exempted from danger.

As the Israelites were in Egypt, so are Christians in the world. As the angel of death passed through the land of Egypt, so the enemy sends trouble and arrows in our world. However, as the blood of the lamb protected the firstborns of the Israelites from the angle of death, so will the Blood of Jesus protect you from the troubles and dangers of the world. From today, make it a point to always plead tot Blood upon your life, your wife / husband, children, household, business, possessions and all that concerns you. You will escape every satanic arrow by the Blood of Jesus!

Prayer

O Lord, let the Blood of Jesus protect me from every satanic arrow, in Jesus Name. Amen!

Confession

No satanic arrow will locate my life!
