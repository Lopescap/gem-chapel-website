Breaking the Chains of Pornography Addiction

In short: Your eye is the door to your life. Whatever your eyes behold is what your heart will naturally pursue and determine the direction your body will follow. The birth of Samson was a mysterious one.

Then went Samson to Gaza, and SAW THERE AN HARLOT, and WENT IN UNTO HER.

— Judges 16:1

Your eye is the door to your life. Whatever your eyes behold is what your heart will naturally pursue and determine the direction your body will follow. The birth of Samson was a mysterious one. Angels actually prophesied about his birth and the exploits that he will do for God. However, instead of Samson to actualize his God-given destiny by being an instrument of glory; he became a tool of mockery in the camp of his enemies. What happened? He simply failed to guard his eyes. The Bible says in Judges 16:1 that when Samson went to Gaza, he SAW there an harlot and since whatever your eyes see, your body follows, he WENT IN UNTO HER.

Never watch what you don’t want to experience in your life. Your eye is the vision of your life. It is what it sees that will be eventually actualized. Your eye sets the pace for motion. It is your eye that attracts you to things. It is God who gave you eyes. Prov. 20:12 says, “The hearing ear, and the seeing eye, the LORD hath made even both of them.” God gave you those eyes for seeing only good things. Give direction to your eye to give direction to your destiny. Stop watching naked women and naked men doing their own things in movies and magazines!

Do away with pornography if you don’t want to destroy your destiny. Do you have any nude pictures in your room? Burn them today! Do you have any music videos with totally or partially naked women in them? Destroy them. Don’t deceive yourself. You are not stronger than Samson and you are not wiser than God. Be like Job who said in Job 31:1, “I made a covenant with mine eyes; why then should I think upon a maid?” Guard your eyes!!

Prayer

O Lord, help me to watch only the right things in Jesus Name!

Confession

I am seriously guarding my eyes!
